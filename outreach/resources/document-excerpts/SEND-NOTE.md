## Pick once while sourcing

- Content: help turning existing knowledge into videos. Use content-01-ideas.png on day 2 and content-02-recording.png on day 6.
- Profile: missing start-here/proof/approach structure or mismatched bio and destination. Use profile-01-highlights.png on day 2 and profile-02-bio.png on day 6.
- Funnel: creator already has a sales page or VSL and there is a visible message/structure issue. Use funnel-01-vsl-opening.png on day 2 and funnel-02-page-and-proof.png on day 6. A visual issue alone does not establish poor conversion.

## Cadence and stopping rule

Day 0 opener unchanged. Day 1 short reminder. Day 2 screenshot 01 and matching caption. Day 3 one short continuation about the same resource. Day 6 screenshot 02 and matching caption. Day 11 one easy reaction question. Day 18 leave the door open and mark dormant if unanswered. Six follow-ups maximum; stop the scheduled sequence as soon as they reply or decline. This is the founder-directed test cadence, not a validated optimum.
