Building a qualified audience

Content Ideation rule of thumb:

Talk about a result, a win, or a problem you've solved for a student or something you talk about in your mentorship.

Use your own experience if you don't have that kind of audience yet.

Titles

how i [result] using [method]
the [method] that helped me [result] (full breakdown)

Lead with the result. Show what's behind it.

Intro

2

Body

Briefly explain who you are, what clicked and what changed.

3

Mid-video CTA

After a useful lesson, invite them to get help applying it. Then keep teaching.

that's one of the things we work through in the program. if you want help applying it, the first link below explains more. if not, no worries — keep watching.
4

Body

Go deeper with another example.
Explain the decisions and mistakes.

5

Final CTA

Recap the result. Name the problem.
Point to the free training.

that's how we got [result]. if you're struggling with [problem], there's a link below designed to sell you something. it starts with a free training on how we do this. if it's not for you, no worries — see you next time.
