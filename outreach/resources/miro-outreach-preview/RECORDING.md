## Recording order

Use the transparent recording frames in order: 00 — Real client work; 01 — Brand and offer; 02 — Ideas and packaging; 03 — Video structure; 04 — Viewer to client. The enclosing Pulsify — qualified audience walkthrough frame shows the full overview. Zoom into each section when recording; the full overview is for navigation.

0–10 seconds: show Nick's original packaging example. $98,000 is founder-confirmed total sales across the engagement, not monthly profit or revenue attributed to this video.

10–25 seconds: explain that the roadmap starts with the creator's experience, offer, solved problems and own approach.

25–50 seconds: show the original trading and ecom references. Use results, student problems and personal experience for ideation. Bracketed titles require actual results, methods and student names. Reference screenshots demonstrate packaging, not proven sales performance.

50–70 seconds: intro → body → mid-video CTA → body → final CTA. Teach their own method, explain the decisions and offer help applying it after a useful lesson. The example creator CTA is part of their content, not another outreach ask.

70–90 seconds: explain the viewer-to-client path. Select DM or free training for the actual business. End with one reply ask for a breakdown of their own brand.

This is a reusable service preview. A recipient-specific audit follows their reply. Use the board as speaking notes rather than reading it word for word.
