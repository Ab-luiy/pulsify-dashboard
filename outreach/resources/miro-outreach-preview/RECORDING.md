## Recording order, about 90 seconds

0-10 seconds: Client work. Show Nick and the packaging artifact. Explain that this is an example of our work across positioning, packaging and outreach. The $98,000 is founder-confirmed total sales across the engagement, not revenue attributed to this video, monthly profit, or a promise to a prospect.

10-25 seconds: Brand. Explain that we understand their business, offer, experience and what makes their approach different, then build the roadmap around it.

25-50 seconds: Packaging. Show original trading and ecom examples. Explain that their results and solved problems supply video ideas; we build ideas, titles, thumbnails and talking points around that. Published titles are copied as shown in the founder's references. Bracketed titles are templates requiring the creator's actual result/method/student, not proven outputs. Source thumbnails are examples of packaging, not evidence of sales performance.

50-70 seconds: Structure. Walk through intro, body, mid-video CTA, body and final CTA. The displayed program CTA is an example of the creator's video CTA, not a second ask in this outreach recording. They teach their own method; we organize its presentation.

70-90 seconds: Sales path. Explain that interested viewers get an appropriate next step. DM or training are alternatives to select for the actual creator, not mandatory simultaneous steps. Application/call shown as the high-ticket path, not a universal funnel requirement. End with one reply ask for their own breakdown.

Use these as speaking notes; do not read the board word for word. The preview follows the founder's Grant/Soo Wei adaptation and deliberately keeps business delivery claims limited to known services. No promised posting volume, assigned account manager, lift percentage, or guaranteed ready-to-buy viewers.
