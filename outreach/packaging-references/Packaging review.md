# Packaging review

Work in progress. All 14 founder-supplied PNGs opened and were visually inspected on 2026-09-15. They contain 15 videos: 14 visible listing title-thumbnail pairs and one watch-page frame. Original files and founder good/bad labels are preserved. This is a screenshot-based craft review, not live YouTube verification.

## Verification boundaries

Titles and visible counts below were transcribed from screenshots. Upload ages are relative to the unknown screenshot capture date, not to this review date. Multiplier badges are third-party overlays as displayed; their calculation was not verified. No CTR, retention, traffic-source, conversion or revenue data is available. A screenshot of a revenue claim does not verify the result. No video URLs were supplied. Four cropped listings omit channel identity; one additional watch-page capture omits the actual thumbnail. Small on-screen interface details cannot all be read or substantiated.

## Recommended examples for the board

Start with E4 for title-thumbnail complement, E1 for a case-study board, T5 for live demonstration, and T7 for a restrained creator frame. Use T4 only as a board/opening design reference until its actual thumbnail is supplied. The two TradingLab examples can teach vague or absolute claims, but cannot be labelled performance failures. More bad examples can be added without changing this first-pass review.

Each example below provides three candidate annotation bullets plus a separate internal evidence note. These are observations and applications of the stored frameworks, not causal claims about why a video earned views.

## Framework checks and conflicts

The five formulas were read from the founder's local titles-thumbnails-generator.skill package in Downloads/AI swipe files & templates/Skills. Apply [[wiki/content-engine/packaging-rules]] and the later [[wiki/content-engine/thumbnail-layout-patterns]] when older skill instructions conflict. Reference videos need not obey our adapted-title conventions to illustrate a useful principle. Do not rewrite their exact published titles to lowercase or add kickers while presenting them as originals.

This collection contains good-labelled examples with uppercase headlines, repeated title figures, tiny face insets, decorative numbers and no parenthetical kicker. The founder has selected them as references, not explicitly repealed every existing production rule. Preserve the useful pattern and flag any conflicting treatment rather than quietly changing the framework. No claim that every example should be copied wholesale.
