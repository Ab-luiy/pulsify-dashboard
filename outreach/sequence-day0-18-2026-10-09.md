# Cold DM sequence, day 0 to 18

One thread, one resource that keeps building. The opener promises something on content, day 2 delivers the board, days 3 to 11 each add one piece to that same board, day 18 closes. Their personal brand breakdown is the reward for replying.

**Stop rule:** the moment they reply, stop the schedule and answer what they said. If they ask for it early, send the board early. Stop on a decline. Never mention their silence. "figured" is used once, on day 2 only.

| Day | Touch | Goes to | Attach |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Opener, text | Every lead you pick | nothing |
| 1 | One specific nod, text | Everyone | nothing |

## Day 0, opener

Your tested opener (62 sent, 5 replied up to 08-22), with only the promise changed so day 2 matches it:

> your profile hijacked my day bro haha, I've been taking a look at your content and honestly most of the stuff in there resonated with me even though im not a trader. made you a lil something on content i think you'd get a lot out of, happy to send it over, lmk!

Swap "a trader" for "in ecom", "on tiktok shop" or "in your space".

Doomscrolled variant, same change:

> yo icl i doomscrolled your content without noticing cuz it had me hooked even though i'm not a trader. made you a lil something on content i think you'd get a lot out of, happy to send it over. lmk

## Day 1, one specific nod

Five minutes on their latest video. One real detail, no question, no ask.

> also that [specific video or moment] was cold, the part where you [one thing they actually did or said]

Example shape: "also that NQ breakdown from tuesday was cold, the part where you showed the trade you skipped". Fill from their real content only. A wrong detail is worse than skipping this day, so skip it if you can't find one.

## Day 3, voice note

About 20 seconds. Say their name and the one thing in the first ten seconds. Talk it, don't read it.

> yo [name], it's ab. quick one on that board. the bit i'd use first is the titles at the top. take a win you've already got, like [a real result or student win from their content], and drop it into the first one. that's basically your next video sorted. anyway, no stress.

If you can't find a real result on their page, say "take a trade you're proud of" (or "a product that worked" for ecom) instead of naming one.

## Day 6, packaging board

Attach `outreach/resources/packaging-dos-donts/packaging-dos-donts.png` ("Give them a reason to watch you"). It ends on the same $139,200 title from the day-2 board, so it reads as the next page.

## Day 11, guru board

Attach `outreach/resources/dont-be-a-guru/packaging-refined.png` ("Don't look like another guru").

> last one on the packaging side. the big channels can run cash graphics cause people already know who they are. on a smaller channel the chart or the board does more work, it shows the lesson before anyone clicks

**View-once phone video**, only if they opened, reacted or watched something. About 60 seconds, selfie camera, one take. No links or numbers inside it, since they can't replay it.

> yo [name], it's ab. been sending you a couple of content bits this week. i was back on your page and [one new observation, not the one from day 1]. honestly if you took [their real result] and filmed it with the chart open like on that last board, i think that's a strong video for you. if you want, i'll map the whole thing out for your channel, title, thumbnail and how to open it. just say the word.

## Day 18, close

> gonna leave it here. if you ever want me to go through your channel and map out your next few videos, titles, thumbnails and how to open them, send me a "yo" and i'll put it together

Then the thread goes dormant.

## When they reply

Answer what they said first. If they want more, build their personal brand breakdown (the thing the board teaches, applied to their channel) and walk them through it in a 90-second Loom, sent as a video file. That's where the custom work goes.
