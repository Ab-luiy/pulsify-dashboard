## The read

**1. 1,500 videos, 1,520 subscribers.** Roughly one subscriber per video. He is out-working every creator we have looked at and it is converting to almost nothing. 44 uploads a week and $67 a month.

**2. There is exactly one signal in 1,500 videos, and he already found it.** "90% Winrate Backtesting IFVGs on NQ", 56 minutes, 681 views. That is ten times his normal. He then made the sequel, "80% Winrate Backtesting IFVGs on Nasdaq", 45 minutes, and it did 97. Worth understanding why the second one underperformed before concluding anything: the 90% is a stronger number than the 80%, "NQ" against "Nasdaq" may matter, and nine days versus two days is not a fair comparison yet.

**3. The daily recap is a treadmill with no exit.** "NQ Trade Recap - July 29 - 10am Po3 + 5m Cont" is a filename, not a title. Nobody searches a date. It has no promise, no stake, and it expires the day after it goes up. He is producing six of these a day at 19 to 73 views each.

**4. The backtest format is the opposite in every way.** Long, evergreen, a claimed number in the title, and a question people actually type into the search bar. It is also the only format on the channel that anybody could find on purpose.

**5. His offer is buried under his affiliate links.** Position eight on the Linktree, below three prop firm discount codes. Same story in the highlight rail, where five of seven are other companies' logos.

**6. On Instagram his voice outperforms his charts by 10x.** The joke about blowing $500 on evals but not buying TradingView, the abs-to-candlesticks line, the grifters-gonna-grift callout. All 4K to 7K on 599 followers. His chart posts do 213. He is funny and has opinions, and that is the thing travelling, not the analysis.

**7. No face anywhere on YouTube.** Green text on a beige chart, thirty times over.
