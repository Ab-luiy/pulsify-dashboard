## The read

**1. The headline number: 101,000 views, 2,243 followers.** Reach is not his problem. Nothing sticking to it is. Contrast with `_acetrades_`, who does 7,371 on 1,698 followers, which is the opposite shape.

**2. His reach content and his depth content are two different accounts sharing one handle.** The 101K reel is a generic beginner roadmap that anyone could have recorded, delivered fully in thirty seconds, giving no reason to follow the person who said it. The 140-point NQ read, which is the content that would make somebody follow, sat at 1,141. The broad stuff travels and converts nobody. The deep stuff converts and travels nowhere. Nothing connects the two.

**3. One thumbnail on four videos.** A viewer who saw one has no reason to click a second, and on a seven-video channel that is most of the catalogue looking like a re-upload. This is the single most fixable thing across both accounts.

**4. The $1,600,000 phone.** Payout figures are the most discredited proof format in this niche, and it is on a 19-year-old's thumbnail four times. His own numbers argue against it: the 101K reel has no money in frame, just him talking in a hoodie.

**5. Titles are topics, not titles.** Liquidity. Market Structure. Candlesticks. All commodity ICT subjects that hundreds of channels already own, each with "- TB Trading Concept" bolted on the end, which eats the characters that show in search and adds nothing.

**6. He is building an audience for somebody else's method.** The site says ICT strategy. The reel closes "follow to learn everything I know about ICT." Every long-form video is a standard ICT concept. Same trap Ace was in with AMD, except TB is still in it today.

**7. His own idea is sitting right there, unnamed.** "Think in objectives" is a genuinely teachable, nameable concept, and he gave it away at the end of a reel under someone else's banner. That is the thing a mentorship would be built on.

**8. The site sells a transaction, not a relationship.** Everything on the page is a discount code, a giveaway or a free room. His revenue is prop-firm affiliate commission, which pays once at signup whether or not the person ever succeeds. Nothing on the page carries his actual thinking, and the FAQ opening on "is this legitimate" means the page is answering a doubt it created upstream.

**9. Build faults on the site:** 31 Discord screenshots duplicated in the markup, four of six prop firm cards marked "Coming soon" with no date, dead X link.
