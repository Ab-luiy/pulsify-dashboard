# Jeremy Haynes

Jeremy Haynes is the primary framework source for Pulsify's funnel infrastructure. He is an internet marketing educator and inner circle operator who has documented VSL call funnels, webinar funnels, and scaling systems in granular detail. His frameworks are the operational backbone of how Pulsify builds and scales backend infrastructure for clients.

## Why this matters for Pulsify

Pulsify's funnel architecture — the VSL, the Typeform/Calendly stack, the confirmation page, the pre-call email sequence, the hammer them strategy — is derived directly from Jeremy's frameworks. When Pulsify is scoping or building a funnel engagement, these are the reference documents. When a client asks why Pulsify uses a specific setup, the answer comes from here.

## What Jeremy has documented

### Call funnel framework
- Financial modeling for single call close and two call close funnels
- Hammer them strategy: 15–20 retargeting pieces + 6 emails/day pre-call
- Ad fatigue detection and creative rotation
- Scaling math: CPL × desired call volume = required daily spend
- Owner taking sales calls for 1–3 months at launch as non-negotiable
- Burn budget (gambling budget) as a required mental frame for any launch
- KPI benchmarks: CPL range $50–$900 depending on model; show rate target 70–80%; close rate varies by offer
- Dispositions framework: real feedback from closers instead of "leads are bad"
- Hiring ahead of demand: hire at 50% calendar capacity, not when full

### VSL framework
- 6-step VSL flow: Hook → Why You → Buying Motives → Offer → Objection Handling → Qualification + CTA
- Summary-first structure: cover everything in first 60–90 seconds
- Typeform + Calendly as the only acceptable tech stack (eliminates 50% application-to-scheduler drop-off)
- Confirmation page structure: headline + main video + 4–12 breakout videos + testimonials
- Pre-call email sequence: 6 emails/day for 48–72 hours (open rates 50–80% when content is relevant)
- VSL length by audience: affluent 2–5 min, general public up to 60 min
- Pixel optimization: never send DQ page data; only fire pixel on qualified confirmation page
- CAPI integration for advanced pixel qualification

### Webinar framework
- Always start live; only switch to evergreen when consistently converting above 8%
- KPIs: $5–$12 CPL, 20–40% show rate, 5–10%+ close rate
- Pre-webinar email volume: 12–14 total, ramping to 6/day by day 2 out
- Campaign structure: 25 ad sets, 1 ad per ad set, dynamically updating registrant list
- Warm audience ceiling: can't scale past a few hundred thousand/month without cold traffic
- House money strategy: warm/organic first → profit → fund cold iterations
- Best days/times: Tuesday–Thursday, 8:30 PM Eastern
- For $5K+ offers: book-a-call preferred, or offer webinar discount to bypass sales team

### Inner Circle page pattern

Jeremy's public Inner Circle page adds a useful page-architecture pattern: minimal page, embedded VSL, name/email gate before application, testimonial path, and strong anti-income-claim disclaimers. The embedded Wistia transcript has not been captured, so use this as page structure only, not VSL script analysis.

## Jeremy's positioning on competitor tactics to avoid

- Never use income claims in ads ("Get to $10K/month in 90 days")
- Never use guarantees — attracts skeptics and the financially unqualified; rich buyers see guarantees as red flags
- Never claim unbelievable revenue numbers without context
- Correct approach: describe the conditions of your ideal buyer

## His documented client results

- Devon (inner circle): joined at $100K/month, took calls himself, used firsthand data to fix confirmation page and email sequences, hit $400K/month within 3 months
- Capital raise client: turned every recurring investor question into a trust asset video, dramatically improved show rates
- $1M+/month client on a $5K offer sold at $3,500 webinar discount, weekly Wednesday 8 PM EST

## Key tools Jeremy recommends

- Wistia (primary VSL hosting — best analytics, engagement heatmaps)
- Vidalytics (second choice)
- Typeform + Calendly (the only acceptable call funnel application stack)
- CAPI (Conversion API) for advanced pixel qualification
