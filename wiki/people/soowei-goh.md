# SooWei Goh

A young consulting operator (claims age 20-21) who scaled a personal-brand consulting business to a stated ~$408K/month with no ads, selling brand/content/acquisition help to coaches and consultants, and who runs an unusually systematized, AI-assisted content operation. Everything here is drawn strictly from his 5 video transcripts (founder-supplied 2026-07-11, extracted in the soowei-goh-extractions folder); claims are his, not Pulsify-verified.

## Why this matters for Pulsify

SooWei is the operating-system counterpart to Niksetting's conversion craft. Where Niksetting supplies the slide-level content mechanics, SooWei supplies the machine that runs them at scale: AI agents for ideation/objection-mining/packaging/clipping, a 6-metric idea scorer, UTM attribution on organic content, the buyer-mirror form, the pay-me-three-times ascension offer model, and a forward-looking read on where the industry goes. He is the strongest independent confirmation of Pulsify's whole thesis — anti-flex, proof-led, AI-operated, mission-driven — because he predicts each of those trends and is already positioned on them.

- Revenue: 30K -> ~$408K/month in ~2 years, no ads; $45K upfront clients; a $14K LTV he considers low.
- Audience: 10K -> 120K followers in ~2 years.

Treat as claims; some names garble across videos (Jesse/Casper, Dario/Dart) and tool names need verification (Trackio, Sand Castles AI, PLAUD).

## Core doctrine (what Pulsify takes)

- **The AI content operating system:** Claude agents for weekly what's-working reports, objection mining from Fathom call transcripts (3+ repeats = a marketing problem to solve with content), packaging/thumbnail research, and long-form-to-clip cutting on an ICP-trained SOP. Founder keeps taste, state, verbal strategy, delivery; machines do research, scoring, clipping, analysis.
- **The 6-metric ideation scorer** (100K-view potential, pre-sell power, proven concept, ICP resonance, intrigue/payoff, brand alignment; 75 cutoff) — a reusable gate.
- **TOF/MOF/BOF by business need:** diagnose what the business needs (leads/trust/conversion), weight content accordingly; ICP-vs-dream-follower Venn for TOF breadth; show-marketing for MOF; objection-solving formats for BOF.
- **The buyer-mirror form** (why-me post-purchase survey) — independently matches Pulsify's onboarding form; his question set is the best captured.
- **The four trust stages:** admirable -> likable -> credible -> trustable, unskippable; value-only content is a one-dimensional, AI-replaceable brand.
- **Pay-me-three-times ascension:** split the A-to-Z offer into single-problem offers sold as problems break (brand -> acquisition -> advisory) — a direct input to Pulsify's Pulse System sequencing.
- **Dollar-per-follower** as the anti-vanity metric (now in the dashboard).
- **The three predictions:** sales calls die (marketing finishes the job), lifestyle marketing dies (proof moats replace replicable flex), value content dies to AI (mission/identity survive) — the adaptability doctrine.
