# Dustin Varano

Info operator and YouTube creator (YouTube "Dustin Varano", Instagram @varano, X @dustinvarano) who runs backend and launches for creators' coaching offers and sells 1:1 mentorship to operators and offer owners. Everything on this page comes from two sources we captured: his 2026-09-28 InfoOS video and his "The Keys" lead magnet. Revenue figures are his own claims, unverified.

## Why this matters for Pulsify

He targets the same buyers (operators and offer owners in bizop niches), runs YouTube long-form as his main lead source, and is building software (InfoOS) that overlaps with the Pulsify portal. That makes him a direct comparison on content, funnel and product.

## Core mechanics (what he says he does)

- Offer: 1:1 mentorship only, no group coaching or course, application through a Typeform; InfoOS access and his SOP library come bundled with it. Says he vets every applicant himself.
- Content: the "flywheel VSL", one multi-hour YouTube video all other videos link to ([[concepts/flywheel-vsl]]); Miro-board videos; raw, lowercase titles; "rank videos by cash collected, not views".
- Sales backend: setter and closer teams, Close CRM, Typeform qualification, Calendly, Fathom recordings, Slack alerts, commission payouts; InfoOS ties it together ([[synthesis/dustin-varano-infoos-teardown]]).
- ICP: moved from beginner operators to operators and offer owners already making money ([[synthesis/dustin-varano-the-keys]]).
- Claims (unverified): $1.9M/month across the offers he runs, a $28k PIF traced by InfoOS, 90%+ dispute win rate.

## How Pulsify uses this

Reference for his structures (flywheel video, proof placement, per-video cash tracking, onboarding speed), filtered through Pulsify doctrine. We do not copy his lifestyle framing: his story sequences and flywheel hook use Dubai, Monaco and luxury-car backdrops, which is the opposite of Pulsify's positioning.
