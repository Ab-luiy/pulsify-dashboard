# Abdullah Dagestani

Founder and sole operator of Pulsify. 23 years old. Syrian nationality, ethnically Dagestani. Resides in Istanbul, Turkey. Second-year mechanical engineering student. Solopreneur. The face of the brand.

## Why this matters for Pulsify

Pulsify's positioning is "trust-recession-proof, value-first, AI-amplified growth operator." That positioning collapses if the founder face is hidden, generic, or invented. Every authority anchor on the VSL, every founder-led video, every sales call leans on this page. It is the single source of truth for what Abdullah has done, what he can prove, and what constraints shape the business.

## Origin

Started an SMMA roughly three years ago with a Discord friend after watching an Iman Gadzhi video. Worked primarily in trading and adjacent biz-op niches. The SMMA was structurally faulty — partner mismatch, no offer clarity, no inbound — and revenue came in spikes rather than systems. Pulsify is the second attempt: same domain expertise, fixed structure, AI as the multiplier.

## Hard authority anchors

These go on the VSL, the IG bio link sequence, and any founder-led sales call.

- **$18,000 generated for a trading client in 2 days.**
- **~$96,000 generated in ~90 days from zero**, trading niche.
- **Nick (Mamba FX adjacent) case study** — significant revenue generated, full content engine built. Churned on expectation/timeline mismatch — see [[clients/nick/overview]] for the full story including the lesson applied to v1 of the offer.
- **Mason Bowstead podcast appearance** — interviewed about the Nick case study and methodology.
- **Jacob (Institutional J) — YouTube grown 0 → 700 subs**, single ~$4k conversion. Counted as a learning, not a win. The lesson: a YouTube channel without a VSL down-funnel does not convert. Filed into [[concepts/profile-funnel]] as evidence for the order-of-operations rule.
- **Profile-funnel ad campaigns** run with measurable spend and CTA-to-funnel conversion.

Do not invent anchors beyond these. When the next paid client closes, append. When a content piece passes 10k views or generates a booked call, append.

## What he is world-class at

- Speaking on camera at length. Comfort with the medium is high; tightening to the point is the active edit.
- Copy, sales infrastructure, ads, content packaging, profile audits, IG and YouTube strategy. Pre-AI domain expertise — Pulsify fuses this with AI.

## What he is dragging through

- Outreach volume execution (currently zero).
- Funnel build for himself (no live VSL, no live landing page).
- Audience size (17 YT subs, IG mostly bot-followed).
- Operational entity and payment processing (no Stripe in Turkey; payments routed through family member's crypto on Binance Turkey — works, but scales poorly past a few clients/month).

## Constraints

- **University.** Second-year mechanical engineering. Exam weeks are non-negotiable. The Pulsify operating model assumes 5–10 day stretches where the founder is unavailable and the agent must run the operational layer. See [[playbooks/exam-mode-autonomous-ops]].
- **Solo.** No co-founders, no employees, no VAs. Hiring only after revenue justifies it.
- **Financial pressure.** 10/10 self-reported as of 2026-05-10. Every plan must produce cash inside 30–45 days.
- **Geography.** Istanbul-based, Turkey-resident. Limits payment processor options and constrains in-person client work to MENA/EU travel windows.

## Personal-brand positioning

Full personal brand. Face on camera. Real name. Real story. Direct delivery — Pulsify voice rules apply to his videos as well as the wiki: no flexing, no cars, no watches, no lifestyle bait.

The deliberate left turn from the operators in [[wiki/people/competitor-watchlist|the competitor watchlist]] is the pairing of (a) raw founder authority with (b) AI as the visible delivery mechanism on every call and in every video. He is the only operator pitching "I will build you an agent that replaces your VA stack" with a personal track record in trading-coach growth specifically.

## What winning looks like (12-month target)

- Revenue: $30k–100k/mo range. The lower end is achievable through outreach + 3–6 active clients without large audience. The upper end requires the audience to start producing inbound.
- Audience: 3,000–5,000 micro-niche YouTube subscribers — large by micro-niche standards, irrelevant by mass-market standards.
- Lifestyle: better apartment, possible car, client retreats in Istanbul or Bodrum.

These targets are decoupled by design. Revenue can hit through outbound. Audience compounds slower. Do not let audience targets gate revenue work.
