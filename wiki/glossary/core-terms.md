# Core terms

Definitions for the key terms used throughout the Pulsify wiki. These terms appear repeatedly across wiki pages and are defined once here to avoid restating them inline.

---

**BOF (Bottom of Funnel)**
The conversion layer of the content funnel. BOF content is designed to push high-intent viewers to book a call. Every BOF video functions as a mini-VSL: client interviews, behind-the-scenes proof, direct CTAs. See [[content-funnel-tof-mof-bof]].

---

**Burn budget**
The ad spend required to generate enough data to make optimization decisions before a paid campaign is profitable. Not a failure — the cost of learning. Every new ad campaign has one. See [[call-funnel-framework]].

---

**CAPI (Conversion API)**
Server-to-server event tracking that bypasses browser-based pixel limitations (iOS 14+, ad blockers). Critical rule: never send disqualified lead data to CAPI or the algorithm learns to find more unqualified prospects. See [[vsl-framework]].

---

**CPL (Cost Per Lead)**
Total ad spend divided by the number of leads generated. Webinar registration CPL target: $5–$12. See [[webinar-framework]].

---

**CPBC (Cost Per Booked Call)**
Total ad spend divided by the number of calls booked. The primary scaling metric for a call funnel. See [[call-funnel-framework]].

---

**CTA (Call to Action)**
The instruction given to a viewer at the end (or within) a video specifying what to do next. Alexei identifies three types: indirect (embedded in storytelling), soft (subtle suggestion), and hard (explicit instruction). All three appear in every video. See [[alexei]].

---

**Disposition**
The outcome assigned to each booked sales call. Core dispositions: Closed, Not Now (reschedule), Not Qualified, Not Interested, No-Show, Nurture. Aggregate disposition data identifies which funnel stage is the active bottleneck. See [[call-funnel-framework]].

---

**DWY (Done With You)**
A coaching delivery model where the operator runs group calls and provides async support rather than individual 1-on-1 sessions. Enables revenue scaling without proportional operator time increase. See [[client-scaling]].

---

**Four content quadrants**
The framework for building hammer them content: (1) common questions, (2) common objections, (3) expectations, (4) follow-up questions. All sourced from real sales call data — not guessed. See [[hammer-them-strategy]].

---

**Hammer them strategy**
The system of pre-call content (15–20 retargeting pieces + 6 emails/day for 48–72 hours) designed to move booked leads up interest levels before they speak with a salesperson. Result: 60–70%+ show rates. See [[hammer-them-strategy]].

---

**High-ticket**
Offers priced at $3,000 and above. Requires a financially qualified avatar who has an existing revenue base and is investing to scale it. See [[offer-creation]].

---

**ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)**
The specific avatar a creator or business is designed to serve. Pulsify's primary ICP for most biz-op clients is "Marcus" — see [[marcus-avatar]] for the full 28-point profile.

---

**Interest levels**
The scale of prospect intent: Curious → General Interest → Moderate Interest → High Interest. Deals close at high interest. Marketing and hammer them content move prospects up this scale incrementally. The salesperson closes, not converts from scratch. See [[hammer-them-strategy]].

---

**Manychat**
The automation platform used to run the trigger word DM sequence in the [[profile-funnel]]. When a viewer DMs a trigger word (e.g., "SBT"), ManyChat fires a qualification sequence and routes qualified leads to a human setter.

---

**Marcus**
Pulsify's primary ICP — "Ambitious Stalled Marcus." Male, ~23 years old, earning $10–$35K/month in a biz-op niche, plateaued after an initial breakthrough, burned by agencies, skeptical of new solutions but still searching. See [[marcus-avatar]].

---

**Mid-ticket**
Offers priced at $500–$2,500. Lower barrier, higher volume. Requires an explicit upsell path to a high-ticket offer — a mid-ticket business with no upsell path has a revenue ceiling. See [[offer-creation]].

---

**MOF (Middle of Funnel)**
The nurture layer of the content funnel. MOF content builds trust, establishes authority, and begins qualifying the viewer as a real prospect. Sales calls start getting booked at MOF. See [[content-funnel-tof-mof-bof]].

---

**Niksetting**
The appointment-setting model that Pulsify's [[profile-funnel]] is explicitly built on. Uses story sequences, grid content, and DM automation to convert Instagram traffic into booked calls without a separate funnel page.

---

**Open loop**
A retention device used in video content: a piece of unresolved information that compels the viewer to keep watching. Must be resolved by the end of the video. See [[packaging-rules]].

---

**Outlier score**
A VidIQ metric measuring how well a video outperformed a channel's average. A score of 7–10 indicates the underlying concept drove significantly above-average performance — a validated idea to target. See [[ayman-arab]].

---

**Profile Funnel**
Pulsify's default conversion mechanism for Marcus-stage clients. Turns an Instagram profile into a functional landing page using trigger word automation, story sequences, and a setter handoff. No separate website required. See [[profile-funnel]].

---

**Setter**
A team member who manages inbound and outbound DMs, qualifies leads, and books calls into the closer's calendar. Does not close. Hired when DM volume exceeds 2 hours/day for the operator. See [[lead-sourcing-outreach]].

---

**Show rate**
The percentage of booked calls that actually show up. Industry average without hammer them: 40–50%. With full hammer them implementation: 60–70%+. A critical KPI for call funnel health.

---

**TOF (Top of Funnel)**
The attract layer of the content funnel. TOF content reaches new audiences, sparks engagement, shifts beliefs, and creates awareness that the creator and the mechanism exist. Gives the "wow," not the "how." See [[content-funnel-tof-mof-bof]].

---

**Trakyo** (transcribed as "Trackio" in the Ayman transcript — same product, trakyo.io)
The attribution tool Ayman Arab uses (or equivalent) to track which specific video drove each booked call and map the full customer journey before every booking. The non-optional tracking layer for any content-driven funnel. See [[ayman-arab]].

---

**Trigger word**
A specific keyword embedded in the Instagram bio CTA (e.g., "SBT") that viewers are instructed to DM. When sent, ManyChat fires the qualification automation. Part of the [[profile-funnel]] infrastructure.

---

**Trust recession**
The current state (2025–2026) of the biz-op coaching market in which prospects have been burned enough times that lifestyle proof, income screenshots, and generic promises no longer generate trust. Mechanism-first content and raw production formats are more effective in this environment. See [[trust-recession]].

---

**Unique mechanism**
The named proprietary methodology that differentiates a coach's offer from every other coach in the niche. Must be specific, logical, credible, and differentiated. Referenced in every video, every VSL, and every landing page headline. See [[unique-mechanism]].

---

**VSL (Video Sales Letter)**
A video-based sales presentation that walks a viewer through a structured conversion sequence: hook → problem identification → mechanism reveal → proof stack → offer reveal → call to action. The core component of a call funnel landing page. See [[vsl-framework]].

---

**Webinar**
A live or evergreen education-to-offer format used at scale. Requires cold traffic to work. Target registration CPL: $5–$12. Show rate target: 20–40%. Step 16 in [[pulsify-order-of-operations]]. See [[webinar-framework]].

---

**Wistia**
Video hosting platform recommended for VSL pages due to resume-play functionality (viewer returns to where they left off) and detailed engagement analytics. See [[vsl-framework]].
