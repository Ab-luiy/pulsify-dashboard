### Layer structure

The reusable stack is photo → tonal treatment → proof card → foreground subject → type and annotations. Use a foreground mask only where the reference actually needs overlap; pictures 6–7 work without one. Darkening is local and directional: preserve enough light on faces, hands and the environment to keep the image personal.

Type is a sans serif with changing weight and occasional italic emphasis. Exact family is unknown. Scale, weight, placement and color establish reading order: headline, one highlighted outcome or mechanism, screenshot, supporting detail. Text is composed around the person and proof, rather than placed in a universal box.

Proof panels alternate white and dark treatments. Screenshot detail is deliberately selected and sometimes enlarged. Cards have generous rounding and straightforward geometry; complex 3D rendering is not required for this effect. Arrows, underlines, circles and diamonds add an informal editorial layer. They guide the viewer toward a specific item rather than decorating every empty area.

The photos supply most of the atmosphere. Styling alone will not replace useful lighting, pose, negative space and image resolution. Instagram controls, phone status bars and capture chrome belong to the screenshots, not the desired final artwork.

### Narrative structure visible in the excerpt

The sequence opens with a self-deprecating result hook, switches to another person's claimed result, names a mechanism, presents more claimed results, shows implementation together, then connects an acquisition/nurture/close pathway. Those are observations of the story's claims, not verified outcomes. Revenue, collected cash, event counts, follower growth and buyer qualification are different measures; the pictures do not establish attribution or causation.
