# Niksetting — "New here?" highlight — the start-here sequence in the wild

**Sequence type:** start-here (delivered as a "10 lessons" educational spine with proof interludes)
**Slides:** 18 captured (slides 1-17 posted ~27 weeks ago, slide 18 added a week later — highlights accrete) | **CTA position:** slides 17-18 (end) | **Goal read:** qualification
**One-line read:** his permanent silent sales pitch: a rags-to-riches hook, ten lessons that each name a pain his offer solves, proof interludes with full funnel receipts, and a soft two-stage CTA — the entire Grow Acquisition offer taught as free lessons.

This is the single most important capture so far: the actual start-here sequence from the man who teaches start-here sequences. Note immediately that the REAL structure is richer than his taught 10-slide template (story 3 / proof 3 / framework 2 / close 2): in practice he runs 18 slides on a lesson-proof alternation. ## Sequence dissection

- **Structure map (actual, 18 slides):** hook+proof (1) -> [lesson-pain (2,4,6,8,10) alternating with proof interludes (3,5,7) and mechanism reveal (9)] -> belief-shift lessons (11-15) -> result/close (16) -> CTA from abundance (17) -> scarcity CTA appended later (18). The taught 10-slide template is the skeleton; the deployed version doubles it with proof interludes and dual CTAs.
- **The lesson template (reusable):** "Lesson N: {one word}" -> three "You might..." concessions (meeting the viewer where they are, exactly the masterclass's stage-empathy) -> "But you don't have {the precise deliverable of his offer}" -> a quotable law in quotes. Ten lessons = ten offer deliverables disguised as education. This IS "sell the vacation, name the pain" executed slide by slide.
- **Emphasis grammar:** black pill-boxes/white text as the default voice; white box + red word for proof headlines; red hand-drawn scribbles for spend receipts; green check/red X + underlines for before/after; quotes always around the "law" line; lowercase "i" everywhere. Alignment flips (left/right) to fit the photo's negative space.
- **Visual grammar:** one luxury frame per slide, rotating registers — real estate (1,6,11,12), fine dining/social (2,10,18), old-money hotel (4), team/tribe (7,16,17), stage/media (9,15), travel (13), supercar+mascot (8,14). The founder appears small-in-frame in wide luxury shots (the wealth is the subject, he inhabits it) and close-up only in authority frames (stage, podcast). Register drops to warm/domestic exactly at the close. Mochi's purple mascot recurs as the single playful thread through the flex.
- **Wealth register verdict:** maximal, unapologetic lifestyle proof — penthouses, GT3, first class. This is the largest single divergence from Pulsify doctrine in the entire capture set, and it coexists with his taught "value beats lifestyle" positioning. His resolution (from video 02/10): visible brand growth itself converts, and numbers/lifestyle are early-stage credibility scaffolding. **Pulsify's adaptation:** keep every structural element (lesson template, proof interludes, receipt density, register drop at close, dual CTA) and swap the visual currency: student dashboards, build screenshots, client calls, real workspaces. The one incongruent frame (slide 11: "keep expenses low" over a villa) shows why our currency is actually safer — his sync law violated by his own flex.
- **Proof density is the real weapon:** slide 5's naked funnel (spend -> every stage -> revenue split reconciling to the hook's number) does more selling than any lifestyle frame. That is directly portable to Pulsify with our tracking data.
- **What Pulsify adapts immediately:** the 18-slide start-here architecture for our own IG (per the ratified test doctrine, measured against the landing page); the lesson template as a generator pattern; the appended-CTA practice (living highlight); asset-bank reuse of photos.
