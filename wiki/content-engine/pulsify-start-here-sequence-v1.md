# Pulsify start-here sequence v1 — the transformation story

**Type:** start-here (permanent highlight asset), transformation register
**Slides:** 12 | **CTA position:** slide 12 (end) | **Goal:** qualification, not volume
**Highlight name:** free per founder ruling. "start here" or "my story" both fit.
**One-line read:** the honest arc from copying a guru to diagnosing businesses, carried on real work artifacts instead of lifestyle, with one reframe driven deep: the follower count is not the constraint.

## Why this shape

Every outreach DM sends the prospect to the profile before they reply. This highlight is what they tap first. Its only job is to make a stranger decide, in 12 taps, that this person understands their business. It runs the universal six steps (curiosity, pain, statement+proof, belief shift, result, close from abundance) with proof interludes between story slides, exactly the deployed Niksetting architecture, with the proof currency swapped from luxury to real work.

The transformation on show is capability, not money. That is a constraint the founder named himself and it is also the stronger play: in a trust recession the honest version outperforms the flex, and it is the only version that survives contact with the truth.

## The slides

### SLIDE 1 — curiosity (pattern interrupt)

**TEXT**
```
i've generated six figures for other people's businesses.

i still don't own a car.

this is the honest version of the story.
```

**EMPHASIS:** black pill-boxes, white text. "honest" flipped to red inside the third pill.
**PHOTO:** the old laptop. Open, on whatever surface it actually lived on, phone-raw, unstyled, slightly messy. The point is that it looks used, not photographed.
**ALT PHOTO:** wide shot of him at that old desk, small in frame, back to camera.
**INTERACTIVE:** none.
**JOB:** curiosity. In a niche where every account opens on a flex, opening on the absence of one is the interrupt.

**Alt opener A:** "i watched one iman gadzhi video and started an agency the same week. / it took three years to learn the part i skipped."
**Alt opener B:** "this laptop is where i lost three years. / it's also the only reason i can do what i do now."

### SLIDE 2 — the scene

**TEXT**
```
three years ago i watched one video and started an agency that week.

me and a guy i met on discord. no offer, no idea who we were actually selling to.

money came in spikes. then nothing for months.
```

**EMPHASIS:** red underline under "no offer, no idea who we were actually selling to."
**PHOTO:** same old laptop, closer, or the old room. Keep the visual thread from slide 1.
**ALT PHOTO:** a real screenshot from back then if any survives. Old Discord thread, old client chat, an old invoice. Blur names. A real artifact beats a staged shot.
**JOB:** obstacle. This is his pain and the viewer's pain at once.

### SLIDE 3 — the pain, named properly

**TEXT**
```
the problem was never effort.

we posted. we sent dms. we built things.

but nothing behind the content was built. so the content had nowhere to send anyone.
```

**EMPHASIS:** red scribbled underline under "nothing behind the content was built."
**PHOTO:** phone-raw shot of a cluttered desk mid-work, or a screen with twelve tabs open. Ambient, not staged.
**JOB:** obstacle sharpened. Pain, not situation, per the framework.

### SLIDE 4 — the turn

**TEXT**
```
so i stopped trying to grow an audience and learned the part everyone skips.

offers. funnels. what actually happens after someone clicks.

first person i applied it to was a trading coach.
```

**PHOTO:** whiteboard, Miro board, or a notebook page with a funnel sketched on it. Real handwriting.
**JOB:** statement, sets up the receipt.

**PHOTO:** the receipt. Payment dashboard, bank line, or the client's own message saying the number. Crop tight to the figure, blur the name.
**JOB:** statement plus proof. This is the densest dwell slide in the first half.

### SLIDE 7 — the honest slide

**TEXT**
```
then i lost a client.

nick. built his entire content engine, real revenue on the board, and he still left.

he expected it faster than it happens, and i never set that expectation upfront. that one's on me.

i set it upfront with every client now.
```

**EMPHASIS:** no red here. Plain white drop-shadow text on the photo. The absence of annotation is the signal that this slide is not selling.
**PHOTO:** the actual content engine build. Miro board, the content pipeline, the docs. Screen-recording frame is fine.
**ALT PHOTO:** the Mason Bowstead podcast frame, since that episode was about this exact case study.
**JOB:** belief shift setup. Admitting the churn buys more trust than the two receipts above it, and it pre-handles the objection every skeptical prospect is already holding.

---

### SLIDE 8 — the reframe (the one belief shift)

**TEXT**
```
if you're at 500, 2,000, 5,000 followers and you think that's the problem

it isn't.

my biggest client win came from an audience that already existed. i didn't add a single follower to it.

the count isn't the constraint. what's underneath it is.
```

**EMPHASIS:** "it isn't." in its own red-boxed pill, alone on the line. Green underline under the final line.
**PHOTO:** a real analytics screen showing a small follower number next to a real revenue or booking number. If that screenshot does not exist, use a plain black background with the text alone. A missing artifact beats a fake one.
**JOB:** the reframe. This is the only belief shift in the sequence and it is the founder-ratified horizon position. Everything before it exists to earn it, everything after it assumes it.

---

### SLIDE 9 — the mechanism

**TEXT**
```
so here's what i actually do now.

i don't grow accounts. i build the three things that make an account make money.

content that pulls the right person in. an offer they can't price-compare. a funnel that catches every one of them.

break one, the other two stop mattering.
```

**EMPHASIS:** each of the three items on its own line, each with a green check. "break one" pill in red.
**PHOTO:** device mockup or split screen showing all three: a piece of content, an offer doc, a live funnel page. Or three stacked real screenshots.
**JOB:** statement. Content, offer, funnel, in that order, which is core Pulsify doctrine and the actual diagnostic he sells.

---

### SLIDE 10 — where he is now (register drop)

**TEXT**
```
this is the setup now.

still a bedroom. still studying engineering full time. still not rich.

but i can look at a coach's page for ten minutes and tell you exactly which of the three is broken.

that's the whole transformation. it isn't the desk.
```

**EMPHASIS:** minimal. One quiet underline under "which of the three is broken."
**PHOTO:** the new setup. Wide, phone-raw, honest. Do not clean it into a showroom. The gap between this and slide 1 is the entire visual argument, and it only works if both photos are equally unposed.
**JOB:** result, plus the deliberate register drop before the ask. Niksetting drops to a warm domestic frame at exactly this point for exactly this reason.

---

### SLIDE 11 — current work proof

**TEXT**
```
and this is what i build for people now.
```

**PHOTO:** the real artifacts. Client dashboard, a landing page he shipped, the CRM, a client call frame, the outreach assets. Two or three tiled behind the line works better than one.
**JOB:** proof that the transformation produced a deliverable, not just a story.

---

### SLIDE 12 — close from abundance + CTA

**TEXT**
```
i'm not going to pitch you.

if you're a coach or creator doing real numbers, and you can feel that something behind your content is broken, i'll build you the one piece i'd fix first.

free. no call, no strings. i just want to be useful to the right people.

dm me "{TRIGGER}" and send me your page.
```

**EMPHASIS:** the trigger word in a red-boxed pill, the only red on the slide.
**PHOTO:** him, close-up, phone-raw, looking at camera. This is the one authority close-up in the sequence and it should be the least produced photo of the twelve.
**INTERACTIVE:** none. Keep the DM as the only path.
**JOB:** close from abundance.

**Trigger word:** founder to pick. Not "audit" or "review" — the ratified prospect-asset doctrine is built work, never diagnosis, so the promise on this slide has to be a built thing. "gameplan," "build," "piece" all fit.

**Volume note:** promising built work at scale is expensive. If the DM volume becomes real, swap the promise to a lighter one rather than breaking it.

## Why this order

Two receipts land before the reframe so the reframe reads as earned rather than asserted. The churn admission sits between the proof and the reframe because trust is highest right after a concession, which is where the belief shift has to land. The setup-now slide arrives after the mechanism so the modest desk reads as proof of the argument rather than as an apology.

## Design direction (applies to all 12)

Per [[content-engine/sequence-carousel-design-system]]: heavy bold sans, headlines in white rounded pill-boxes with black text, body copy as white drop-shadow text on the photo. Lowercase throughout including "i." Alignment flips left and right per slide to sit in each photo's negative space. Annotations are marker-brush, never clean vector, one to three per slide maximum. Red for pain or emphasis, green for the right move, blue for directional arrows.

No lifestyle frames. No cars, watches, hotels, cash. Every image is a proof screenshot, a device mockup, a proof wall, or a real workspace.
