# Pulsify SaaS highlight sequence (copy v4, two-businesses angle)

Permanent highlight for outreach prospects who check the profile. Core frame: info is a second business on top of your craft; Pulsify (system + consultation) runs it alongside. Never labelled an app. Ask: none. Slide 7 = founder on camera + motion graphic with the NEW logo. Design/motion: Codex, per [[synthesis/pulsify-product-launch-direction]]. Status: v4 awaiting founder lock.

## Slide 1
i've been working on this for a whole year now.
because everyone in this space is stuck on the same problem.

## Slide 2
you're already good at what you do.
trading. tiktok shop. whatever it is.
and you know a mentorship can make you more than the thing itself.
but info is a whole business on its own.
so now you're learning two businesses at once.

## Slide 3
and that's where it gets heavy:
- you want to post, but you don't know what to say
- you know what to say, but nobody's watching
- people are watching, but you don't know where to send them
- you have a page, but you can't tell who's actually interested
same problem. different angle.

## Slide 4
so you post something.
you're not sure if it worked.
a couple of people dm you.
you lose half of them in the chat.
and you have no idea which post brought them in.
then you do it all again tomorrow.

## Slide 5
so you try harder.
more posts. more tools. another chatgpt tab at 2am.
you start copying what everyone else does.
and you end up promoting their format instead of building your own.
meanwhile the thing that actually pays you is getting less of your time.

## Slide 6
that's where most people plateau.
then they pick the main business, because that's what keeps food on the table.
and the info side dies quietly.
it's not that they're bad at it.
they just had no direction and no one to connect the dots.
none of that is your fault.

## Slide 7 (on camera + motion graphic, new logo)
On-screen: so i built the bridge.
Spoken (~20s): you shouldn't have to give up what you're good at to build an info business. so i took everything i've learned about content, funnels and selling, and built one place that runs it next to what you already do. what to post. how to say it. where to send people. who's ready to buy. this is pulsify.

## Slide 8
now you sit down and you already know what to make.
everything's in one place. no more 100 tabs.
it's written in your voice.
the people who see it land somewhere that sells.
and you're not figuring it out alone. we work through it with you.

## Slide 9
even when you're not sure a post was good,
you still see who watched, who clicked, and where they dropped off.
we built our own tracking for exactly that.
"you never post blind again."

## Slide 10
none of this was built overnight.
every framework was tested first.
then turned into one place that does the heavy lifting, so you can keep doing what you're good at.

## Slide 11
right now it's only for the people i work with.
it's getting sharper every week.
and i'm not stopping until this is a seven-figure company.
