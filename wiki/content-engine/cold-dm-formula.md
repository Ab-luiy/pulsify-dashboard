# Cold DM formula (casual-peer, gift-first)

The repeatable structure for a first-touch cold DM that ships a pre-built asset (usually a landing page) to a creator. Built and pressure-tested with the founder on 2026-07-06 over a full iteration pass. This is the current standard for IG and email cold opens. Feed me a creator plus one real detail from their content and I generate this without re-deriving the voice.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: this supersedes the DM voice in [[playbooks/pulse-system-outreach]] Step 4, which bans "bro"/casual openers and compliments and says "the asset is the message, no 'want me to send it over?'". The founder deliberately moved to a warmer, casual-peer voice with a genuine content compliment and a soft reply-inviting close. Resolution: for cold DMs, this page is the standard as of 2026-07-06. The old playbook's diagnostic Format A is retired for DMs; its research/follow-up/call steps still stand.

## Why this matters for Pulsify

The DM is the first three or four words a creator sees in an IG request preview. It decides whether they open it at all. The old diagnostic voice ("submitted your funnel, your Typeform lets unqualified leads through") reads like an audit from a stranger and triggers sales resistance. The new posture is a peer operator doing them a favor with zero ego and zero begging: I noticed something, I built you something in response, it's yours if you want it. That gets the reply.

## The five beats

1. **Casual opener.** Lowercase, peer, no name-drop, no quote. The first words have to read like a real person, not a pitch, because that's all the IG preview shows. The lane is understated and lightly self-deprecating (you got pulled in), never fawning. Founder-approved default rotation (2026-07-06):
   - `ngl your channel kinda took over my week` (the favorite)
   - `not gonna lie your vids kinda hijacked my week`

   Both work because they admit you watched a lot without gushing about the creator. Rotate between them; a stronger, less-glazing "rabbit hole" variant (`went down a bit of a rabbit hole on your channel this week`) is a fallback. Retired: `yo been deep in your vids this week` (fine, but flatter than the approved two).
2. **Genuine reference plus a plain nod.** `the way you run products at break even and let the recurring carry it, really like that.` One specific detail pulled from an actual video, transcript, or the Lead Studio profile. It proves you watched past the thumbnail. A plain "really like it," never glazing ("smartest thing in the niche").
3. **Soft gap, framed as opportunity not flaw.** `also noticed you don't really have a page that captures people and educates them on what you can help with.` Never name the gap as a failure. Never mention a dead IG or a bad funnel. The gap is an opening, not a verdict.
4. **The gift.** `so i just went ahead and put one together for you real quick.`
5. **Ownership plus a soft reply-inviting close.** `it's 100% yours, take a look and lmk what you think [link].`

## The template

Fill the brackets:

> yo been deep in your [vids/channel] this week. [genuine nod to the one specific thing they teach], really like it. also noticed you don't really have a [landing page / real page] that captures people and educates them on what you can help with, so i just went ahead and put one together for you. it's 100% yours, take a look and lmk what you think [link]

## Worked example — Frank Wieler (real)

> not gonna lie your vids kinda hijacked my week. the way you run products at break even and let the recurring carry it, really like that. also noticed you don't really have a page that captures people and educates them on the model, so i just put one together for you. it's 100% yours, take a look and lmk what you think [frank-wieler.pages.dev]

The reference is Frank's own least-obvious take (break-even ROAS, let the MRR carry it — from [[raw-sources/client-raw/frank-wieler/profile.md]]), which is exactly the detail that can't be faked from a thumbnail. That is what earns a nine-figure-brained operator's reply.

## Non-negotiable rules

- Lowercase, casual, frat-adjacent (yo, bro, ngl, lowkey fine). Not street/hood, not corporate.
- No em-dashes. No emojis. No exclamation marks. Commas and periods only.
- The reference goes in the middle, never the opener. Do not open with a quote of their words — it kills the preview.
- One specific detail max. More than one reads as trying too hard.
- Never name the gap as a flaw. Never hit ego (no "your IG is dead," no "your funnel is weak").
- Abundance posture: you built it, it's theirs, no begging. Optional light scarcity ("only build a few of these") if it fits.
- Drop the link, but still invite a reply ("lmk what you think") so they don't just lurk and ghost. (Founder decision 2026-07-06: link in the first message on IG.)
- Stay safe-generic about their setup unless a fact is confirmed. Don't assert "Gumroad"/"skool" unless you know it. Wrong facts read as mass outreach.
- Trim hard: 30-45 words. The shortest version that keeps the specific detail wins. Founder tested length on IG and cut ~50%.
- Vary the opener and the reaction verb across a batch ("stuck with me" / "reframed it" / "really like that") so two sends never rhyme.

## Grading rubric (out of 100)

Used when iterating a draft:

- Base competent DM in this structure: ~85.
- +5-10 for a real, least-obvious reference from an actual video (a generic placeholder caps the score at ~85).
- +2-3 for ego-safe gap framing (opportunity, not audit).
- +2-3 for ruthless length (every extra clause costs).
- -points for em-dashes, glazing, naming the gap as a flaw, or a reference so generic it could be sent to anyone.
- A 95 = real specific detail + ego-safe + tight + reply-inviting close.

## Cold email variant

Same voice, same five beats. Email adds two things: a subject line and a sign-off. Everything between them is the DM with line breaks. (2026-07-06: no cold-email asset existed before this — the Ben Bader swipe file is warm-list nurture, hammer-them is post-booking. This variant ports the DM formula into email.)

**Subject line — the open-decider.** Lowercase, short, curiosity, never salesy. Two lanes:

- **Curiosity:** `made you something`, `you're leaving money in the description`. Gives nothing away, safe, pairs with any body.
- **Witty situation-metaphor (customizable, founder-approved 2026-07-06):** `million dollar model, no front door`, `ferrari engine, bicycle brakes`, `you built the engine, forgot the keys`, `great model, nowhere to send people`. These are a pattern, not fixed lines. Shape: **[their impressive result/model] + [the missing or broken container]**, or **[premium engine] + [cheap failure point]**. Swap both halves per creator. Aimed at the situation, never the person — playful, not an insult. These dig into the pain without naming a flaw.

**One-joke rule.** A witty subject plus the witty opener is two jokes before he reads a sentence. Pick one: witty subject with a straight body open, or a curiosity subject and let the body carry the one joke. One witty element total.

**Sign-off.** Lowercase first name. No title, no "best regards."

### Worked example — Frank (tuned)

> subject: you built the engine, forgot the keys
>
> not gonna lie your vids kinda hijacked my week. the way you run products at break even and let the recurring carry it, really like that.
>
> also noticed there's no real page capturing people and walking them through the model, so i went ahead and built you one.
>
> it's 100% yours. take a look and lmk what you think:
>
> [link]
>
> — [name]

Note the tightening from the raw DM port: dropped the doubled connector ("anyway i also"), swapped "educates them on" for the warmer "walking them through," trimmed the gift to "went ahead and built you one," and gave "it's 100% yours." its own beat with a period.

**Deploy risk (not copy).** A cold email with a single bare link to a fresh `.pages.dev` domain is a spam trigger, and deliverability tanks without SPF/DKIM/DMARC on the sending domain. A single hand-sent email from a normal inbox lands fine; batch sends need the sending infrastructure set up first. This is a separate, unsolved setup item — do not treat the copy being ready as the send being safe at volume.

## How Pulsify uses this

This is the default cold-DM generator. The founder vets and picks the lead (the human "is this a good lead" call stays with him), then hands over the creator plus a transcript or their top video. I pull the one specific reference and produce the DM at a 95. The asset it points to is usually the pre-built landing page (Codex-owned, per [[playbooks/pulse-system-outreach]] and the landing-page standard). Email uses the same five beats with a different opener so the same person sees fresh copy on both channels.

## The follow-up (drafted 2026-08-04, untested)

Two prospects received a full asset and went silent. The diagnosis is structural rather than a copy problem: **the document has no CTA by design, and the first DM asks for nothing, so nowhere in the sequence is the prospect ever given a reason to type.** They read it, think well of it, and have nothing to reply to. The follow-up has to carry the ask the doc deliberately does not.

Its job is to be the easiest possible message to answer. Not a reminder, never "did you see it."

**Option A, the small question. Recommended.**

> yo did the thumbnail part land or was that too much at once? only asking cause im putting these together for a few people and wanna know what's actually useful

One question, answerable in a word, about them rather than us. The stated reason for asking is not a pitch, and "for a few people" carries quiet social proof without a claim. Swap the noun per prospect: thumbnails for a packaging asset, the story sequence for a sequence asset.

**Option B, gift again.**

> forgot to put this in the doc. [one specific extra thing]. no reply needed, just thought you'd want it

Reopens the thread with the same gift-first posture and asks nothing. Needs one genuinely useful extra thing in hand.

**Option C, the clean exit.**

> no stress if that wasn't useful. if you did want to run any of it and got stuck, i'll answer anything on it. either way good luck with the eval stuff

Permission to be done, which sometimes draws a reply on its own. Can read slightly wounded, so it goes last.

**Timing:** 3 to 5 days, proposed not proven. Log what happens; this section becomes doctrine only when reply data exists.
