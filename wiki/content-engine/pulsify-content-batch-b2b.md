# Pulsify content batch — B2B / operator-facing (parked)

> **⚠ POSITIONING WARNING — read before shooting.** These nine titles speak in
> *operator / business-of-coaching* language: "growth operating," "don't start
> an agency," "$9k/mo VA team," "coaching backend." That vocabulary attracts
> **aspiring operators, agency owners, closers, and appointment setters** — the
> exact people on Pulsify's OUT list ([[project_yt_lead_criteria]]). It does NOT
> attract the B2C money-skill coaches we actually want as clients (day traders,
> Amazon FBA, dropshipping, TikTok-shop coaches). Use this set only if/when
> Pulsify deliberately markets to other operators. For client acquisition, use
> [[pulsify-content-niche-batches-2026-06]] instead.

Nine packaged title + thumbnail pairs originally built for Pulsify's own channel
(~17 subs). Built with the validated title formulas, competitor bank, and Ayman
thumbnail rules from the titles-thumbnails generator. Three pairs per pillar.

**Figures are real or honest:** Nick's $20k→$50k/mo arc (confirmed video
testimonial; his true floor was ~$10k trading-only), Pulsify's own price band
($5–12k base), and a defensible industry cost estimate for a 3-person VA team
(~$9k/mo, per the offer page's "replaces a 3-person VA team"). No fabricated
client results.

**Suggested shoot order (warmest hook first):** 4 → 7 → 2 → 1 → 5 → 8 → 3 → 6 → 9.

## Notes for production

- Every title obeys the hard rules: lowercase, specific figure/measurable, no question marks, parenthetical kicker, under ~60 chars, front-loaded.
- Every thumbnail: face in, ≤4 words, completes (never repeats) the title, authority background, deadpan/mild-surprise expression.
- Pillar 2 (Nick) videos are the warmest — they carry real proof, shoot those first.
- Swap in fresher client proof as new wins land; re-run the generator per new result.
