# Next video - sales and funnel automation

The next Pulsify video should show Marcus how AI can automate and improve the sales/funnel layer of an info-offer business. The video should not sell "AI tools." It should show the operating system underneath booked calls, qualification, pre-call nurture, objection handling, and follow-up.

## Why this matters for Pulsify

This video sits between content and funnel. Marcus already understands he needs more leads. What he usually misses is that more leads do not matter if the funnel leaks, the show rate is weak, the application form filters badly, and no pre-call system builds belief before the call.

This video should make the [[concepts/pulse-system|Pulse System]] feel practical, not theoretical.

## Core angle

Most coaches think their sales problem is more calls. The real problem is that the system between attention and the call is leaking.

AI can now compress the buildout of that system:

- Diagnose the funnel leak
- Rewrite the application flow
- Build pre-call assets
- Create objection-specific follow-up
- Train setter/closer scripts from actual call data
- Track where prospects disappear

The claim is not "AI closes deals for you." The claim is "AI lets a serious operator build the sales infrastructure faster than a traditional team."

## Structural direction

### Hook

Show the viewer the hidden leak:

They are chasing more content, more DMs, and more booked calls while half the revenue is disappearing between opt-in and close.

### Problem

Marcus has no sales infrastructure:

- Weak or missing Typeform
- Generic Calendly flow
- No pre-call sequence
- No objection content before the call
- No follow-up based on buyer type
- No setter/closer tracking
- No disposition data

### Mechanism

Position this as the Pulse Brain layer:

Pulse Brain is the lead and sales infrastructure layer of the [[concepts/pulse-system|Pulse System]]. It turns attention into qualified calls and turns booked calls into showed-up, pre-sold conversations.

### Demo

The video should show a practical build:

1. Take a coach's broken funnel.
2. Map the current flow.
3. Identify the leak.
4. Use AI to rebuild the Typeform/application questions.
5. Generate a pre-call sequence based on the buyer's wound.
6. Create three short pre-call asset scripts.
7. Show how the CRM/status tracker would label each lead.

### CTA

Invite the right viewer to apply or DM with their funnel if they already have a converting offer but no sales infrastructure.

## What not to do

- Do not make this a generic "AI sales automation" video.
- Do not overpromise that AI replaces sales skill.
- Do not focus on tools before diagnosis.
- Do not turn it into a tutorial for beginners with no offer.
