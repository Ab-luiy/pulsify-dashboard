# Pulsify methodology carousel v1 — the back-end

**Structure:** methodology-as-document (structure 1 of 3 in [[frameworks/carousel-system]])
**Slides:** 8 | **CTA:** slide 8, routes to the story highlight, no trigger word
**Goal:** a profile-clicker arriving from a cold DM finishes this knowing exactly what gets installed and whether they want it. It is a landing page sitting in the grid.

## Why this structure and not the taxonomy one

The taxonomy carousel (teach a framework free, chip-coded) is the save-and-reach play. Reach needs either an audience or algorithmic pickup and the account has neither. Every viewer this month arrives from a DM the founder sent, which means the carousel's only job is conversion, not discovery. The methodology structure does that job on an audience of one. Build the taxonomy version the month reels start pulling strangers.

## The scope note worth reading before building

The standing rule in the vault is that niche content is the craft itself and never meta-content about content and funnels. That rule governs reach content, where meta-content pulls other operators instead of coaches. This carousel is not reach content. It is an offer document shown to people already in the DMs, and its audience is coaches by definition. The distinction is the traffic source. If this ever gets used as a reach play, the rule applies again and the carousel is the wrong asset.

## The artifact constraint (real, flag it)

Per [[concepts/pulse-system-mechanism]], no client has run the full v1 mechanism end to end. Nick had the Content Engine and parts of the Funnel Stack, never the agent layer. So pillars 4 and 5 have no client-side artifact.

The honest fix, which is also the better one: use the founder's own systems as the artifacts and label them as his own. He has the CRM, the operator console, the funnel view-tracking, the integrations hub, the outreach pipeline, all built and running on himself. "This is what I run on, and it is what I install" is a stronger claim than a borrowed screenshot, and it is true.

**Never stage a client dashboard that does not exist.** A missing artifact beats a fake one, and it beats it by a lot.

## Why this order

The wedge goes before the pillars because a feature list read cold is a price objection waiting to happen, and read after the wedge it is a relief. The two pillars with real client artifacts (Content Engine, Funnel Stack) sit in the middle where swipe depth is still high. The two carried by the founder's own systems sit at 4 and 5, where the reader has already decided, so the honest "my own system" label reads as transparency rather than as a gap.

## Design direction

Per [[content-engine/sequence-carousel-design-system]]. Cover pairs display serif italic with a giant bold sans number, key nouns hand-underlined. Interior slides run a giant light-gray ghost number behind a heavy sans headline and a lighter sans body. Marker-brush annotations only, never clean vector, one to three per slide. Red for the pain or the wrong path, green for the right move.

No chip row on this one. Chips belong to the taxonomy structure, where a reader is comparing items. Here they are comparing nothing, they are reading a spine.

No lifestyle frames. Every image is a proof screenshot, a device mockup, a proof wall, or a real workspace.

## Artifact shot list
