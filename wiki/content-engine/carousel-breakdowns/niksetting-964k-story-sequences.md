## Carousel dissection

- **Cover analysis:** the precise dollar figure ($964,853, not "~$1M") is a specificity hook — exact numbers read as real. Story-ring viewer overlay signals the medium being sold.
- **Structure map:** cover (specific-number hook) -> 7 type slides (name+number woven / definition / 3-chip matrix / real mockups) -> final CTA with a reach-proof donut. The 7 slides ARE the value; the carousel teaches the whole taxonomy for free and sells the "in-depth breakdown" as the next step.
- **Design system:** the number-woven-into-the-word typography is the signature; the three-chip color code (purple=cadence, gray=function, green=fit) is a reusable at-a-glance system Pulsify should adopt for any "types of X" carousel. Real mockups below the fold reward the swipe and prove each type isn't theoretical.
- **The swipe engine:** numbered 1-7 (strongest driver) + the promise of "all 7 types" in the cover creates completion pull.
- **Caption + CTA:** comment-trigger ("sequence") here vs the DM-trigger ("GA") on the onboarding carousel — he varies CTA mechanics per post; comment-triggers boost engagement/reach, DM-triggers route higher-intent. Worth A/B awareness for Pulsify.
- **Authority signal vs surface language:** STEAL the matrix itself (it's now framework canon), the number-in-word typography, the 3-chip code, the real-mockup-proves-it structure. SWAP the surface: the lifestyle-type framing ("creates desire," poolside) and biz-opp/lifestyle-flex register -> Pulsify's lifestyle type is humanity-not-flex, and the "creates desire" function is served by mission/transformation, not possessions.
