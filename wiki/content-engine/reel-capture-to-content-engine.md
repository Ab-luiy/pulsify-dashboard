# The reel capture-to-content engine

Reels have two sources. One is redistribution: cutting a long-form video or a sales call into clips. The other, and the one most creators underuse, is their own mind. A win, a strategy, an objection they just handled, a belief they hold, all of it is content the moment it is captured and routed through a proven angle. This engine gives the creator a place to dump raw thoughts and get back formatted content, and it removes the guesswork of "will this work" by routing every thought through a validated angle and then letting the data confirm which ones convert.

## Why this matters for Pulsify

Content is the mover of everything, and the richest content source is what is already in the creator's head. Most creators lose those thoughts because there is no capture surface and no confidence that a raw idea is worth posting. The founder named the exact gap: he can turn anything into a piece but hesitates because he lacks data to back the intuition. The fix is a system that (1) captures the thought instantly, (2) routes it through an angle that has already worked, so it is a choice not a gamble, and (3) feeds every posted reel into the same diagnose loop the sequences use, so the intuition gets backed by real numbers over a few weeks. The system builds the proof the creator feels is missing.

## The two modes

- **Redistribution.** Paste a long-form video or a recorded call. The engine finds the punchy 30 to 60 second moments, cuts them, writes a hook, and captions them. This is the low-effort half: one long asset becomes a week of clips.
- **Origination.** A thought becomes a talking-head reel. The creator thinks it, the engine frames it (hook, beats, CTA placement), the creator films it in their own words. This is the half that taps the mind.

Both modes feed one content calendar and the same review gate.

## The capture inbox

A single always-open surface where the creator drops a raw thought the moment it hits, by one line of text or a voice note. No formatting required. Examples: "closed a client who was skeptical about paying upfront", "most people set their bio to a job title and get skipped". The friction has to be near zero or the thoughts are lost.

## The translation layer

One captured thought returns several formatted options, because one idea should not be one post. The default expansion is four:

- A reel (hook plus talking-head beats).
- A story sequence (the idea across the 6-step slide structure).
- A carousel (the idea as a saveable set).
- A long-form segment (the deeper breakdown for YouTube).

This is the derivative pipeline already in the vault: one idea, many formats, matched to where each format sits on the [[content-engine/reel-format-ladder]].

## The angle library (removes the guesswork)

Every thought routes through one of a fixed set of proven angles, so the creator is choosing a validated path, not inventing a format each time:

- A win to a credibility sequence or a proof reel.
- A new strategy to a value-driven sequence or a breakdown.
- A recurring belief to a philosophy carousel or a reframe reel.
- An objection you heard to a Q and A sequence or a myth-buster reel.
- A behind-the-scenes moment to a day-in-the-life story or a lifestyle reel.
- A client transformation to a proof pin post plus a long-form breakdown.

The angle set maps directly onto the seven story-sequence types and the reel ladder, so the whole content system stays coherent.

## The reframe rotator (repeat without repeating)

One core belief gets generated in many presentations: a reel, a story, a carousel, a long-form breakdown, and a bare hook, each with a different angle and opening. This is how the money message gets repeated for months without feeling repetitive, and it is exactly the "same idea reframed" doctrine from the messaging system. The creator picks one belief they want known for and the rotator keeps producing fresh presentations of it.

## The confidence loop (intuition, then data)

Every reel that posts is logged into the same drop-off and performance diagnosis the story sequences use. After a few weeks the engine can say which angles and which kinds of thoughts actually convert for this creator, so the intuition stops being a guess. This directly answers the founder's stated hesitation: you start on validated angles, and the system builds the data that proves which of your thoughts are worth more.

## How Pulsify uses this

1. **Service delivery surface:** the Reels tab in the client dashboard is the capture inbox plus the two modes plus the angle library plus the rotator. The creator dumps thoughts and clips videos; Pulsify reviews; the creator films and posts.
2. **The one-idea-to-many rule:** no thought produces a single asset. The default is a reel plus three sibling formats, so content volume compounds from a small number of real ideas.
3. **Guardrails:** the currency rule holds here too. Origination reels are proof, strategy, and belief, never lifestyle flex.

## Open questions / gaps

- Auto-clipping quality (finding the right 30 to 60 second moments in a long video) needs a real transcript-plus-scoring pass; the manual version works today.
- The confidence loop needs per-reel metrics captured the way sequences are; the metric surface exists, the reel-specific inputs need wiring.
- Voice-note capture needs a transcription step; text capture works now.
