# Niche craft title batches — by money-making niche

Title + thumbnail templates for content in each target niche. **The content is the
craft itself** — how the creator made their money, the strategies, the products,
the setups, the obstacles they beat. Trading content is about trading. FBA content
is about how you made money on Amazon. Dropshipping content is about the products
and ad strategies that worked.

**It is NOT about:** content systems, funnels, "selling your course," repurposing,
or any meta-layer. Those angles attract nobody and signal nothing. The money-making
strategy IS the hook.

## How to use figures honestly

These are **angle templates** — the structure and hook style for each niche. The `$`
figures shown are **illustrative placeholders**; swap in the real creator's actual
numbers before publishing. The only confirmed real client number we own is **Nick,
trading** ($10–20k/mo trading-only → $50k/mo trading + info offer —
[[clients/nick/overview]]). Don't publish a specific result as real unless it is.

## Two title tiers — play both worlds (same as the query bank)

Every niche carries two tiers, for the same reason the lead queries do
([[youtube-lead-sourcing-query-bank]]):

- **BROAD / top-of-funnel (wide reach):** milestone/journey framing (`$0 → $10k`,
  `first $1k`, `0 to six figures`) or no number at all. Evergreen, highly
  searched, maps onto *tons* of channels and viewers — not one data point.
  This is the discovery layer.
- **SPECIFIC / proof (high credibility):** pinpoint odd numbers (`$38k`, `$27k`,
  `$84k`). Credible and clickable, but describe one creator's exact result.
  This is the proof/MOF layer.

A pinpoint figure isn't wrong — specificity sells — but without a broad tier the
library only speaks to a sliver of the niche. Build both; lead discovery with
broad, close with specific.

## Notes for production

- Hard rules unchanged: lowercase, specific figure/measurable, no question marks, parenthetical kicker, under ~60 chars, front-loaded.
- **The hook is always the money-making craft** — the strategy, the product, the trade, the number. Never the meta-layer (content/funnels/selling).
- Figures are illustrative templates — swap in the real creator's actual numbers before publishing. Only Nick (trading) is a confirmed real client result.
- Niche vocabulary signals relevance: setups/funded/price action; product/sourcing/PPC/private label; winning product/ROAS/ad strategy; commission/affiliate/seeding/creator marketplace.
