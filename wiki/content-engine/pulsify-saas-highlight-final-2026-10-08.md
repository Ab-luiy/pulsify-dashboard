# Pulsify SaaS highlight, final 13-slide version (founder-approved 2026-10-08)

This is the shipped copy and design for the pre-launch highlight. It supersedes [[content-engine/pulsify-saas-highlight-14-slide]], which stays as history. Slide 14 was deleted by the founder. Slide 9 is the founder's own video. Renders are in `output/highlight-saas/claude-v2/final-r16/` (slide 1 is `01.png` there, built from `slide1_mockups8.py`; slides 2 to 13 come from the same set as `final-r15/`, built by the `slides_r4` to `slides_r12` chain).

## Why this matters for Pulsify

The highlight is a shiny-object pre-launch shown to cold prospects. The founder's rule: nobody watches all slides, so every slide has to be gratifying alone, show how much Pulsify knows, and make the viewer feel what they are missing. The ask is only "watch". No CTA, no scarcity.

## Final copy and design, slide by slide

1. Type behind the founder (one line): "i've been working on this all year," then "all day every day." Small logo mark above. Then "i noticed something every creator i've worked with has in common." and "everyone is stuck on the same problem." Zoomed-out photo, top filled with the wall's own black, founder centered, exposure lifted.
2. "you're already good at what you do." Underlined bullets: trading, tiktok shop, e-commerce, amazon fba. "running your own offer, teaching people exactly what you do, will make you more money than the thing you do now." "but info is a whole business on its own." "your own offer. your own unique mechanism. your own positioning." Folded icon groups: YouTube + Instagram (content + audience), Cal.com + Calendly (sales calls), Stripe + Whop + Fanbasis (payments). "so now you're running 2 businesses at once."
3. "the whole chain." Six cards: content (top, middle, bottom of funnel side by side), personal brand (people start buying into you), traffic (runners heading right), landing page (vsl, webinar, profile funnel, ascension, challenge), sales team (closer, setter, manager), cash in the bank (Stripe, Whop, Fanbasis). Closing text on a revenue-chart card: "most people have never run it. some don't even know what a kpi is. and that's fine. nobody teaches you this part."
4. Same photo as 3, blurred. "it breaks somewhere:" with four red-X lines (post / nobody watching / where to send / who is closing).
5. "your traffic is leaking." Inbox card with the founder's four supplied reaction photos as avatars, black inbox card, rows named by lead source in Title Case (Youtube Lead, Carousel Lead, Story Lead, Reel Lead) with message previews ("bro this is exactly what i need", "seen 2 days ago", "booked for thursday 3pm", "ghosted after the call") and status pills (replied, seen no reply, booked, no close). Gradient-initials version kept as final-r16/05-initials.png. Photos are celebrity likenesses supplied by the founder, so the rows carry no names; do not label them as real people.
6. Approved as is. Brez layout on the slide 5 photo, blurred, no ghost text: "so you try harder." red X list, "you start copying the big names." black box with red "their", "MEANWHILE THE THING THAT PAYS YOU GETS LESS OF YOUR TIME."
7. "that's where most people plateau." "then they pick the main business, because that's what keeps food on the table." "and the info side dies quietly." "none of that is your fault."
8. "here's what most people miss." "the top of every niche is 3 or 4 names." Unnamed niche leaderboard plus a "new face, new idea" climbing 2k to 18k+. "but every month, a new face breaks in." 13. "none of this was built overnight." "every framework was tested first." "it's a one-place stop that does the heavy lifting." "you keep doing what you're good at." No logo.

## Rules the founder set while building this (reusable for client sequences)

- Palette for story elements: red, black, white and green (green only for good numbers). No yellow or orange. Real brand logos keep their own colours.
- Brez typography: bold white stack, red underlines (thick, solid), red X marks, black box with one red word. Kobey-style red gradient label boxes behind one phrase per slide.
- Reuse a few photos two or three times with a blurred and dimmed carry-over instead of one new photo per slide. Natural cover crops only. No mirrored fill. A zoomed-out wide shot with the top filled by the photo's own black is allowed on slide 1.
- Redesign app screens as clean designed cards with a few words each. Never paste raw screenshots. Never print "example workspace" or "sample data".
- Numbers on slides 8, 11 and 12 are illustrative and unlabelled by founder choice (intrigue, not proof). Never present them as client results elsewhere.
- Real brand glyphs (open Simple Icons set) for platforms; Whop and Fanbasis from the Codex asset folder. No TikTok Shop glyph exists, so TikTok plus a bag badge is used.
- No real people's names or faces for the niche leaderboard (association and accuracy risk). Use unnamed rows, or the client's own real top names with their permission.
- Subtle logo only on slide 1. Logo removed from slides 2 to 14.
