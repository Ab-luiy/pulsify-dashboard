# Copy voice guide — AI tells to avoid

The canonical standard for how Pulsify writes. Governs every word that leaves the building or gets published: outreach DMs, Loom scripts, VSLs, emails, landing page copy, YouTube scripts, and the wiki itself. It sits on top of the positioning rules in [[content-engine/packaging-rules]] and the asset rules in [[raw-sources/prompts/outreach-asset-prompt]] — those decide *what* to say, this decides *how it reads*.

Sourced from the AI-writing-tropes list (tropes.fyi by ossama.is), filtered and adapted to Pulsify's copy. Not every trope in the original applies to short sales copy; what is kept here is what actually shows up in DMs, scripts, and pages.

## Why this matters for Pulsify

The entire brand is raw, authentic, human — a real operator who watched your content and diagnosed your business. The moment a prospect smells a templated AI message, that positioning collapses and the message reads as spray-and-pray. In a trust recession, an AI tell is the same trust-killer as an income-screenshot thumbnail: it signals low effort and a sale being run on you. Every pattern below is a tell. Removing them is not stylistic polish, it is the difference between "this person studied me" and "this is bot #4000 today."

## Scope — what this governs and what it does not

Governs the prose mechanics of all outbound and published copy, plus wiki prose.

Does **not** override two things:
- **YouTube title packaging.** The [[content-engine/packaging-rules]] and the titles-thumbnails skill mandate a punchy, parenthetical-kicker title format on purpose. Titles are a validated format, not prose. Leave them alone.
- **Internal reference scannability.** Bold-lead bullets and tight fragments are fine in internal wiki/SOP docs built for fast scanning. The bans below apply to *copy a human reads as writing* — DMs, scripts, VSLs, emails, pages, and any wiki *prose* paragraphs.

## The principle: one is fine, ten is slop

None of these devices are banned outright. A single deliberate "you don't need more leads, you need a system" can land in a VSL. The tell is *reflex and volume* — the model reaching for the same move ten times because its training rewards it. Use a device when you choose it for effect. Never let it be the default shape every sentence falls into. If you cannot say why a device is there, cut it.

## Word choice

- **Magic adverbs.** Cut "quietly," "deeply," "fundamentally," "remarkably," "arguably" used to make a flat sentence feel profound. Say the thing plainly.
- **AI vocabulary.** Drop "delve," "utilize" (use "use"), "leverage" as a verb, "robust," "streamline," "harness," "seamless," "certainly." These are dead giveaways.
- **Ornate nouns.** No "tapestry," "landscape," "paradigm," "synergy," "ecosystem," "framework" where a plain word works. "The trading-content space," not "the ever-evolving trading landscape."
- **The copula dodge.** Use "is" and "are." Stop replacing them with "serves as," "stands as," "represents," "marks." "Nick's offer is the proof," not "Nick's offer serves as the proof."

## Sentence structure

- **Negative parallelism (the #1 tell).** Kill "It's not X — it's Y," "not because X, but because Y," and "The question isn't X. The question is Y." This is the single most recognizable AI shape. At most one per piece, chosen on purpose.
- **The countdown.** No "Not a bug. Not a feature. A design flaw." Stop negating two things to reveal a third.
- **Rhetorical self-Q&A.** No "The result? Devastating." Do not ask a question nobody posed just to answer it.
- **Anaphora pile-ups.** Do not open three-plus sentences in a row the same way ("They assume... They assume... They assume...").
- **Tricolon reflex.** One rule-of-three is elegant. Three rule-of-threes back to back is pattern failure. Vary the count.
- **False ranges.** "From X to Y" only when there is a real spectrum with a middle. "From innovation to transformation" has nothing in between. Just name the two things.
- **Filler transitions.** Cut "it's worth noting," "it bears mentioning," "importantly," "notably," "interestingly." If it matters, just say it.
- **Trailing "-ing" analysis.** No "...highlighting its importance," "...reflecting broader trends," "...underscoring its role." These tack fake significance onto a plain fact.

## Paragraph and format

- **Fragment spray.** Stop chopping copy into one-thought-per-line fragments as standalone paragraphs for fake punch ("He did it. Openly. In public."). Real people write in connected sentences. A fragment is a tool, not a default rhythm.
- **Listicle in a trench coat.** Do not write a hidden list as "The first... The second... The third..." prose. Either write a real list or write real prose.
- **Bold-lead bullets** (in prose/copy). Do not start every bullet with a bolded keyword and colon. Almost nobody writes by hand this way. (Internal scannable docs are exempt — see scope.)
- **Em-dash addiction.** A human uses two or three em dashes in a piece, naturally. AI uses twenty. Default to commas, periods, or parentheses. This one is on me as much as anyone.
- **Unicode decoration.** Type straight quotes ("like this"), not curly ones. Use "->" not the arrow glyph. No decorative unicode a person would not type.

## Tone

- **False suspense.** Cut "here's the kicker," "here's the thing," "here's where it gets interesting," "here's what most people miss." Deliver the point without the drumroll.
- **Patronizing analogy.** Drop the reflexive "think of it as..." / "it's like a...". Explain the thing directly; reach for a metaphor only when it is genuinely clearer.
- **"Imagine a world where..."** Skip the futurism invitation. State what changes.
- **False vulnerability.** No performative "and yes, I'll be honest..." or "this isn't a rant, it's a diagnosis." Real candor is specific and slightly uncomfortable, not a polished aside.
- **"The truth is simple."** Do not assert that a point is obvious, clear, or simple instead of proving it. If you have to say it is clear, it is not.
- **Stakes inflation.** A DM about a funnel gap is not "the defining shift of the decade." Keep the stakes the real size.
- **Pedagogical voice.** Cut "let's break this down," "let's unpack," "let's dive in." You are talking to an operator, not a classroom.
- **Vague attributions.** No "experts say," "studies show," "industry reports suggest." Name the source or cut the claim. (Reinforces CLAUDE.md: never invent a stat, never validate an unverified person.)
- **Invented concept labels.** Do not coin "the supervision paradox" / "the acceleration trap" and use it as if it is a defined term. Naming a thing is not the same as making the argument.

## Pre-send checklist (scan every asset for the top tells)

Before any DM, script, email, or page goes out, scan for these eight — they cover most of the damage:

1. "It's not X, it's Y" negative parallelism — present? Cut to one or zero.
2. Em dashes — more than three? Convert most to commas or periods.
3. "Here's the thing / here's the kicker" — cut.
4. "Serves as / represents / stands as" — replace with "is."
5. Bold-lead bullets in copy a person reads as writing — unbold.
6. Curly quotes or arrow glyphs — replace with straight characters.
7. Rule-of-three stacked more than once — vary it.
8. "It's worth noting / importantly / notably" filler — delete.

## How Pulsify uses this

This is now the default voice for everything the AI drafts. When generating any asset through [[raw-sources/prompts/outreach-asset-prompt]] or any other route, apply these rules in the final pass before output. When linting the wiki, flag prose pages that violate them. When a sales asset deliberately breaks a rule for effect, that is allowed once and on purpose — the ban is on the reflex, not the considered choice.

Added 2026-09-29 ([[synthesis/dustin-varano-the-keys]]): the fastest fix for templated AI copy is better examples, not more instructions. Give the model 2-3 real pieces in the exact format being written, then read the draft out loud; if it still sounds templated, swap in better examples instead of editing line by line.
