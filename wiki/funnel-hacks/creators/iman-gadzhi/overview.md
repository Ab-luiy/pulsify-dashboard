# Iman Gadzhi

Iman Gadzhi runs Consulting.com, an education/coaching company whose flagship product is the Quantum Mastermind — a recurring $28,800, 60-seat, 3-day event in rotating luxury locations (Andalusia Sept 2026; prior: Dubai, Cape Town, London). He sells it with zero paid acquisition on the coaching funnel, using a 5.96M-subscriber YouTube channel and a fan-run clip economy as the entire top of funnel.

## Niche and positioning
Biz-op / make-money-online education, positioned at the premium end via environment, exclusivity, and transparency ("we open the books"). Repositioning over the last 12 months from young-rich-kid lifestyle flex to "future billionaire / mentor / operator."

## Offer
- Product: Quantum Mastermind (recurring high-ticket event) + Consulting.com programs
- Price: $28,800 (mastermind), application-gated
- Promise: experience + information + connections; access to Iman, his C-suite, and an 8-figure peer room

## Audience size
- YouTube: 5.96M subs, 474 videos
- Instagram: @imangadzhi 2.7M; @consulting 10.1K
- TikTok: @imangadzhi 539.4K (23.1M likes)
- Fan clips: @imangadzhiclips 332K, @imangclips 89.4K, plus others

## My read

The funnel's genius is subtractive, not additive. No VSL, no opt-in, no email sequence, no nurture, no paid coaching ads. He removed every standard funnel component and replaced all of them with two things: enormous organic content gravity and deliberate friction. The friction (no link on the 2.7M account → tiny brand account → upfront $28,800 → application call) is the qualification system. He can afford to delete the funnel machinery because the audience is pre-warmed at a scale almost no one else has.

The replicable insight for content (not for copying): for creators without 5.96M subs, the friction-as-filter model fails — they need the VSL and nurture layers he deleted. The video angle writes itself around that tension.

The strongest single fact (stated precisely): the current Quantum Mastermind funnel runs **no active paid ads** (Ad Library confirms only Hills Eyewear ads), and Consulting.com brands its whole model the **"Negative Acquisition Flywheel"** — their own term for getting paid to acquire customers, with a stated ~$2M ad spend against $70M+ revenue (≈35:1). The defensible content beat is the ratio + the no-ads-on-the-funnel fact + their "$20M from one YouTube video" claim — not a literal "never spends on ads."

The full offer ladder (Monetise → UpLevel → Quantum → Masterminds), the named C-suite (CMO Luis Berger built "$20M+ VSL funnels"), and the proprietary software stack (Synthesise, Flozy, GhostWriter OS) are captured in [[funnel-layer]]. Company trades internally as "Educate."
