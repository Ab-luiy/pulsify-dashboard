# Iman Gadzhi — content breakdown

Synthesised from the YouTube + IG + TikTok + clip-ecosystem capture. This is the attention engine that replaces paid acquisition.

## Pillars

Four content pillars cycle across the catalog:

1. **Wealth-building tactics** — "Best Online Business to Make $10k+/month," "3 AI Businesses Will Make You $373/Day," "Copy & Paste THIS Strategy." The how-to hook. Highest-volume pillar.
2. **Identity / transformation** — "Disappear And Come Back Unrecognizable," "Become Unrecognizably Successful," "Monk Mode," "Dark Motivation." Sells a future self, not a tactic.
3. **Lessons / principles** — "17 Money Lessons," "10 Years of Business Knowledge in 19 Minutes," "7 Books I Wish I Had Read." Authority-by-distillation.
4. **Lifestyle / status** (declining) — "$3M Watch Collection," "$30M Private Jet," "Hasbulla Porsche." Was a top pillar 2–3 years ago, now nearly retired from new uploads.

## Title formulas

- **The 17 signature**: 7 and 17 recur deliberately ("17 Money Lessons," "7 Principles," "17 Laws of Success," "7 Habits"). Odd, specific, non-round. Never "10" or "20."
- **Dollar-specific promise**: "$373/Day," "$13k/Month," "$10k+/month," "$2,700/Week." Always an oddly specific number — $373, not $400.
- **Copy & paste / laziest**: "Copy & Paste THIS Strategy," "Laziest Way To Make Money." Low-effort framing, repeated because it works (1.3M views on the "Laziest Way... AI" video, 92 VPH).
- **Identity command**: "Disappear And Come Back Unrecognizable," "Stop wasting your life," "Unf*ck Your Life." Imperative verb, second person.
- **Time-boxed authority**: "10 Years of Business Knowledge In 19 Minutes," "Give me 16 minutes and I'll brainwash you." Compresses a big claim into a small time cost.

## Authority signals

- The number: "+$100m by the age of 26," repeated in the channel bio and across the brand.
- Longevity: "started this channel in 2015: before I'd found any success." Owns the full arc — the receipts are public.
- Asset ownership: bio reads "Owner: @consulting (Est. 2016), Co-Owner: @whop ($3B+ GMV)." Authority by what he owns, not what he claims.
- The clip economy: 332K + 89K+ followers of fan-run clip accounts he doesn't pay for. The strongest authority signal is that other people distribute him for free.

## Platform-role split (the core architectural insight)

Every platform has exactly one job and does not try to do another:

| Platform | Role | Funnel mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube long-form | Authority + depth (warmest leads) | None on-platform; description → IG |
| YouTube Shorts | **Not used at all** | — |
| Instagram @imangadzhi | Brand gravity | No external link — routes to @consulting |
| Instagram @consulting | Pre-qualifier | The only link → landing page |
| TikTok @imangadzhi | Pure reach | No bio, no link |
| Fan clip accounts | Free distribution | None — awareness only |
| Paid Meta ads | Eyewear product only | Not used for coaching |

Nothing converts except the @consulting link. Everything else exists to build enough gravity that the motivated seek out that one link themselves. The friction is the filter.
