### Title pattern analysis

Four dominant title structures Iman uses, ranked by frequency in the top 30:

1. **Numbered listicle promise** (~16/30): "7 Principles For Teenagers To Become Millionaires," "17 Life Lessons," "8 Money Tips," "5 Habits," "7 Books." The number is almost always 3, 5, 7, or 17 — never round numbers. 17 is his signature.
2. **Lifestyle / status flex** (~5/30): "$30M Private Jet at 23," "INSANE $3,000,000 Watch Collection," "Limited-Edition Ultra Luxury Car (1 of 5)," "Hasbulla drive my Porsche GT3."
3. **Demographic-targeted promise** (~5/30): "Teenagers... Become Millionaires," "in your 20s or 30s," "Best Side Hustles to Make $550/day," "Best Online Business to Make $10k+/month."
4. **Transformation / identity** (~4/30): "Disappear And Come Back Unrecognizable," "How to take back CONTROL," "Become Unrecognizably Successful," "Monk Mode."

Recent shift visible in the last 29: lifestyle-flex titles have nearly disappeared. The current 2026 catalog is identity + business model + AI. The "Hasbulla / private jet / watch collection" era is gone from the front-page lineup. This is a deliberate repositioning — title-level pivot from "young rich kid" to "future billionaire / mentor."

Worth a deeper check — many big creators run a hidden Whop/Skool clip-payout program, and Iman co-owns Whop, which would make a clip-payout program trivial for him to run. Open question, not confirmed either way.
