# Funnel hacks

This is the Pulsify funnel-hacking operation. The goal: take any big-name coach in the biz-op space and reverse-engineer their entire attention-to-conversion machine, end to end, with screenshots and timing data, then turn that breakdown into top-of-funnel YouTube content.

**Purpose: content and entertainment, not implementation.** These breakdowns exist to fuel Pulsify's own YouTube videos in the funnel-hack genre (Alexei-style). Specific tactics may inform Pulsify's own work as inspiration, but the captures are not a blueprint to copy. Pulsify's positioning is its own — these are research and content material.

The model is Alexei-style funnel breakdowns, but more thorough, more visual, and replicable across creators. Every capture follows the same protocol so creators are directly comparable and a synthesis layer compounds across the whole space.

## Why this matters for Pulsify

Funnel hacks pull curiosity traffic (big names in the title) while demonstrating authority. They feed the top of Pulsify's funnel by attracting cold viewers who don't yet know they want what we sell, then deliver the Pulsify diagnosis at the end of every breakdown — pulling them down funnel into the worldview.

Beyond content, this is intelligence. We learn what's working in the space at the level of mechanics, not opinions. Every creator captured updates the synthesis page that informs Pulsify's own offer, funnel, and content choices.

## What every capture covers

Two layers, captured fully, every time:

**Attention layer** — how they garner attention from absolutely cold audiences:
- YouTube long-form
- YouTube shorts
- Instagram (feed, reels, stories, highlights)
- TikTok
- Clipping accounts (creator-run and fan-run)
- Clipping agencies (if findable)
- Republishing patterns across platforms
- Paid ads (if traceable)

**Conversion layer** — how they take attention to sale:
- Link in bio and all destinations
- Landing page (full screenshots, copy, CTAs)
- Opt-in flow (what they collect)
- Email nurture sequence (every email logged with timing)
- SMS sequence (if collected)
- VSL (presence, length, structure, transcript)
- Webinar (presence, format, pitch)
- Application form (questions asked)
- Call booking flow
- Pricing reveal mechanics
- Sales call structure (if inferable)
- DM closing (auto vs manual)
- Retargeting ad creative

## Output per creator

Every captured creator gets a folder under `creators/{creator-name}/` with:

- `overview.md` — who they are, niche, offer, audience size, my read on their positioning
- `attention-layer.md` — full breakdown of every attention channel
- `funnel-layer.md` — full breakdown of every conversion step
- `content-breakdown.md` — content pillars, title formulas, thumbnail patterns, hook patterns, production style
- `miro-board.md` — the A-to-Z visual map (link or embedded structure)
- `assets/` — screenshots organized by attention / funnel / content

## Cross-cutting synthesis

Once 3+ creators are captured, the `synthesis/` folder gets pages like:

- `biz-op-funnel-patterns.md` — what's common across the space
- `attention-stacks.md` — which platform mixes are working
- `vsl-anatomy.md` — VSL patterns across the space
- `pricing-reveal-tactics.md` — when and how big coaches reveal price
- `clipping-economy.md` — how the clipping/republishing layer actually operates

These synthesis pages are the real asset. Individual breakdowns make YouTube videos. The synthesis tells Pulsify what to build.
