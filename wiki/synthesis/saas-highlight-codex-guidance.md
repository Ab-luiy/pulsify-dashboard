## Pattern library (observed)
1. Photo persists. Consecutive slides reuse one photo with a different text state (brez 1-2, 3-5, 6-7; niksetting seq 3 slides 3-6). Our slides 4+5 and 12+13 are natural pairs. Do not force a new photo for a continuation; the founder's rule (every slide a different photo) applies to slides, not to the added text states, so keep distinct photos per slide unless he says otherwise.
2. Text zone. Text sits in the upper 60 percent, left or centred, over a uniform dark gradient; the subject lives in the lower part. Never put key text under the story UI bars.
3. Type. Instagram-style system sans, equals Helvetica for us. Regular for body, Bold for the headline, one emphasised phrase per slide (bold, underlined, or in a highlight box). Sentence case, no all caps except tiny labels.
4. Highlight pills. Black rounded pill with white text for the key sentence (brez 1, niksetting seq 2 slide 5). Accent-colour box for annotation text (niksetting seq 3). One pill style per slide.
5. Hand-drawn marks. Underline under a phrase, a loose ring around a number, a curved arrow from text to the subject, X marks for what you lack. Strokes are uneven and fast, not geometric.
6. Proof cards. Rounded card holding a screenshot or one big number, sitting between the photo and the text; the founder may overlap it (Siggyfoss 5, Shelby 3).
7. UI on screens. UI can be composited onto a screen in the photo (niksetting seq 3), with a numbered label and a small circled digit. Use real portal UI only.
8. Progress dots at the top for a numbered run (3 dots, one lit per step). Use on the three feature slides.
9. Progressive reveal. Earlier text dims when a new line lands (Shelby 4). Use on slide 4 (list items arrive one by one if animated).
10. Link sticker pill (white, rounded, icon plus bold caps). Our highlight has no CTA, so do not add one.

Not copied: cars, watches, bank balances, luxury hotels, price tags, "reply with" triggers, scarcity lines. The craft transfers; the flex does not.
