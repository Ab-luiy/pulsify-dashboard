# Personalized cold outreach VSL architecture

A personalized cold outreach VSL is not a Loom audit. It is a process-selling diagnostic. The job is to show the prospect the specific gap in their business, map the broken path, show the corrected path, and earn the next diagnostic conversation.

## Why this matters for Pulsify

Most cold VSLs are weak because they point at surface fixes. Pulsify's advantage is diagnosis: content -> offer -> funnel. If the VSL only says "your landing page is weak," it sounds like every other operator. If it shows how the weak landing page is one symptom of a broken content-to-call path, it becomes harder to ignore.

## Core structure

### 1. Specific opening

Open with the prospect's actual business, not a compliment.

Bad:

- "Love what you're doing."
- "I checked out your page."

Better:

- "Your content is creating attention, but the path from attention to qualified calls is leaking."
- "You are making the same founder-dependent mistake most coaches make after their first proof."
- "The issue is not that you need more posts. The issue is that your offer and funnel are not catching the demand your content creates."

### 2. One-wound diagnosis

Pick one wound from [[people/marcus-avatar]]:

- content stall
- lead-flow stall
- program stall
- backend stall
- sales-infrastructure stall
- launch plateau

Do not diagnose all six. One VSL, one wound.

### 3. Current broken path

Show the current path visually or verbally:

Content -> profile -> CTA -> form/calendar -> nurture -> call -> close

Then mark where it breaks.

Examples:

- Content gets attention but no buyer intent.
- Profile does not pre-qualify.
- CTA is too casual.
- Application path lets unqualified leads through.
- No pre-call nurture exists.
- Sales call has to educate from zero.

### 4. Consequence if unchanged

Make the cost clear without drama.

- More content will create more noise.
- More leads will create more unqualified calls.
- More sales calls will expose the weak offer.
- More automation will speed up the wrong process.

### 5. Corrected path

Show the Pulse System version:

Content -> unique mechanism -> profile/VSL -> qualification -> pre-call nurture -> sales asset -> follow-up

This is where the personalized VSL becomes more than an audit. It shows the machine underneath the fix.

### 6. One asset preview

Attach or show one built asset:

- rewritten VSL hook
- rebuilt profile funnel
- Typeform questions
- 7-day story nurture
- 5-video pillar map
- offer mechanism map
- pre-call email sequence

The asset is the proof. Do not ask permission to send it. Send it.

### 7. Boundary and fit

Use the Jordan pattern. Say what Pulsify can and cannot fix.

Example:

Pulsify can build the content, offer, and funnel infrastructure. It cannot create authority if the prospect has no real skill, no proof, and no willingness to execute.

This increases trust because it breaks the fake-guarantee pattern Marcus is used to.

### 8. CTA

The CTA is not "book a free call."

Use:

- "I can map the full version."
- "Worth walking through the rebuild?"
- "If this diagnosis is accurate, the next step is a 30-minute map."
- "Reply and I will send the full Pulse map for your offer."

## Five-minute version

| Minute | Job |
|---|---|
| 0:00-0:30 | Name the prospect, stage, and specific wound. |
| 0:30-1:30 | Show the current broken path. |
| 1:30-2:30 | Explain why the usual fix will not solve it. |
| 2:30-3:45 | Show the corrected Pulse System path. |
| 3:45-4:30 | Walk through the asset built for them. |
| 4:30-5:00 | State boundary, fit, and CTA. |

## What to avoid

- Do not make a generic audit.
- Do not diagnose every wound at once.
- Do not flatter.
- Do not over-explain the whole offer.
- Do not use fake scarcity.
- Do not promise revenue Pulsify cannot prove.
- Do not make AI the hero.
