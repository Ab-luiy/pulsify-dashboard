# Dustin Varano's "The Keys": what Pulsify adds from it

Dustin Varano's lead magnet is 13 short SOPs covering YouTube, Instagram, offer, sales-team and ops. The founder opted in and asked for it to be read in full and folded into the vault as extra knowledge: it enriches what we already do and never overrides it. This page says what each key adds, where it now lives in the vault, and where it disagrees with Pulsify doctrine. Full paraphrased notes: `raw-sources/inspiration/dustin-varano-the-keys-notes.md`.

## Why this matters for Pulsify

Dustin sells to the same buyers we do (operators and offer owners in bizop niches) and runs YouTube as the lead engine, so his playbook is a close comparison. It is also a lead magnet: several pages end in a plug for his software (InfoOS), his in-house Miro builders or his 1:1, and he says the flywheel outline he shares is about 10x shorter than his real one. Read it as one operator's field notes, n=1, with every revenue figure his own unverified claim.

## Where it disagrees with Pulsify

> ⚠️ Contradiction: the flywheel page tells creators to "flex lifestyle briefly" in the hook and to post lifestyle vlogs as TOF, about one in every 3-5 videos. Pulsify's positioning is the opposite (no cars, watches or lifestyle; see CLAUDE.md core philosophy and [[concepts/trust-recession]]). Resolution: we keep his structure and drop the lifestyle parts. His own Miro page supports us: he says results-based proof pulls a higher quality viewer and lifestyle proof drags in a less serious crowd.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: his funnel layers don't match ours. His TOF is lifestyle vlogs, his MOF is the flywheel, and his BOF is in-depth breakdowns for people who already know the model, which is what [[concepts/content-funnel-tof-mof-bof]] calls MOF. Resolution: we keep our layer definitions; his model is logged on that page as an outside view, not adopted.

Unverified claims we do not repeat as fact: YouTube ranks on hidden "session time"; linking to Instagram gets a video throttled; uploading from the US decides which countries see it; longer videos always perform better; the 90%+ dispute win rate. All of these come from his own channel and offers, with no data shown.

## How Pulsify uses this

Most of it is extra depth on things we already do: proof placement, story sequences, onboarding, contracts, SOPs from calls. Three pieces are genuinely new to us and worth acting on:
1. Per-video cash tracking. Already specced and ticketed as #36; his page confirmed two details now in the spec (pinned-comment links tracked separately, and why we tie bookings by email instead of carrying tags through the calendar).
2. The flywheel video as a candidate "anchor video" for the Growth work, whose first job is creating a full YouTube video. Untested for our clients.
3. Same-day testimonial capture as a standing habit, which directly feeds the proof system.
