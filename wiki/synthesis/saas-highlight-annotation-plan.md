# Highlight annotation plan: arrows, shapes and figures

Layer 4 and 5 of the Siggyfoss stack (type, then one annotation) for the 14-slide highlight. The Siggyfoss captures show: curved arrows, underlines under the key phrase, circles and a circular magnifier with a ring and a translucent connector, diamond list markers, a tap icon, rounded proof cards, and a few small icons. They are one device per slide, never several. This plan keeps that discipline.

## Toolkit (build once as Canva-style elements, reuse)
- Accent: the homepage blue #6871f4 (soft tint #eef0ff, strong #5863ef) from output/home-draft/index.html. One accent on every slide. White type, Helvetica Neue. No second colour.
- Arrow: hand-drawn curved arrow, 6px stroke, rounded caps, accent. Used to point at one thing.
- Underline: single hand-drawn stroke under the emphasised phrase, accent.
- Highlight block: solid accent rectangle behind one word (matches the homepage emphasis style), white bold italic on top.
- Circle: loose hand-drawn ring around one detail, accent.
- Magnifier: circular crop of a UI detail, 4px accent ring, soft translucent accent triangle connecting it to the source spot.
- Diamond marker: small solid diamond used as list bullet.
- Card: white or dark rounded rectangle (radius about 28px), soft shadow, holds a screenshot or diagram.
- Tap icon: small hand-tap graphic bottom centre on slides that continue.
- Coin mark: from brand/logo, only slides 9 and 14.

## Per-slide overlay (one device each)
1. Hook: highlight block behind "a whole year". Nothing else.
2. Two businesses: underline under "two businesses". Optionally a small "1" and "2" in circles beside the two screens if the photo has two clear objects.
3. Chain: the six words drawn as a left-to-right chain on a dark card with accent arrows between them, ending on "cash in the bank". This is the one diagram slide.
4. Breaks: diamond markers on the four lines. Circle one word per line only if space allows.
5. DMs: arrow from the quoted line "no idea which post" to the blurred inbox; dark card behind the subject if a body shot exists.
6. Try harder: no arrow. Only a highlight block on "2am". The darkest, quietest slide.
7. Plateau: underline "plateau". Quiet.
8. New faces: highlight block on the quote line; small arrow from "new face" to the founder in frame.
9. Video: coin and the motion graphic. Burned subtitles in white with the accent on the key words.
10. Voice: underline "your voice". Tap icon.
11. Sales: three small diamond markers on closing, calls, numbers. No scoreboard card until the Sales Co-pilot ships.
12. Tracked: card with an enlarged UI detail, circular magnifier with the accent connector, arrow to the circle. Underline the quote.
13. Not overnight: circle one node in the graph or one notebook; underline "every framework was tested first".
14. Close: no device except the small coin mark and the tap icon. Warm, plain.

## Motion hints for the stills that animate
Arrows and underlines draw on (about 0.4s), magnifier scales in from the source spot, diamond markers appear one per line. Nothing loops. Keep it to instagram story limits and let the photo carry the mood.

## Build order for whoever makes it
1. Build the toolkit once (arrow, underline, block, circle, magnifier, card, diamond, tap) as editable elements.
2. Build slides 12, 3 and 5 first, since they are the hardest (card, diagram, subject over card). If those read well at phone size, the rest are photo plus type.
3. Review the three at phone size before the other eleven.
