# MVP phase 3 and 4: cleanup and a live click-through

Follows [[wiki/synthesis/mvp-plan-2026-10-02]]. Phases 1 and 2 are live (Pages 04741545, then a34ebb48 for the Copywriter chat). This page records what phase 3 removed, what the live click-through found, and the product calls that wait on the founder.

## Live click-through (read-only operator preview of the test client, 2026-10-03)

Opened the live portal as the test client through the operator preview and read every main screen. No console errors anywhere.

- Home: checklist shows the new ten steps plus the client-side tail, "3 of 11 completed" after voice, audience and YouTube auto-completed, next steps are proof, plan first video, send for review.
- Plans: eight phases from the offer table with real dates. To-dos: four client-owned items. Team: Pulsify team and Pulsify AI assistant.
- Copywriter: opens as a chat in "runs in your own AI" mode; composer disabled in preview as intended.
- YouTube new video: first of ten questions with the client's onboarding answer offered as a hint.
- Leads, Performance, All-time tracker, Revenue, Integrations, Media: load with honest empty states.
- Fixed on the branch: the Content empty state said the team adds items; it now says items arrive when the client sends a video for review.

Not covered: writes (preview is read-only), so sending a real brief, saving, and review submission are only tested with mocks. A real signed-in write test needs the client's own session.

## Phase 3: removed (branch claude/mvp-phase3-cleanup, not deployed)

Unreachable code, verified: `/admin` redirects operators to /app and signed-out visitors to sign-in; `/portal` redirects clients to /app/portal. Nothing served the old files.

- Legacy operator console: admin.html, admin/ (all), dashboard builder tool, six shared scripts and styles only it used, five more orphaned shared files.
- Legacy client portal: portal/index.html and its fifteen scripts and styles, thumbnail library copy (the app has its own).
- Old pages already redirected by middleware: portal.html, client.html, settings.html, campaigns.html, integrations.html, js/provision-prompt.js.
- Tests that only tested that dead code: phase2-shell, and the legacy-portal part of sequence-variants. Backend suite 205 of 205.
- Kept on purpose: portal/login, portal/references (images in use), shared theme and shell styles (404 page, onboarding, docs), public funnel scripts, the access gate rules in the middleware.
- Functions bundle compiles. About 33,000 lines removed.

Left alone: the ad-hoc live-site browser scripts in tests/*.cjs (not part of the test run; several drive the deleted pages and can be deleted next).

## Duplicates that need a founder call

1. YouTube has two ways to make a video. "New video" (ideation, title, build, review) and the older "Create long form" wizard (concept, title, thumbnail, outline). Recommendation: make New video the front door and fold the thumbnail step into it, keep the old wizard only for thumbnails.
2. "Cash collected" appears on Home, All-time tracker and Revenue overview. Recommendation: Revenue is the home of money, Home keeps one number, Tracker drops the cash panels.
3. Three libraries of what was made: Content, Instagram "My projects", YouTube "My video projects". Recommendation: Content is the one place, the other two become filters of it.
4. Reels and Carousels are still on the Instagram page though both are out of scope for now. Recommendation: hide until reopened.
5. Tracker still says "Connect a payment rail" although payments are logged by hand.
