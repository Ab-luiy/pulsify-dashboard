# Complete carousel reference examination

All 76 stored carousel images were opened individually and visually examined. This is the source-backed examination of their elements, composition, figures, copy, continuity, colors, branding and subject layering. The findings feed one shared production system for carousels and stories: [[content-engine/reference-replication-profile]] and [[playbooks/reference-replication-sop]]. The source images remain intact. The [source atlas](../../output/carousel-reference-audit-2026-10-08/atlas.html) displays every original alongside its individual analysis; the [manifest](../../output/carousel-reference-audit-2026-10-08/manifest.json) records dimensions, hashes and exact paths.

## Why this matters for Pulsify

Content creates attention and trust. The offer supplies the mechanism and fit. The funnel supplies a clear next action. These references join all three through design: an operator's real scene gives context, the artifact makes work inspectable, and copy explains why the reader should care. A reusable renderer must preserve those relationships. A generic headline over a blurred photo fails before the copy is read.

The founder explicitly requested faithful replication of the visual craft, including photos, subject-over-text depth, real app icons, profile demonstrations, charts and funnels, with the same standard applied to stories. That is the current direction. It does not turn another creator's clients, autobiography, logo or revenue into Pulsify's own evidence.

## Core mechanics

### 1. Nine visual families, not one flattened template

**Viktor feature family, G1.** Large thin sans feature verbs, white body, compact dark message crops, controlled hand underlines and violet connectors. The headline shifts from peach through pink/lilac to saturated purple across the set. The photo remains the setting. Feature evidence occupies available space around an operator; the person can overlap cards. G1-1 has a special outlined/translucent name treatment, while the interior headlines stay lighter and cleaner.

**Result-tile family, G2-2–14.** White dot-matrix multiplier, italic serif identity and glass endpoint/duration pills. Thirteen different real scenes stay recognizable. Dot-matrix type passes behind hair, caps or heads. The component hierarchy repeats; the photo-responsive positions vary. G2-1 is a keyword invitation, not another result tile.

**Proof-wall close, G3-1.** A tiled content archive behind a speaker and a right-hand count/invitation. NG-1 and NP-2 also use archive density, with different type and staging. The transferable component is a real evidence wall, not a mandatory huge number or the same brand identity.

**Personal chronicle, G3-2–10.** White sans, selective bold, narrow blue underlines, small handle, top/bottom paragraph groups and space for the photograph. It tells an attributed personal history through mistakes, changes and diagnosis. It needs neither a ghost number nor a card on every slide.

**Red case/system family, K1.** White bold headings with red rectangular backing, glowing bracket numbers, compact paragraphs, real proof crops, white connectors, colorful conceptual funnels and cyan/blue delivery CTA. Paired setup/reveal frames reuse the photo and dim prior information. Proof sometimes passes behind the subject and sometimes deliberately covers it.

**Cyan lesson family, K2.** White bold/regular copy, cyan emphasis and glowing bracket numbers, gray handle, scene-led lesson frames. Its bridge uses analytics crops behind the subject; most lessons need only type and a relevant photograph. The close repeats the same invitation treatment.

**Warm method family, NG.** Studio/work scenes, large white sans title, pale italic serif ordinal, a thin vertical body rule, selective authentic-looking wall-plane artifacts and curved editorial arrows. People, chairs and tables retain their foreground depth. NG-5 is a plain work scene without an added receipt panel.

**White taxonomy family, NT-2–8.** Solid white canvas, black sans titles/definitions, huge pale italic ordinals, purple/gray/mint chips with stable axis meanings, varied phone collages. Layouts include two phones, six phones, a central column and staggered route. The cover and close switch to photography; chip grammar does not apply to unrelated carousels.

**Concept-word family, NP.** Enormous shadowed white one-word titles, white or black body selected against the scene, varied photo-sensitive alignment, occasional genuine work archives. Interiors have no compulsory ordinal. The final thesis closes without an explicit keyword CTA. Several source phrases are awkwardly broken around figures; preserve depth but improve reading flow.

### 3. Shapes, forms, boxes and figures

Rounded rectangles appear as glass metrics, message crops, translucent flow nodes, platform pills and device screens. They differ in material and role. G1's black artifact crops are opaque enough for white source text; G2's thin outlined pills inherit the photograph. K1's nodes need readable labels and clear connectors. Replacing all of them with identical charcoal cards discards the reference.

K1-4's three-stage funnels combine ellipse-like mouths and tapering bands to imply a volume path. Their widths are conceptual, not known conversion proportions. Reproduce their geometry in SVG with named stages, stable gradient direction and subtle band depth. Where actual stage totals are supplied, declare whether width encodes count, area or only categorical order. Never imply a measured funnel merely because its silhouette narrows.

NG-8's analytics wall contains varied forms: a territory map, revenue bridge, conversion funnel, cohort lines, heatmaps, donut, network and bars. Most are too small to inspect as evidence on a phone. Its visible function is scale/analytic atmosphere. The earlier breakdown's identification of the entire mosaic as a particular product is not established by this image. Our client output should feature a real relevant screen and magnify the important measure.

There is no single rule that all figures must be screenshots. Conceptual graphs and genuine evidence coexist. Every figure must disclose which it is. A conceptual flow needs named stages and a sensible route; a measured chart needs a dataset, metric, unit, period and method. A source crop can be retained faithfully without pretending its hidden data was recovered.

### 4. Lines, arrows and comparison

G1 has violet curved/elbow routes between work outputs. K1 has white branched and rounded routes. NG mixes thin body rules with curved editorial arrows. NT-8 uses a thick pale route behind devices, terminating in a dot. G3 uses straight clean blue underlines. NP uses shadows rather than an annotation on every phrase. These line languages belong to their families.

An arrow should identify a person, point to a decisive artifact or connect two actual states. Store its endpoints against components so resizing preserves its meaning. A comparison should use the same entity, metric, currency, timeframe and scale. Where identity or periods differ, state the difference rather than making an apparent before/after.

G2 uses two endpoint amounts and duration; G1-4 uses completed versus pending work; NG-6 contrasts successive communication states; K2-1 contrasts views with commercial usefulness. The earlier story audit adds count/price blocks, dated progression, same-profile comparisons and a receipt magnifier. Choose the axis that answers the buyer's question. Red/green signaling alone does not prove one option is better.

### 5. Composition and the person behind/in front of the artwork

The essential recipe is a sandwich: intact base photo, rear artwork, registered restoration of the person from that same photo, then critical foreground type/annotations. The restoration is an occlusion mask, not a newly generated sticker-person. Clothes, fingers, cap edges, hair, chairs and table edges can each need inclusion depending on the intended overlap.

Keep photo, mask, crop, rotation and scale in one transform. A separate resize or translation creates a duplicate face, halo or seam. Restore the original pixels through the alpha. Inspect hair on both light and dark test backdrops; inspect each place a card or letter crosses the silhouette. Do not infer source software from a flattened image.

G1-3 places outputs behind a standing person; G2 places giant type behind heads; K2-2 places evidence behind a selfie; NG restores entire foreground people/furniture over rear-wall artifacts; NT-10 puts a donut and arrow behind two men. K1-8 puts the receipts in front instead. Depth is a composition choice, not an obligation.

Required words, result labels, units, periods, node names and CTA stay legible. A subject may hide a redundant card edge or part of a recognizable display word, provided the complete meaning remains clear. K1-6's hidden middle-route labels and NP-4/8's sentence fragments are source defects to improve. Do not preserve a defect merely to prove imitation.

### 6. Photography, color matching and branding

The setting is visible in most references: cafes, work desks, rooms, landscape, studio, stage or travel scene. Reduced exposure is sometimes used, but a flat image does not reveal how it was achieved. The founder has now explicitly rejected obliterating the environment with blur. Do not use blurred/mirrored extensions, random replacement skies, edge smears or zooming out to manufacture text space. Select a useful real crop, relocate copy, choose another registered photo or use a narrow contrast treatment where approved.

The shared profile separates **source-family palettes** from **Pulsify brand overrides**. Reference white type unites mixed warm, green and cool scenes. G1's progression is controlled, not random per-frame gradients. G2's glass tint follows environmental color. K1 red creates emphasis even for positive results, so the old universal red-means-pain claim is too narrow. K2 cyan is an identity cue. NT's three chip colors encode information. NP's black/white body adapts to the actual luminance behind it.

For the active SaaS highlight, maintain the approved Helvetica, graphite/silver, red hand underline and restrained good-number emerald decisions. No saturated blue is an inherited current-highlight override. A separate reference-study mode may preserve source cyan/blue/purple as supplied; it is not a silent SaaS UI reskin. Exact source hex colors and font names are not recoverable with certainty from compressed flattened captures.

Branding is a repeatable grammar: headline weight, annotation hand, material, placement rhythm, artifact choice and a recognizable subject or mark. Small handles recur in G3/K2; product naming drives G1; repeated typography drives G2; the studio scene and method rhythm drive NG. Pulsify's mark should have a purposeful reveal or ownership role, including behind-subject depth where useful. Do not paste a logo on every slide or copy another creator's identity as our own.

### 7. Profile demonstrations

NG-3 teaches a whole profile through a large projected screenshot: avatar, bio, highlight rail and pinned posts receive distinct labels. NT-2 nests a bio critique inside a value-story phone. K1-1 uses a deliberately blurred profile beside result evidence; it shows context without exposing profile detail. These are three different jobs, not one generic IG mockup.

Store the real screenshot and date first. Define regions for handle/avatar, followers/post counts, bio, link, highlight circles and pinned grid. For a before/after, use the same account and comparable dates. Highlight the component being taught; leave supporting parts visible. A screenshot of followers supports a follower count, not automatically buyer qualification, income or the claim that a bio change caused growth. Use genuine app icon assets as identification. Icon presence is not an API connection.

### 8. Copy, story tension and anticipation

**Feature demonstration, G1:** recurring task → concrete output → practical relief. Each next feature fulfills another part of the cover promise. Short verbs provide immediate orientation. The photo and cards should make the stated output inspectable.

**Case archive, G2:** named identity → endpoints → duration. Template repetition makes comparison quick. It is a proof collection rather than a fully established narrative; the stored CTA-first ordering should not be mistaken for verified storytelling order.

**Personal chronicle, G3:** starting constraint → expensive learning → change → operating model → reader's plateau → diagnostic intervention. Its useful persuasion comes from a known cost and a fitting mechanism. A creator's personal history cannot be swapped into ours by changing the name.

**Case mechanism, K1:** outcome → diagnostic consultation → ecosystem setup → visual payoff → hybrid-route setup → visual payoff → launch plan → evidence/next asset. The two repeated-photo reveal pairs reward continuation by adding the promised mechanism. This is stronger than making the viewer swipe to see the same promise reworded.

**Lesson set, K2/NP:** result or retrospective premise → finite lessons → one useful belief/topic per frame → bounded next asset or thesis. Concrete objections can become content topics. A single strange concept word can invite explanation, but the body must then explain it.

**Method and taxonomy, NG/NT:** finite cover contract → visibly indexed stages/categories → actual work/examples → fit-specific next action. Ordinals and consistent labels help orientation. They are plausible completion cues, not measured retention guarantees.

For every frame write: viewer's current question; one answer; evidence or honest example; what changed; next question; and the next frame that resolves it. A loop can span two frames if the setup already gives useful context. Close all material questions before the final invitation. A CTA offers a named asset or diagnostic step, with a real destination and delivery.

### 9. Copy that earns strong buying intent

The founder's phrase about selling snow expresses the demand for powerful persuasion. Pulsify diagnoses before treating: relevance and demonstrated value make a suitable buyer act. Trying to sell an irrelevant intervention weakens fit and trust.

Start with a buyer's concrete moment, not a vague growth aspiration. Explain the consequence, show the cause we can actually treat, demonstrate the intervention, answer the surviving objection, then name the next step. Use the client's language where it is documented. Keep confidence proportional to proof.

| Generic draft | More useful study pattern | What must be true |
|---|---|---|
| Our system saves time | Show the work that happens between a call ending and the follow-up being ready. | The listed outputs exist and can be demonstrated |
| Your profile does not convert | Show where a visitor can identify the offer, see relevant evidence and choose a next step. | Real profile and destination, no invented conversion rate |
| Our content makes sales | Trace one documented viewer-to-conversation path, including what can and cannot be attributed. | Actual events, identity/consent context, bounded attribution |
| You need better systems | Name the repeated handoff where a task stalls, then show the proposed handoff. | A diagnosed bottleneck and a fitting intervention |
| DM for more | State the agreed action and the actual walkthrough or diagnosis it obtains. | Asset exists, fulfillment defined, CTA works |

These are composition/copy patterns, not approved current product claims. Do not turn a sample into a receipt, a count into causal proof, or an attributed opinion into a universal law. Follow [[content-engine/copy-voice-guide]]: direct, varied sentence construction, no filler, no false story, no forced formulaic contrasts.

### 10. Source weaknesses and numerical findings

- G2-5 and G2-7 compute multipliers from zero: undefined. Use an absolute change or endpoint for our own proof.
- G2-14's six-fold headline conflicts with $20k → $200k/month, which implies ten-fold.
- G2-4/8/10/11/13 use rounded ratios. Keep a rounding label or the exact calculation when we reproduce the comparison.
- K2-2's four visible view totals sum to 34,864,274. They do not alone reconcile the 38M cover; they could be a partial collection.
- K1's event screenshots and date windows do not establish the qualified weekly-call headline by themselves. K1-7's phased plan overlaps day 11.
- NT-10 does not supply exact donut segment values. A faithful capture is possible; independent data reconstruction is not established.
- NG-8's dashboard mosaic does not establish a product identity or an implemented tracking capability. It is not permission to invent a working interface.
- NT-9 is a fragment. G2/G3 order is uncertain. Several tiny report/board details and occluded map labels remain unreadable.
- Some NP copy is fractured around bodies or crosses dark objects with black type. Source masking craft and source readability are separate judgments.
- No supplied engagement number establishes saves, completion, buyer quality or attributable profit. Design hypotheses need our own measurement.

## How Pulsify uses this

Use the JSON/Markdown profile with actual reference images and actual registered client assets. The profile is a portable instruction contract; the manifest supplies exact originals; recipe anchors show which frame contributed each move. Stories and carousels use the same component/layer system, with format-specific recomposition. The copy framework still decides the communication job before artwork.

Improve through a recorded loop: save the exact copy/assets/layout/version, identify one variable and its expected effect, publish only through the authorized workflow, record available per-frame and downstream measures, retain audience/time context, and compare similar periods. For stories, distinguish reach ratios from user-level survival. For carousels, do not invent per-slide retention if the account exposes only post-level metrics. Track qualified conversations, booked/attended calls and collected revenue where attribution exists. Save buyer explanations alongside tracking evidence. Revenue, profit and collected cash are distinct outcomes; there is no promise of unlimited lucrative growth.

The inspected carousel prompt at `output/standard-preview/functions/_shared/carousel-prompt.js` currently mandates a ghost number, limits layouts to cover/item/concept/close, normalizes chips by taxonomy and emits descriptive artifact slots. Its layout sanitizer contains no photo transforms, alpha masks, layer geometry, graph nodes/edges or source-proof links. That contract cannot express these compositions faithfully by itself. [[playbooks/reference-replication-sop]] specifies the versioned integration path. This study does not claim the renderer has been wired into or deployed in the live app.
