# Pulsify 90-day plan — May to August 2026

The execution plan that takes Pulsify from $0 MRR to first paid v1 client, with the funnel and content engine running, by 2026-08-08. Written backwards from the cash event.

## Why this matters for Pulsify

Founder financial pressure is 10/10. Funnel is not live. Outreach is at zero. University constrains time. Without a sequenced plan, the founder optimizes whichever asset feels closest each day and nothing finishes. This plan picks the order, names the constraints, and makes the trade-offs visible.

## The plan in one sentence

Ship the IG profile renovation and a recorded VSL inside 14 days, run 100 outbound DMs/Looms in the next 30 days against the existing IG-inbox hit list, hold 5 sales calls, close 1 paid v1 client at $5,000+ base, and have one paying engagement live by Day 45 with a second by Day 90.

## The 90-day target

| Metric | Target by Day 90 |
|---|---|
| Outreach sent | 300 |
| Calls booked | 15 |
| Calls held | 10 |
| Clients closed | 2–3 |
| Cash collected | $15,000–$25,000 (base only; performance fee accrues) |
| YouTube subs | 100 |
| Long-form videos shipped | 12 |
| IG profile state | renovated, profile-funnel live |
| VSL state | recorded, hosted, live |
| Typeform + Cal.com | wired and tested |

These targets are conservative. The headline guarantee ($100k for the client in 90 days) implies founder confidence that one closed client produces meaningful performance fee inside 90 days, but cash projections here exclude that for safety.

## The phases

### Phase 1 — Days 1–14: ship the funnel and the first content

The bottleneck this phase removes: nobody can close from a funnel that doesn't exist.

**Deliverables:**
- IG bio rewritten, highlights rebuilt (start here / results / mechanism / demo / apply per [[playbooks/pulsify-funnel-v1]]).
- IG bot-followers culled.
- One pinned long-form YouTube video shipped this fortnight, founder-face + Miro demo.
- VSL recorded using [[content-engine/pulsify-vsl-v3]] as the script.
- VSL hosted (Wistia or equivalent).
- Typeform built with the 7-question structure.
- Cal.com wired to Typeform; pre-call email sequence drafted.
- Hit list exported from founder's IG inbox into a single `outreach/hit-list.md` (pending creation).

**What the agent does:** drafts every script, every email, every IG caption. Researches every operator on the hit list and produces a one-line "lead-with" for each. Builds the Typeform field structure. Drafts the pre-call email.

**What the founder does:** records on camera, edits or directs editing, makes voice/positioning calls, hits send.

**Capacity check:** ~20 hours of founder time spread over 14 days. Compatible with university work as long as no exam falls in this window.

### Phase 2 — Days 15–45: outbound at volume, first close

The bottleneck this phase removes: the funnel exists but nobody is in it.

**Deliverables:**
- 100 outreach touches sent. Mix of: IG DMs (60), Loom-first DMs (30), email or X (10).
- Reply rate target: 15%+ (15 replies).
- Booked-call target: 5.
- Held-call target: 3.
- Closed-client target: 1 at $5,000 base + 30% performance.

**Outreach approach:** asset-first DM per [[playbooks/pulse-system-outreach]] and the Grant Garcia method per [[playbooks/ai-outreach-grant-method]]. Open with something built specifically for the prospect — a video angle, a hook for their next video, a short Loom on a leak in their funnel. The agent does the build; the founder approves and sends.

**Content cadence during this phase:** 1 long-form/week + 2–3 shorts/week, but capped at what is sustainable. Outreach is the priority lead-gen mechanism; content compounds in the background.

**On the first closed client:** the engagement begins immediately. Week 1 of the [[playbooks/pulsify-delivery-sop]] runs in parallel with continued outreach. This is the stress test for delivery + outreach simultaneously.

### Phase 3 — Days 46–90: deliver, prove, double down

The bottleneck this phase removes: one client doesn't make Pulsify a business.

**Deliverables:**
- Client #1 weeks 2–6 of delivery executed cleanly. Brand Brain, profile renovation, content engine, funnel install — all live by Day 75.
- 200 additional outreach touches (cumulative 300).
- 5 additional calls held.
- Client #2 closed (target Day 60–75) at $5,000+ base.
- Client #1 starts producing measurable attributable revenue. First performance-fee event ideally lands inside Day 90.
- Two case-study-grade content pieces shipped on Pulsify's own channel: one breakdown of the Pulse System, one walkthrough demo of the agent.

**Capacity tension:** 2 clients in active delivery + outreach + own-content + university. This is the saturation test. If founder hits a wall in this phase, the agent absorbs more of the labor (especially outreach drafting and content research).

## What blocks this plan

Listed in priority of likelihood, with mitigation.

1. **Exam weeks land mid-phase.** Mitigation: [[playbooks/exam-mode-autonomous-ops]]. Outreach pauses, content pauses, agent maintains client delivery via async drafts.
2. **VSL recording delays.** Mitigation: ship a placeholder VSL by Day 7 (founder-face screen-share, 8 minutes, raw) and replace with polished version by Day 21. Outreach can begin on the placeholder.
3. **First close takes longer than 30 days.** Mitigation: don't drop outreach volume; review pricing-tree behavior on calls; check qualification (likely too high a bar early — accept clients at $15–20k/mo if the will to execute is visible).
4. **First client churns or fails to hit cadence.** Mitigation: recorded risk in [[playbooks/pulsify-offer-v1]]. Founder runs the Week 1 integration discipline tightly to surface this in Week 2 rather than Week 8.
5. **Cash crunch forces accepting a wrong-fit client.** Mitigation: discipline on disqualifiers. A wrong-fit close is a 6-month free-work liability per the unbounded guarantee.

## What this plan deliberately does not include

- **Paid ads.** Zero budget. Phase 4 (Day 91+) adds them only after organic produces consistent inbound.
- **Hiring.** Zero hires until 4+ clients are paying base + performance.
- **A second niche.** Trading-coach focus is held for the full 90 days. Adjacent biz-op niches (ecom, dropship, FBA) are tested only by accident — if a non-trading inbound lands, sell them, but don't pursue.
- **Webinar build.** [[frameworks/webinar-framework]] reserved for Phase 4+.
- **Dashboard rebuild.** Current `dashboard.html` is parked. Outreach dashboard is a separate workstream beginning after this plan ships.

## Daily and weekly cadence (the only schedule that survives execution)

**Daily (Mon–Sat):**
- 30 min: agent decision queue + outreach queue review.
- 60 min: outreach send (target 4–6 touches/day during Phase 2; 8–10/day during Phase 3).
- 60 min: client delivery work (Phase 3+).
- Variable: content shoot or content prep on shoot days.

**Weekly:**
- Monday morning: dashboard read, plan the week, pick the shoot day.
- Wednesday: content shoot or content review.
- Friday: weekly metrics log, agent runs lint report.
- Sunday: protected (university, rest).

**Exam weeks:** override above. Founder runs decision-queue check daily (10 min). Everything else paused.

## Open questions / gaps

- The hit list is in the founder's IG inbox but not yet exported. Single highest-leverage Phase 1 task.
- The placeholder-VSL approach (Day 7 raw, Day 21 polished) trades polish for speed. Confirm the founder is willing to send people to a raw VSL during the first two weeks.
- The two-client target by Day 90 assumes 1 close per ~50–70 outreach touches. If reply/booking rates undershoot in the first 2 weeks of Phase 2, the funnel layer (VSL specifically) is the first place to look, not the outreach copy.
