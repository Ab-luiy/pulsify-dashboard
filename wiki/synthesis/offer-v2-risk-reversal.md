# Offer v2 — risk-reversal rebuild (proposal)

A proposal to swap the v1 headline guarantee — "$100,000 in new revenue in 90 days or Pulsify works free until they hit it," unbounded, no execution gate — for a credible, bounded, execution-gated structure that closes a higher percentage of booked calls and caps founder downside. Everything else in [[playbooks/pulsify-offer-v1]] stays. This is a draft for the founder's call, not a decision.

## Why this matters for Pulsify

Swing 2 of the $1M push is "make every booked call worth more." The fastest way to do that is not more traffic — it's removing the one thing on the call that makes a qualified, ready-to-buy prospect hesitate. Right now that thing is the guarantee. It is the boldest line in the offer and, in this market, the boldest line is doing the opposite of its job.

## The core problem with the v1 guarantee

The v1 guarantee hurts in three directions at once:

1. **It is not credible.** "$100k in 90 days or I work free" is the exact sentence every burned biz-op buyer has heard from a guru who disappeared. Pulsify's whole thesis — [[concepts/trust-recession]] — is that in 2025–2026 a big unconditional promise *raises* the skepticism wall instead of lowering it. The guarantee fights the positioning the rest of the brand is built on. We sell "raw, no flexing, value-first," then open the close with the most flex-coded promise in the niche.

2. **The downside is unbounded.** No time cap, no hour cap, no client-side execution gate. [[playbooks/pulsify-offer-v1]] already flags this as a known, founder-accepted risk: two clients missing the number during a capacity-constrained window (exams) consume 100% of founder time at zero marginal revenue while five other engagements run. A guarantee that can bankrupt your calendar is a guarantee you will eventually be afraid to honor — and prospects feel that hesitation.

3. **It guarantees the wrong variable.** $100k collected depends on the client's own closing skill, will, and cadence — things Pulsify does not control. You can install the system; you cannot make a coach take their sales calls. Guaranteeing an outcome you don't control is how a guarantee becomes a dispute.

## The principle: credible beats maximal

In the trust recession, the guarantee that closes is the one the prospect *believes*. A smaller, specific, conditional promise out-closes a giant unconditional one, because belief — not size — is the conversion lever. The reframe: guarantee the **inputs you control** (the system, the cadence of output) at a number a skeptic finds obviously deliverable, then let the rev-share prove the upside.

## The rebuild — a risk-reversal tree

Three structures, in order of how I'd deploy them. They stack with the existing base + performance model and the negotiation tree in v1.

### A. System guarantee (the new default headline)

> "In 90 days your full back-end is live and producing — content engine shipping 2 videos/week, funnel wired, sales infrastructure running, AI agent installed. If any piece isn't built and live, I keep building free until it is. You're never paying for a promise; you're paying for an installed machine."

Guarantees what Pulsify 100% controls. Credible to a skeptic, impossible to dispute, fully bounded by your own delivery (which you'd do anyway). This becomes the headline.

### B. Bounded, gated revenue guarantee (the upside layer, optional add-on)

> "On top of that: hit your cadence — 2 videos/week, take every booked call, full integration — and if you haven't generated $50k in new attributable revenue in 120 days, I work free until you do, capped at 60 additional days. Miss cadence two weeks in a row and the continuation clause voids."

Three changes from v1: number drops to a believable $50k, the window has a hard cap (60 extra days, not infinite), and there's an execution gate (the exact mitigation v1's own open-questions section recommended). Offer this only when a prospect needs a revenue number to move — many won't, because A + the Nick proof already does the work.

### C. Month-1 rev-share trial (the friction-killer, already in the playbook)

> "Worst case: month one is pure performance. Small base, we split new revenue 80/20 in your favor, and if you don't see [conservative number] you walk and I keep nothing. Best case we lock the 90-day engagement."

This is already the call-closer in [[playbooks/pulse-system-outreach]] — v2 just promotes it from "friction handler" to a named, standard risk-reversal. It's the strongest close for a cold or skeptical prospect because the risk genuinely sits with Pulsify for 30 days.

**Recommendation:** Lead with **A** as the headline on every call and in the VSL/asset. Hold **C** as the close for any prospect with friction. Use **B** only when a specific revenue guarantee is the thing standing between you and the signature — and even then, gated and capped. Retire the unbounded "$100k or free" line entirely. The Nick case ($10–20k → $50k/mo, on video) already supplies the believable revenue story; you don't need to promise it, you can show it.

## What stays exactly the same

- Base + performance dual structure and the [[playbooks/pulsify-offer-v1]] negotiation tree ($5k+30% open, $5k+30% floor).
- The qualification gate, the integration requirement, the disqualifiers.
- The week-by-week delivery and capacity logic.

Only the headline risk-reversal changes. The economics don't.

## The funnel's real weakest link right now

Separate from the offer: the funnel is essentially unbuilt (17 YT subs, no live VSL, no Typeform, no wired booking). But outreach (swing 1) is the active bridge, and it converts DM reply → asset → call **without** the funnel. So the highest-leverage closing-rate fix on the funnel side is not "build the whole funnel" — it's the **pre-call path**: a one-link Cal.com booking + a 60-second pre-call asset (per [[concepts/hammer-them-strategy]]) so booked calls actually hold and arrive warm. That's the cheapest layer that moves close rate today.

> Note: the founder's personal VSL has been removed from his own funnel by decision (VSL stays a client deliverable, not part of Pulsify's own funnel), and the IG profile aesthetic renovation is parked. Neither is the lever here — the lever is booking path + pre-call warm-up.
