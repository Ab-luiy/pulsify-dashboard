# Pulsify founder and product launch direction

Founder-requested direction: a warm personal story intercut with premium motion showing what the app can do, supported by Instagram stories and a carousel. The five supplied X references are filed in [[visual-references/studies/pulsify-launch-2026-10-03/README]]. The plan below is an original draft for discussion and production, not evidence of client outcomes or a finalized SaaS offer.

## Why this matters for Pulsify

Content brings the right coach into the conversation. The offer combines useful infrastructure with help deciding what to say and how to sell it. The funnel gives the viewer one clear next step: ask for a walkthrough. The launch should demonstrate content → offer → funnel using one example a coach recognizes.

The founder wants the feeling of a builder on his rise. That comes from visible decisions, work and progress. Keep the client's problem present so the personal arc earns attention. “Change the content game forever” is an ambition; demonstrate the changed workflow before using a category-changing claim.

## Core mechanics

### Positioning and narrative

Working line: **I built the way I work into an app.** Alternative product line: **Turn what you know into a content plan you can actually execute.** These are proposed copy, not approved testimonials or claims.

The pain hypothesis is scattered execution: experience in someone's head, rough ideas in notes, packaging decisions elsewhere, and no clear next action. It must be validated against real prospect language. Avoid claiming all creators have the same problem or pretending the app alone creates authority.

SooWei's documented mission, identity and implementation angle contributes founder motive and access to judgment. Niksetting's documented sequence contributes personal photos, lessons, proof, product reveal and a warmer close. Pulsify's version shows real work, current product states and attributable evidence. Source income, luxury imagery and promised multiples do not transfer.

### Proposed 65-second film

| Time | Picture and movement | Narrative job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–8s | Founder, warm desk, eye-level camera; short opening caption | Recognizable execution friction |
| 8–15s | Notes and a real working page; one phrase isolated | Explain what led to building |
| 15–28s | Actual YouTube flow: experience/pain → idea → title/thumbnail | First useful product proof |
| 28–36s | Return to founder; music drops under natural voice | Personal motive and a moment of enjoyment |
| 36–49s | Same idea → plan/tasks → Content review; purposeful close-ups | Show that the idea becomes executable work |
| 49–57s | Founder reviewing an angle beside the app; brief second format preview only if clear | Explain consultation's role |
| 57–65s | Founder invitation, then calm product lockup and one CTA | Start a qualified conversation |

Keep one example title or image across the product shots. It supplies continuity like the photography reference's persistent image. Use actual screen recordings or faithful animated extracts of verified UI. The metaphor must not appear to promise a click the app cannot perform.

### Draft spoken spine

Read aloud and personalize before recording. Approximate duration depends on the founder's delivery; shorten to fit after the first take.

> You can know your subject inside out and still get stuck turning it into content. What do you say? How do you package it? What happens after the idea?
>
> That's the part I've been working on with Pulsify: connecting the decisions so you can see your next move.
>
> Start with something you've actually experienced. Find the problem worth talking about. Build the angle, the title and the plan around it.
>
> [Personal insert: what surprised you about building this, and what was actually fun. Use a true moment.]
>
> Then bring the work into review. You can see what needs to happen, who owns it and what needs another pass.
>
> And there's room for me in that process: helping you decide what matters, how it connects to your offer and what to improve.
>
> If you want to see how this could work for your content, message me “PULSIFY.” I'll walk you through it.

The CTA is a proposed manual DM invitation, not a promise of an automated reply or booked consultation. Confirm the walkthrough capacity before publishing. Do not say “I've spent years,” “clients already made X,” or describe an emotional building moment until the founder supplies the truth.

### Nine-story sequence

Stories carry the fuller copy. Alternate personal imagery, a talking clip and clear app evidence. Text boxes should fit photo negative space, with readable contrast and selective emphasis. Avoid putting every frame on a flat graphic background.

| Frame | Draft content | Asset |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | “I've been building something around the part of content people keep getting stuck on.” | Founder at desk, warm light |
| 2 | “You have experience. Getting it into a useful idea, a title and a plan takes a different set of decisions.” | Notes/work photo |
| 3 | One true observation from doing this work; attach a concrete example | Founder photo or short talk |
| 4 | “Here's the flow.” Show experience → angle → packaging | Actual app capture, limited text |
| 5 | Show the same example entering a plan and review | Enlarged proof screen; hold for reading |
| 6 | Talking clip: what made building this interesting and one thing you changed | 10–15s candid founder recording |
| 7 | “The app gives the work a place to move. We can work through the decisions together.” | App + review/session image |
| 8 | One small useful takeaway the viewer can apply today | Cozy personal photo; fuller copy |
| 9 | “Want me to walk you through it for your content? Reply PULSIFY.” | Founder close; one invitation |

This uses curiosity → obstacle → proof → belief shift → close from [[frameworks/story-sequence-system]]. A launch does not need invented revenue proof: the demonstrated workflow is process proof. Add outcome proof later when it is real and publishable.

### Eight-page carousel

Working headline: **Your experience deserves a better route into content.** The carousel develops the argument; it is not a transcript of the film.

1. Founder portrait, headline, one small app detail. Strong scale contrast.
2. Execution friction: experience → notes → disconnected decisions. Three concrete fragments, not an unsupported market statistic.
3. Founder angle: why this workflow was worth building. Final copy requires his true story.
4. First product moment: experience becomes an angle. Large, legible current screen.
5. Packaging: that angle becomes a title, thumbnail direction and plan. Preserve the same example.
6. Follow-through: tasks, ownership and review. One useful before/after state; label demo content.
7. App plus consultation: infrastructure alongside help choosing and improving the work. No invented pricing or support entitlement.
8. Personal close and the same walkthrough CTA.

Proposed composition: photo cutouts and masked app crops, restrained grain, large type, small captions, one central visual per page. Background movement translates into still light and texture. Avoid glass on every card or unreadable full-dashboard screenshots. A design should remain good with animation paused.

## How Pulsify uses this

### Product truth at production time

Shared memory on 2026-10-03 documents guided YouTube flow, planning/tasks and review handoff to Content; shared AI switches and a client-own-AI prompt handout for Copywriter/carousels. The new chat/Gemini/per-client-key and learning work was not documented as deployed at the time of reading. Verify the actual deployed build immediately before capturing.

Do not animate a real-time “generate everything” flow from a prompt if only the handout/manual workflow is live. Do not claim autonomous optimization, guaranteed leads, revenue or self-learning. If an AI feature is demonstrated, explain the actual client-key setup where relevant. The launch can still show planning and review clearly without a generation claim.

[[infoos-build-2026-10]] supplies lead/link/content tracking context, but a whole attribution tour would overload this first film. Use a separate capability film later. Current portal/navigation changes are being worked on concurrently; old fixed-sidebar screenshots should not dictate the shoot.

The earlier delivery model was service-led, with the app as a client window. The founder now wants app plus consultation messaging. Preserve that as a requested direction while pricing, access, consultation frequency and service scope remain open. This page does not silently replace the commercial model.

### Art direction

Warm low-light founder footage; natural skin; graphite and charcoal product surfaces; restrained coral/violet/slate glow outside the key content; silver-white controls; quiet grain. Founder shots breathe. Product inserts get a quicker tempo, then land on readable states. Use a few intentional transitions tied to meaning, with clean cuts for human register changes.

“Crazy Photoshop freelancer” translates into composition, masking, lighting, depth, texture and rhythm. It does not require maximal effects in every scene. Source glass/cursor tricks are tools to emphasize decisions. Bakers ribbons belong in small ambient zones so the UI remains clear. Cozy should feel specific to the founder's real space.

### Footage and asset checklist

- Founder opening, personal midpoint and closing takes; leave a pause before and after each line. Record clean room sound and voice separately if needed.
- Five real photos: desk, hands working, wider room, a review moment, a relaxed personal moment. Leave negative space for story text.
- One grounded content example followed across the app; no private client data or keys on screen. Use founder-owned or clearly labelled demonstration content.
- Current UI states: input questions, idea card, title/thumbnail, plan/tasks, Content review. Capture at high resolution and keep important text legible after cropping.
- Current logo, font and approved colors; original layered scene assets. Founder portraits require real footage/photos, not invented likenesses.
- Licensed music and sound; music underneath speech. Source-track names do not establish commercial rights.

### Production and review

Make four designed stills first: founder opener, app reveal, workflow proof and close. Then animate a 10–15s section to validate the founder/product transition. Complete the long film after the visual system and pacing read clearly. Keep scene assets and timing editable for revisions and cutdowns.

Code-rendered motion is a feasible route for repeatable UI shots: deterministic time-based states, browser frame capture and video encoding. A compositor/editor can combine these with founder footage. Neither approach supplies art direction by itself. No third-party code was installed in this study, and no finished render has been created.

Proposed deliverables: one 9:16 master, one short product reveal, nine stories and an eight-page 4:5 carousel. Those are production targets, not current exported assets. Review at phone size with platform overlays: typography, reading time, truthful screen states, subtitle placement, skin color, transition clarity, audible speech and a readable CTA. Review the full film once muted and once with sound. Do not claim the polished film is an app-generated export unless the product actually creates it.
