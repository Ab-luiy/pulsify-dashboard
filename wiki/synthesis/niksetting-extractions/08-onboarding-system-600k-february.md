# CORE CLAIMS

**Claim 1 — The two things that matter: outcome and experience.**
Quote: "there are two things that matter. It's the outcome that a customer gets and it's the experience that a customer gets."
Restated: The whole system splits into outcome infrastructure (roadmap, SOPs, accountability) and experience infrastructure (personal videos, pens, retreats, awards).

**Claim 2 — The pre-call video pre-sells; calls close in 30 minutes.**
Quote: "The first thing they see a preall [pre-call] video. This is a video with me and my COO where we will be breaking down our offer so they are prepared the moment they get on the sales call. When they get on the sales call, most of the time they are already highly qualified. They are already pre-sold and they're closed within 30 minutes because we frame and position our marketing to be able to attract these dream clients."
[= 04's Herman pre-call fix + 15's 80/20 marketing inversion, operationalized.]

**Claim 3 — Small personal touches are the differentiator ("date your clients").**
Quote: "when you date a girl, it's always about the small things. It same counts when you date your clients." Examples: the personalized congratulations video ("a personalized video of me welcoming them to the program"), the custom pen ("I'm going to send them a custom grow acquisition pen. I'm gonna like write them a um handwritten letter, a note with my signature... This is what nobody is doing.").

**Claim 4 — No coaches, ever; specialists deliver their own domains.**
Quote: "we don't hire coaches we don't work with coaches we have never worked with coaches we don't have a coach on salary because every single client that gets on board get to speak with our executive team[.] this are people working in the trenches with grow acquisition daily[.] we have a very lean team it is around 10 people... when we host our setter training, our setter gives that training, not a random coach. When we host a ops meeting, my ops manager does that training... we do content. I do that myself... we have specialists in each field."
Anti-generic warning: "as a founder, you must take this aspect of the company so [ __ ] serious because if you're not, then people will always have the feeling that everything that you're doing is extremely generic."

**Claim 5 — Written accountability separates them from visual-only programs.**
Quote: "people don't only want to have visual information and implementation. People need to have accountability. People need to have SOPs. People need to have documentation to be able to track accordingly. And this allows us and the team to accordingly hold each client accountable based on all the written accountability touch points as well as all the visual accountability touch points because we will make comments everywhere."

**Claim 6 — The 50-question market research document: know the client in 12 hours.**
Quote: "Herman tells them all the time, listen, you need to take 20 minutes out of your time to fill in the most annoying document of your life. And we are not doing what our clients want. We are doing what they [ __ ] need... You're probably not asking 50 specific questions, which is the reason why you get to understand who your client really is after 3 months and we get to understand who our clients are after 12 hours. That is the difference."
[Direct analogue of Pulsify's Brand Brain intake — same function, same friction-acceptance. Cross-ref [[playbooks/brand-brain-intake]].]

**Claim 7 — The roadmap converts thoughts into execution; clarity loves simplicity.**
Quote: "A road map gives clients visibility of how to turn their thoughts and feelings into action and execution... the most important thing a customer wants is a customer wants clarity. And that clarity is the only thing that can shape their vision into [ __ ] reality." / "this system looks extremely simple. And you're completely right. The moment you start over complicating this and you start to add complexity to it to make it look [ __ ] fancy is the moment you lose every single customer... Clarity loves simplicity."

**Claim 8 — Three things cannot be systematized.**
Quote: "You can systematize almost everything that I show you here today. There's just a couple things you can[not] systematize and that are the one-on-one calls you have with your clients. The second thing you can[not] systematize is the group calls... or the mastermind call[s]... And the third thing that you can[not] systematize is these beautiful client retreats because you obviously can't systematize an inerson experience. But that is the reason why it gives that extra personal touch."

**Claim 9 — Fulfillment beats marketing at the finish line.**
Quote: "Even though your marketing could be excellent, you will never beat the person with an excellent fulfillment. You will never[.] at the end [ __ ] line you'll always lose compared to the person with an amazing product."

# FRAMEWORKS AND STEP-BY-STEPS

**The customer journey (in order):**
1. **Pre-call video** — founder + COO break down the offer before the sales call.
2. **Sales call** — pre-sold, closed within ~30 minutes.
3. **Congratulations video** — personalized welcome after payment.
4. **Three required steps:** book the personalized onboarding call ("where their future game plan will be made"); get access to all digital assets; join the Discord ("access to all the channels... all the resources and SOPs").
5. **(Sometimes fourth) Client retreat invitation** — if one happens within 30 days ("eight spots, a private villa... seven days and these seven days have a complete routine").
6. **Physical touches** — custom pen + handwritten signed letter.
7. **Awards** — "A 100K club award, a 500k club award, and a million dollar club award," handed out in person ("26 members that will come physically in person to meet us in Marba [Marbella], Spain").
8. **Team exposure** — executive team intro videos (Isaiah's welcome video quoted: "I'm Isaiah. Welcome to Grow Acquisition. I know how good Nick's front end is... which is where not only Nick, but myself and the team come into place. I create this video to set expectations").
9. **Modules + written SOPs** — visual and written for every process step.
10. **Start-here section** — "a 20[m]inut[e] onboarding video of every single step that they need to complete before they get started... as well as the market research document."
11. **Market research document (50 questions, ~20 min)** -> **personalized roadmap.**
12. **Post-close 5-minute conversion survey.**
