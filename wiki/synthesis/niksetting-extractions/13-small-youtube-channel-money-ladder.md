# Niksetting 13 — "I have a small YouTube channel, how do I make money?" — Claude extraction

The shortest transcript in the set (337 words) and the densest: a six-step revenue ladder from small channel to $10M media company. Reads like a scripted short — every step maps to a claim developed at length elsewhere in the corpus, so this is effectively his own index of the system.

# SOURCE INVENTORY

- **Title:** "I have a small Youtube Channel, how do i make money?" — 337 words, scripted short-form monologue.
- **One-line topic:** The six-stage monetization ladder for a small YouTube channel: niched videos -> undocumented content -> in-person dominance -> second channel -> media company -> distributed faces.

# THE SIX-STEP LADDER (verbatim, complete)

**Step 1 — Niched videos -> $100K/year.**
Quote: "You must solve one tiny piece of an expensive problem. Format is completely unimportant. Can be raw, can be whiteboard, it doesn't [ __ ] matter. 25 of these and you get multiple people to pay you five figures for your link in description. Now you're at 100K a year."
Restated: 25 hyper-niche videos solving fragments of an expensive problem monetize via a five-figure offer in the description. [Note "expensive problem" = 18's market-with-capital rule; "one tiny piece" = micro-niching, cf. Pulsify's [[concepts/micro-niche-youtube]].]

**Step 2 — Undocumented content -> $1M/year.**
Quote: "you switch from documented content where you sit down to record to undocumented content where you do the work and the camera happens to be on. The information is the exact same, but you see how the level of authority changes and how much time you will save on ideiating and procrastination. Now you're at a million a year."
[The cleanest definition of the documented/undocumented pair in the corpus — resolves the terminology wobble across 10/18.]

**Step 3 — In-person dominance -> $3M/year.**
Quote: "now you're still a niche specialist in your market. We must become an industry leader needing just one thing, dominance. We change our format now to in-person masterminds and events while the topics can be completely similar. Now you're doing 3 million a year."
[= transcript 10's format 4, authority pairing.]

**Step 4 — Second YouTube channel -> $5M/year.**
Quote: "create a second YouTube channel. This is the best trick that I can give you where you will be talking about specific lessons, not from a technical standpoint, but a philosophy standpoint. At this point, you're the master already. You give your perspective on topics and no one can judge your wrong. Your main channel now goes more broad to max out the temp [tempo?] that we've been talking about because conversion is already a habit for you. You're doing five million a year."
[Inverts transcript 04's channel-role description — see CONTRADICTIONS.]

**Step 5 — Media company -> $10M/year.**
Quote: "you stop becoming a creator. You build a media company within your business and trust somebody else to do it for you. The strategy, the ideation, the production. Show up and leave. You're at 10 million a year."

**Step 6 — Distributed faces (no revenue figure).**
Quote: "you own the distribution, but others multiply it. You're not the only face anymore because you bring your own most valuable team members in the [ __ ] content. That's exactly what matters what I'm doing."
[= 04's de-centering play and 10's team-in-content, positioned as the terminal stage.]

- Ladder: 25 niched videos -> $100K/yr -> $1M -> $3M -> $5M -> $10M/yr.
