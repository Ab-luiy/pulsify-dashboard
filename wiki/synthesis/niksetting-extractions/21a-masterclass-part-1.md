# CORE CLAIMS (deltas vs transcript 02)

**Claim 1 — The masterclass promise.**
Quote: "you don't have a system for generating money... For the next two hours, I'm going to walk you through the system... by the time we finish today, you'll be running it on your own audience."

**Claim 2 — The repetition multiplier: 10x / 3x / 50x.**
Quote: "if I focus on something and for me it's like a 10x multiplier because I hear it every single day. For any of my clients it could be a 3x multiplier because they see it in a course, they see in the group calls... For a random viewer that sees you whatever once every week and doesn't really care. It could be like a 50x, right? They need to hear the same thing for 50 times until that person is actually in the same state that you are the moment you hear it once."
Restated: Message exposure needed scales inversely with proximity — the quantified version of repeat-past-boredom. ("You want to understand like how [ __ ] annoying it was to say the word the profile funnel like after like 2,000 times.")

**Claim 3 — Premature pivoting destroys compounding.**
Quote: "if you have an idea and you test it, you need to do it long enough consistently to understand if it works or not... your audience is just at the starting point of understanding it. So, if you change because in your mind it's like saturated, you start over and over again. So you never build that brand credibility... the only reason a loyal audience is built is by trust and trust is built by repetition."

**Claim 4 — One mechanism, every testimonial (the sales-call unlock).**
Quote: "If I have a testimonial with Mark or Ibrahim or Ammer or Conrad... It's not one person I help with X, one with this, one with this system... They all got successful because of the profile funnel, right? So Herman hops on a sales call with someone. It's like, 'Oh, you're an Airbnb coach.' Oh, yeah. We have like two people that did the exact same thing. They got to 200 grand a month."
Restated: Uniform mechanism attribution turns every client into evidence for the same claim — the compounding value of never diversifying the story.

**Claim 5 — His own before/after.**
Quote: "Three years ago, I I was earning around 30 grand a month. So this was like in the beginning of 2023... and my profile look like this... now today we do 500k plus every single month. We hit our record month in February. This month we do a little bit lower. We're like consistently at 450 to 550. And we have 174 followers [174K] with everything that we've done over the past like 3 years."

**Claim 6 — You don't need more reach.**
Quote: "truth be told, you don't need to reach more people. At least in all honesty, like most of you don't at all. You need to convert the ones who already know you exist."

**Claim 7 — The niche-doubt killer: ask Claude.**
Quote: "Go to claude.com. Type in or do like a voicemail with whisperflow. Say like I'm in a niche. How many people are in my exact niche?... Are you going to get an accurate answer? I can promise you you're not going to get an accurate answer. But I can promise you that the accurate the answer that you're going to get is more accurate than the answer in your brain... you'll break your thought pattern immediately."
[Third corpus instance of Claude-as-tool (04, 09). The prescribed questions: niche population, how many do what you do, how much money the niche has made.]

**Claim 8 — Go all-in when it works; most people slow down in comfort.**
Quote: "most people slow down when things go well because it's comfort. other people... start to actually gain traction and put all their attention and work into something that doesn't work because they want to make it work... when you feel like something is working super well, you should go like all in. When you got that first client, like [ __ ] get another one... Don't pause ever until it stops."
Platform risk: "What if Facebook decides to block you one day?... I have my recurring revenue for my clients. It's the only thing I have then because new traffic is done."

**Claim 9 — Growth is team-learning, not monthly revenue.**
Quote: "there are months where we make less than we make the prior month, but we learn more because internally we feel like we made progress together... growth isn't measured by the amount of money you make. It's measured by the ability to understand how the team is moving... stagnation is like the worst thing. That's why you all pay me."

**Claim 10 — The 90-day follower's real blocker is one simple question.**
Quote: "Imagine someone follow you like 90 days ago... They've watched 30 more real[s] since and maybe a YouTube video... but they still don't know whether you can solve their problem or not... The only thing that you think they need is a longer form YouTube video with way more information... but the only thing they need to see is like... an offer dock... Or it could be a transformation... show them the end destination." / "Mark and Conrad, one of our biggest pain points is does this work for my niche?... I think half of you that paid had the question... the most simple questions is always the answer to getting more buyers."

**Claim 11 — The 5% ceiling and buyers-only feedback.**
Quote: "you have an audience of 100,000 people. There will for sure never be more than 5% that would ever buy anything from you... like 1% is already very very good." / "95% of your audience responds and likes and engages with your stories. Your buyers never ever do that. Most of the time, your buyers aren't the people that like, they don't save, they don't do anything... you're listening to advice and feedback from people that will never pay you." / "Nobody likes your story sequences... the people that like your stories are the exact people that will never pay you."
Right feedback source: "It's the people that took the action not the people that set [said] certain words."
[= 20's buyers-only feedback + the invisible-traffic thesis, quantified.]

**Claim 12 — The algorithm rewards volume; your bank account doesn't.**
Quote: "Instagram algorithm is optimized to pose more and more. So, you should post more. Like [ __ ] that. That's like it's like the 5% that you should focus on... The algorithm rewards you for the volume. Your bank account doesn't." / "20% of the things that you're doing deliver 80% of the actual results."
