Quote: "value is when you provide people the thing that they actually want... let's say people want to hire a setter. It's like a setter doc script, a system. You provide them that... # FRAMEWORKS AND STEP-BY-STEPS

**The five story-sequence types (completed):** Q&A (part 2), educational, credibility, value-driven, lead magnet.

**Educational sequence:** "You pick one topic that your ICP cares about. You walk them through it across six different slides." Belief-shift delivery (old way/new way). Outcome-led.

**Credibility sequence (structure):**
1. "You open with a result. 170K, last seven days, love the team master [mastermind]."
2. "Then you have just wins across the board" — group meeting, client win, "different client win from Alex."
3. "you close with a position. So[:] you have made a piece of content and doubting about posting it..." (a belief-shift close that doubles as education).
Materials: "screenshots, client messages, Stripe dashboards."

**Value-driven sequence:** "You tell a story that contains something genuinely useful... You set a scene and you build tension for that scene... You walk them through a framework or a lesson. The goal is to show your level of thinking and to make them feel like they got something for free." Visual quality mandatory.

**Lead magnet sequence:** CTA on slide 1 (volume goal); ends in the email list. Hook = outcome + specificity + speed.

**The universal 6-step slide structure (every sequence):**
"Every story sequence follows the same six steps. It's [1] curiosity, [2] obstacle, name what blocks them. You call out the pain... [3] Statement[,] back it with proof of why certain things work. [4] Believe [belief] shift the frame transition. [5] You show the result and trust[.] [6] close from abundance."
Pain-vs-situation rule: "If I say, 'Oh, are you making 50 grand a month?' That's not a pain. That's the situation. And the situation hits way less hard than the pain does."
Mastery note: "after a few weeks you won't need this... it becomes an instinct."

**The email sequence (8-9 emails / 14 days):**
"The email sequence closes what the story sequence opens. Eight to nine emails over 14 days. Each one reframe[s] the same idea."
- Email 1: "deliver the lead magnet" + proper introduction + profile picture on the sender ("when you introduce yourself to someone, [with] a bag over your face. It's [ __ ]").
- Email 2: "tell them the story behind it" (example: "me and my creative director Max thought about implementing a new short form content format... attracting almost double the qualified leads").
- Emails 3-5: "different client wins" (example: "Jay, you'll make 300 350K in like six months and you share the client result").
- Email 6 (and 4-6 blur): "the process about unclarity, impatience, invisible" — value/education reframed; "It's the same [ __ ] as the story sequence... you can tell the same [ __ ] here."
- Email 7: "CTA 2DM [to DM]."
- Email tone rule: "emails is very like forced right very corporate... the leverage you can create for yourself is making it personalized[.] if you do the opposite of what everybody does, you get the best results."
- Emails route traffic to YouTube (confirmed in Q&A: "you send value through email and direct the traffic to YouTube? >> Yes. Exactly.").

**Lead magnet opt-in page fields:** "where are you currently [in] your journey[?] Monthly revenue. Select your range... Get your game plan in 30 seconds. 2.5K downloads. Now it's I think eight [8K]."
