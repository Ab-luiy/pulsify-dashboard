# Niksetting 09 — "You're not ready for the next phase of social media" — Claude extraction

The market-math video: social media -> interest media, entertainment -> education, and the full TAM calculation proving niche content beats broad reach ($65M pipeline in 6,500 business coaches). The quantitative backbone of the whole views-don't-matter doctrine.

# SOURCE INVENTORY

- **Title:** "You're not ready for the next phase of social media." — 1,753 words, talking-head with on-screen written calculation.
- **One-line topic:** The interest-media thesis and the customer-pipeline-value math: why 6,500 right viewers are worth more than millions of entertainment views.

# CORE CLAIMS

**Claim 1 — Social media is becoming interest media; education sells, entertainment doesn't.**
Quote: "We're moving away from social media to interest media. We're moving away from entertainment and we are slowly moving to education. People don't like to get entertained anymore. People love to get educated. That's how the world is moving. And education sells. Entertainment doesn't."
Restated: The platform game is re-sorting by interest/topic, and educational content is the monetizable kind.

**Claim 2 — The grandma-slap test: views without pain-relevance never convert.**
Quote: "if I would stand on the street and if I would slap a grandma in the face... It will most likely get a lot of views, an insane amount of views. But what I'm showing isn't related compared to customer conversion... you will never be able to convert a customer the moment your topic isn't related to their pain. The easiest way to solve this is by making niched videos around topics that your audience cares about. So you have a current avatar, make videos that that avatar cares about. And that's very simply said. That's very boring, but it's exactly how it works."

**Claim 3 — Ask converted clients which content converted them.**
Quote: "I was on a mastermind in um Spain 2 weeks ago and we had 25 people... I asked all these guys one question... 'what is the most valuable piece of content that you've watched from me?' And I got so many valuable answers... where we saw a pattern in the feedback... based on the videos that they liked the most and the videos that made them actually convert."
Chain: "the right information from the right people will allow you to make the right content for the right audience. And the right content for the right audience will mean the right audience for the right amount of conversions."
[= 20's only-listen-to-buyers + 19's lesson 16, operationalized as a mastermind survey. Direct parallel to Pulsify's onboarding-form question "which video converted you."]

**Claim 4 — The market math (his on-screen calculation, verbatim figures):**
- "there are 30 million... online entrepreneurs."
- "coaches, specifically course sellers and online creators, there are 5 million of them."
- "those making 100 grand a year. We are talking about 200,000 people."
- "A million a year... It was anywhere between 15 to 18 grand [thousand], but let's say it's 15 grand. Okay? 15,000 online entrepreneurs... making 1 million a year." (Caveat: "I might be wrong. So, make sure you research this on Claude or online.")
- Business coaches specifically ("anything related to lead generation... conversions... personal branding" — excluding health/relationship/fitness/dating): "There are 450,000 people."
- "Those making 100 grand a year are 70,000 people."
- "Guess how many people make over a million a year? 6.5,000 people... specifically... US and Europe."

**Claim 5 — Most of your current views are structurally worthless.**
Quote: "there are 70,000 people but most likely only 500 of them are watching your content right[?] and all the other tens of thousands hundreds of thousands of views that you get a month are... online entrepreneurs that will never buy from you in the first place." / "You rather want to have 100,000 views with a 100,000 business owners that really care about what you do, have massive interest, have capital, and relate with your topics, have these pain points than getting 1.5 million views of just entertainment purposes."

**Claim 6 — Customer pipeline value: the $65M niche.**
Quote: "I always love to calculate customer pipeline value... It's your current offer price. Meaning, let's say $10,000 compared to the amount of people that are behaving themselves in your niche, the exact avatar. Meaning, it's 6 and 1/2 [thousand]... $65 million specifically that you will be able to make from this market." / "if you would niche down to the max and you would sell to everyone, you would have 6,500 customers and you would make 65 million [ __ ] dollars. Now, I don't know if you can even name one person that made $65 million with info, right? Maybe three people."
Corollary: "the guys that made 10 20 million... sold to 10 20% of the market... and they made an insane amount of money."
Restated: Even a maximally narrow niche's total pipeline exceeds what any player has ever extracted — niching down cannot run out of market. [The exact counter to the client fear Pulsify handles when pitching micro-niching.]

**Claim 7 — More views should worry you.**
Quote: "the more views you get currently, the less money you make based on this exact calculation, the more you should [ __ ] worry. And this is the exact reason why conversion is important."

# FRAMEWORKS AND STEP-BY-STEPS

**The market-stage segmentation (client-count ladder):**
- 10 clients: "just made his first money online. He's making like 100 grand a year."
- 50 clients: "anywhere between 500 grand to 700 grand a year, right? Like 50,000 a month."
- 250 clients: "one of the top dogs... at least one, two, 3 million a year." ("realistically, let's say, a 100 people, 200 to 500 people" at this tier)
- 1,000+ clients: "one or two people... making at least 10 million a year."

**Customer pipeline value formula:** offer price x addressable avatar count (e.g., $10,000 x 6,500 = $65M).

**The convert-survey loop:** ask converted clients which content converted them -> pattern the answers -> make more of that.

# VOCABULARY

- **Interest media** — the successor to social media in his framing.
- **Customer pipeline value** — offer price x avatar population.
- **The avatar** — used straightforwardly as ICP.

# CONTENT MECHANICS

- Niched videos around avatar pain topics as the only conversion-valid content.
- Views as a diagnostic of misalignment when they outpace the niche's population.

# BELIEFS AND CONTRARIAN TAKES

- Entertainment content is a dead strategy for sellers.
- High view counts are a warning sign, not a win.
- The market is small, and that's good news.
- Openly instructs viewers to verify his figures on Claude — data humility inside a confident frame.

# EXAMPLES AND NAMES

- **The Spain mastermind (25 people)** — the convert-survey source.
- **Claude** — again named as the research tool.

# STORIES

- The mastermind survey and its pattern finding (contents of the pattern not shared — see GAPS).

# CONTRADICTIONS AND AMBIGUITIES

- The figures are self-flagged estimates ("I might be wrong") yet the entire $65M conclusion is built on them.
- "6.5,000 people" then "currently 7,000 people in the US and Europe" — rounding drift.
- 450,000 business coaches vs 70,000 at $100K vs 6,500 at $1M — the tiers mix "business coaches" and earlier "all coaches" numbers loosely.
- The client-count ladder's tier populations ("100 people, 200 to 500 people") don't sum against the 6,500 cleanly.

# GAPS

- The actual pattern found in the mastermind feedback (which content types converted) is withheld.
- Sources for all market-size figures.
- No guidance on operationalizing "interest media" beyond niched avatar videos.
