# App audit, 2026-10-01: cleanup, readiness, enrichment

Every client portal page was opened live through Kyle Kirshner's workspace on 2026-10-01 (the only workspace in production: 2 visits, $0). The operator console needs the founder's own sign-in, so it was judged from its menu, its code, its tests and its live data endpoints (all returned 200), not from clicking through it. Screens are in output/qa/2026-10-01-audit/.

## Why this matters for Pulsify

A prospect judges the product in the first five minutes of a call. Too many menu items, empty pages and repeated cards make it look unfinished even when it works.

## What to clean up

1. The client menu has 16 pages. Proposed 10: Home, Content (calendar, plans, to-dos as tabs), YouTube, Instagram, Copywriter, Performance (revenue and all-time tracker as tabs), Leads, Pages, Brand (voice and media library), Settings (team and integrations inside).
2. The operator menu has 20 items, five of them for outreach alone (Leads, CRM, Outreach queue, Outreach tracker, Inbox). Proposed 11: Home, Today, Clients (client brain, client work, reviews and funnels as tabs inside each client), Leads (CRM, queue and tracker as tabs), Inbox, Content, Pages, Email, Payments, Integrations, Settings.
3. Home shows two onboarding cards at once ("Start your onboarding" and "Launch your workspace"). One card.
4. The top bar holds Ask, clocks, Censor, bell and the light switch on the client side, and adds search and the date range on the operator side. Keep the bell and light switch; move clocks and Censor into the open island.
5. Plans, To-dos, Team and Content calendar still say "from the client dashboard" in their copy and are empty unless the operator fills them.
6. Small: a font preload warning in the console on every page.

## Ready to use

- Me: Today, Inbox, Outreach tracker, CRM and Leads, Client funnels, Client brain. The data endpoints answer and the tests pass; not yet judged by daily use.
- Clients: onboarding form, Home funnel score and what to fix, Instagram stories with the review gate, YouTube videos ranked by cash, Leads hub, Pages, Settings, Media library.

## Not ready

- AI writing is off in production (no key), so every draft is copy and paste.
- The guided new-video YouTube flow is built but unreleased (branch claude/growth-youtube).
- Instagram daily sync has failed since 19 Sep (#25).
- Integrations: all four connections read "not connected" (client sign-in integrations are parked until the first client pays).
- How clients pay Pulsify (#24), and sign-in hardening (#5, #7).
- No demo workspace: a sales call has only a $0 workspace to show.

## Needs enrichment

- Empty pages should offer a starter, not just say "nothing yet": a starter roadmap for Plans, a starter list for To-dos, example story sequences and title and thumbnail pairs in the YouTube and Instagram flows, example answers in onboarding.
- A demo workspace with labelled example data.
- Short plain-words help on each score (Funnel score, Client status).
- The onboarding walkthrough video (already on the backlog).

## Open questions

- Whether the founder wants the menu merges above.
- Which of the four unreleased or broken items to do first.
