# The messaging and reframe system

The persuasion layer of the personal brand: how a coach's mechanism, positioning, and beliefs get encoded into every piece of content. This upgrades [[concepts/unique-mechanism]] from "have one" to "how to weaponize one," and it is the layer the story-sequence and reel frameworks both draw their words from. Built from both corpora; corroborated claims stated as rules, single-source items attributed.

## Why this matters for Pulsify

Positioning, not skill, is the observed difference between lookalike accounts earning 5K and 50K. Every Pulsify client arrives as a category member ("trading coach," "ecom mentor") competing on price and deliverables — a race to the bottom. This system takes them to category of one, gives them the reframe that makes their content unhearable-once-heard, and calibrates the money language that decides who shows up on their calls.

## 1. Category of one (the positioning recipe)

The three questions (in order):
1. **Name it uniquely.** "You currently have a framework, a process, due to the fact that you have a skill. How can you name this so uniquely that nobody else can copy it?" (Amer: "identity architect," not "mindset coach." The mechanism gets a proprietary name: the profile funnel, the identity thermostat.)
2. **Build vocabulary the market repeats.** Coin the terms, use them everywhere, let clients and competitors carry them. When people repeat your words, the credit routes back to you.
3. **Position around belief, not service.** People must see an author/expert/specialist, never a service provider, course seller, or guru.

Rules that follow:
- Wrong words enter you into invisible competitions; unique framing removes you from every comparison set ("if you're a category of one, you can't be compared").
- Easiest-to-understand beats best — for bios, offers, and content alike. "A confused mind always says no."
- Offer "a different way," never "my way": the market has heard everyone's way.
- SooWei's identity layer: combine three hard-to-replicate identity anchors into a one-of-one (his: revenue at an age + university + family values). Distinct from the mechanism name; both are needed. For clients, source the anchors from the buyer mirror, not from self-image.

## 2. The reframe formula (one reframe, driven so deep they can't unhear it)

The three-step build:
1. **Find the agreed belief** — what the prospect already tells themselves ("I just need better habits and more grinding").
2. **Reframe it** so agreeing with the old belief becomes impossible ("You've never made a conscious decision in your life, ever").
3. **The induced conclusion** does the selling ("I need to rewire my identity, not just my habits") — which is precisely what the offer delivers.

Deployment rules:
- ONE reframe per brand era, repeated consciously and unconsciously across all content, until the market associates it with the creator. Not five reframes; one.
- The repetition multipliers govern patience: the creator hears it 10x more than clients, ~50x more than a cold viewer. Boredom is the signal it's starting to land, not the signal to change.
- Every piece of content passes the stimulation test: "does it stimulate people to use my mechanism?" — mostly indirectly.

## 3. Direct vs indirect (the mechanism dial)

- **Direct** = say the word ("the profile funnel has helped me scale — here's a client"). Easy, and needed in proof/CTA content.
- **Indirect** = never say the word; shift the belief the mechanism answers ("the way people make decisions in 2026 will be different... people are moving from offers and frameworks to people and brands"). Harder, therefore more valuable; this is what makes content feel like insight instead of advertising, and it pre-positions the offer so the sales conversation starts at deliverables, not persuasion.
- Working ratio: indirect carries the content engine; direct appears in bio, proof pin posts, and explicit CTA assets. (Engineered CTAs remain primary — founder-ratified dial 3.)

## 4. Money language calibration

- **Divide by three:** any revenue figure in copy attracts people earning roughly a third of it. Write the aspirational number at ~3x the client you want on calls.
- **Numbers are stage scaffolding:** lead with figures while credibility is thin; retire them as proof accumulates ("I don't use numbers anymore").
- **Vocabulary reach check:** insider words feel universal inside the bubble ("info business" ~200K people). Check reach before building copy on jargon — ask Claude, literally (both creators do).
- **Pain, not situation:** "are you making 50 grand a month" is a situation; the reason they hate being there is the pain. Call the pain.
- **Sell what they want, deliver what they need** — packaging follows desire; fulfillment follows diagnosis.

## 5. The polarity law

Attract and repel equally — bio, content, brand. "The most powerful brands on earth are hated and loved"; the lukewarm middle produces followers who never buy. SooWei's version: lean into the extremes even if you're privately moderate. Pulsify's stance IS a polarity play: anti-flex in a flex industry — say it loudly, accept who it repels.

## 6. The objection-to-content loop (corroborated three ways)

Sales calls and DMs are the messaging R&D lab: mine transcripts (Claude on Fathom/call notes) for objections; any objection heard 3+ times is a marketing failure -> make the content that kills it (belief-shift reel, breakdown sequence, pre-call breakout video). The 0.1% iceberg: for every spoken objection, ~999 silent holders. Also run the convert survey (buyer mirror / "which content converted you") — the answers are next quarter's content calendar.

## How Pulsify uses this

1. **Client onboarding:** mechanism naming + three identity anchors + THE reframe are Week 1 outputs alongside the Brand Brain — they gate the content engine (nothing to say until they exist).
2. **Generator inputs:** the story-sequence generator and titles-thumbnails skill both consume the named mechanism, the reframe, and the trigger word; this page is where those get minted and recorded per client (clients/{name}/overview.md).
3. **Pulsify itself:** our mechanism (the Pulse System), our reframe candidates, and our polarity (anti-flex, proof-currency) run through the same machinery — the founder page's authority anchors are the three-identity build.
