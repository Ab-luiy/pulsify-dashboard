# The carousel system

> Corpus expansion, 2026-10-08: [[synthesis/carousel-reference-forensic-audit-2026-10-08]] adds a complete examination of all 76 stored carousel images, with nine visual families. The three structures below are observed options; their conversion performance is not established by the screenshots. Feature demonstrations, case-mechanism reveals, result archives and sourced personal chronicles also occur. For current production use [[content-engine/reference-replication-profile]] and [[playbooks/reference-replication-sop]]: purpose/copy first, actual photos/proof, photo-sensitive composition, registered subject depth and semantic figures. Exact source claims/identity are not Pulsify proof, and one ordinal/chip/template grammar does not describe the full corpus.

The canonical Pulsify framework for Instagram carousels. Carousels were the thinnest area of both transcript corpora (one section in Niksetting 17: life-update mode + storyline mode); this framework is built primarily from three captured, fully-designed Niksetting carousels ([[content-engine/carousel-breakdowns/]]), which show what the transcripts couldn't — the design craft and the swipe mechanics. It is the save-optimized authority format, sibling to [[frameworks/story-sequence-system]].

## Why this matters for Pulsify

Carousels are the grid's authority-and-save format: they get bookmarked and re-opened, which is a strong buyer signal, and they can carry depth a reel can't. Niksetting's carousels also do something strategic — a carousel can BE a landing page (his 7-step methodology carousel) or a full framework taught for free (his 7-type carousel). For Pulsify, a carousel is how a client's mechanism or proof gets packaged into one saveable, swipeable document. This framework unblocks the portal's carousel studio.

## The two base modes (from transcript 17)

- **Life-update mode:** photo-led, congruence-first — "I've been in Dubai for a week, here's the experience." Status/credibility, verbal-visual sync (what's shown matches what's claimed). Lower text, higher aesthetic.
- **Storyline mode:** headline + 5-6 steps + CTA. "This is how I made $4 million as an online consultant. Step 1... Step 5... CTA: DM/comment the word." The teaching/conversion carousel.

## The three proven structures (from the captures)

1. **Methodology-as-document** (the grow-acquisition carousel): cover (hook + proof wall) -> N numbered pillar slides, each = ghost number + bold title + one-sentence promise + a staged photo carrying a real artifact (dashboard, doc, chat journey, notifications) -> CTA slide (close from abundance + trigger word). This packages an entire offer/process as a scroll — a landing-page alternative. For Pulsify: the Pulse System's pillars templated exactly this way.
2. **Taxonomy/matrix** (the 964k carousel): cover (specific-number hook) -> one slide per type/item, each with a definition + at-a-glance chips (a 3-chip color code: e.g. cadence / function / audience) + a real example mockup -> final CTA slide with a live proof element (a reach donut). This teaches a whole framework for free and sells the deeper version. The chip system is the reusable innovation.
3. **Philosophy/concept set** (the 7-lessons carousel): cover -> N one-word concept slides, each a single belief-shift over a cinematic photo, big drop-shadow typography -> the last concept doubles as thesis/close. Packages beliefs as a saveable set; the carousel equivalent of a value/breakdown story sequence.

## Design system (the replicable craft)

- **Cover = the thumbnail.** It is the scroll-stopper and gets the deepest design investment: a hook line (specific number, "pov:" frame, or one provocative word) + a visual that either shows proof (a wall of wins/thumbnails) or signals the medium. Everything downstream assumes the cover earned the first tap.
- **Typography as system:** large sans titles, one branded flourish (Niksetting: numbers woven into words, or single-word drop-shadows). Pick one type treatment and hold it across the set.
- **The number/sequence drives the swipe.** Numbered pillars (1->7) are the strongest completion pull; one-word concepts rely on curiosity instead. Cross-slide continuity (a consistent frame, a promised count) keeps the swipe going.
- **Every slide carries a proof artifact** where possible — a real dashboard, chat, notification, screenshot behind or beside the text. Shown, not claimed.
- **At-a-glance chips** (the 964k innovation): color-coded pills that let a reader absorb metadata without reading prose. Adopt for any "types of X" carousel.
- **Register:** dark, warm-lit, high-production is his default. Pulsify's: clean and real over luxurious — the artifacts (student dashboards, build screenshots, whiteboards) carry the credibility, not the setting.

## Cadence and role

- ~1-2 carousels/week (transcript 17), mostly proof or breakdowns.
- Carousels are the **save format** — the account-level metric that matters most for them is save rate (still undefined in the sources; provisionally saves/reach). They also convert followers via link/DM CTAs (comment-trigger for reach, DM-trigger for intent — vary per post as he does).
- One long-form idea can become a carousel, a set of reels, and a story sequence (the derivative pipeline).

## The CTA

- Storyline and methodology carousels end on a trigger word: comment "X" (boosts engagement/reach) or DM "X" (routes higher intent). Match to goal.
- Caption mirrors and reinforces the carousel's spine and repeats the trigger.

## How Pulsify uses this

1. **Client deliverable / studio:** the carousel studio (portal P4) generates from this framework — pick a structure (methodology / taxonomy / philosophy) + topic + proof, get slide copy + design direction per slide + the chip scheme where relevant. Same contract discipline as the sequence generator.
2. **Pulsify's own:** the Pulse System as a methodology-carousel; the story-sequence types as a taxonomy-carousel (our own version of the 964k matrix); our core beliefs as a philosophy set. These double as sales assets.
3. **Proof currency swap (standing):** adopt every structure and design move; swap luxury visuals for real-work artifacts and student results.
