Use for: "why don't people book / show up / buy", leads, sales calls, the landing page, VSL, application form, ads and webinars.

Check first, with their numbers:
- Walk the path: views, profile visits or clicks, DMs or applications, booked calls, calls that showed, closes, cash. Read their videos, leads, calls and plan before you answer, and find the step where people drop.
- Call outcomes if they are logged (no-show, not qualified, not interested, closed).
- If nothing is tracked, say so plainly. Tracking which video brought each call is the first fix.

What good looks like:
- Find the closest bottleneck before prescribing anything. Clicks dropping: more trust and proof content. Clicks but no bookings: missing or weak bottom-of-funnel content, or friction on the way to booking. Booked but not showing: framing between booking and the call. Showing but not buying: the offer, the pitch, or the wrong people on calls.
- Call outcomes tell you where: many no-shows means the pre-call framing is missing; many not-qualified means targeting or qualification upstream is off; many not-interested means the wrong people or a gap in the offer; a high close rate with low volume means it works and needs more traffic.
- Order matters. Offer and mechanism come before the content system, and the content system before a VSL, ads or a webinar. Don't build a later step before the earlier one works. Paid ads come only once the organic funnel converts and the math works.
- The profile funnel: content leads to a bio that passes the 3-second test, then two paths (a DM trigger word, and their long videos), stories closing daily in the background, and email holding the relationship.
- An application before the calendar filters people: rapport, where they are now, the biggest thing stopping them, commitment and budget, how soon, then contact details.
- A VSL: a hook (result, who it's for, the mechanism), a mirror of the problem, the mechanism as the reason past attempts failed, proof with the transformation first and from the original source, the offer with the price, one call to action to the form.
- Page length follows the audience: a tiny, pre-sold niche needs a headline and a booking link; a large audience needs the full page.
- Call math, with their numbers only: revenue target divided by price gives closes, divided by close rate gives shows, divided by show rate gives bookings.
- Webinars need cold traffic and ad budget, so they come late.

Avoid:
- Prescribing pre-call emails when the real problem is that nobody books.
- Presenting industry benchmarks as their targets.
- Recommending ads before the funnel converts organically.
