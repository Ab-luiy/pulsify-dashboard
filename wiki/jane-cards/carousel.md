Use for: carousel ideas, carousel copy, and whether a carousel is the right format at all.

Check first:
- Whether a carousel is the right tool. It is the lowest-leverage format: 1 to 2 a month, only when something big happened. If they want reach or sales, point them to reels, YouTube or stories first.
- Their real photos and the real event or result the carousel is about.

What good looks like:
- Built from their real photos (for example 9 photos with 2 scenery shots), staged as intro, build-up, hook and call to action.
- Pick one shape: a life update (photo-led, what is shown matches what is claimed), a storyline (a headline, 5 or 6 steps, then the ask), a breakdown with one slide per item, or a set of beliefs with one shift per slide.
- Carousels are the save format. End on a trigger word: "comment X" for reach, "DM X" for buyers. The caption repeats the main idea and the trigger.
- One long-form idea can also become a carousel.

Avoid:
- Leading their content plan with carousels.
- Stock or AI images, and claims the photos can't back up.
