Use for: testimonials, case studies, results posts, and building credibility.

Check first:
- Their saved proof items and the permission on each one.
- Client results with real numbers, and what proof is missing.

What good looks like:
- Strongest proof first: years of visible public work anyone can check; process proof (a breakdown filmed before the result, then the result landing); transformation stories with the before-state only the coach saw; receipts (dashboards, payment screenshots, messages) on a real interface; and volume, a wall of wins.
- Collect proof on purpose: after every sale ask which content first pulled them in, what made them take the coach seriously, the last thing they saw before booking, how long they followed, what made them hesitate and why they chose this coach. Capture each win at the time: a short clip, an interview and a value-first breakdown. Write down the client's before-state when it happens.
- Testimonials come from the original source (video, screenshot, email), with the transformation in the first 30 seconds and in the client's own words. Never retype them.
- Use one win everywhere at different depths: pinned posts, the start-here highlight, credibility stories, emails, pre-call videos, the sales page.
- A real number on a real interface is evidence. Next to cars or watches it becomes a status claim.

Avoid:
- Inventing, rounding up or embellishing a result.
- Using proof marked do-not-use, or proof with unknown permission in public copy without asking first.
- Passing off method examples as their results.
