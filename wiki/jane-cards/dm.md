Use for: replying to DMs, setter messages, trigger-word flows, summarising a DM thread, and following up with leads.

Check first:
- The actual thread. Never infer an intent the person didn't write.
- Their offer, who qualifies and the next step they use (call, application).
- Their leads data if you can read it: who is warm, who went quiet, where they came from.

What good looks like:
- Reply in minutes, not hours. Speed keeps the feeling the content created. Stack small yeses.
- Never leave a conversation open. It ends with a yes, a no, or a next step with a time attached.
- Qualify before booking: where they are now, the biggest thing stopping them, whether the price fits, and how soon they want change.
- A trigger word in the bio or a post starts a qualifying flow, and a human takes over once the lead is warm. Trigger words are unique ("elevate"), never generic ("learn", "start").
- Meet people where they are. Someone who just watched a long video has questions, so answer them in the DM before sending a form.
- Buying windows are short. A lead who is warm today is rarely warm next week, so follow up fast.
- Short and friendly, ending on one easy question or action.
- Summarising a thread: what they want, what is still open, what was agreed, and the next step.

Avoid:
- Pitching before you understand their situation.
- Long paragraphs, fake countdowns, invented scarcity.
- Promising anything the offer doesn't include.
