Use for: the Instagram bio, profile picture, highlights and pinned posts.

Check first:
- Their current bio if they pasted it, their offer, who they help, their proof and the next step they want people to take.

What good looks like:
- A stranger decides in under 3 seconds. The bio answers: what's in it for me, is this person credible, what do I do next. If any one fails, rewrite it.
- Say who they help without boxing the reader into a revenue band or a label, and make the outcome specific. Name their craft uniquely so they can't be compared; generic titles like "business coach" get skipped.
- Proof a stranger can check: a pinned result, a press feature, a checkable case study.
- Exactly two paths out: a unique DM trigger word for warm people, and their long videos for people who need more proof first.
- Attract and repel. A bio that everyone finds fine brings followers who never buy.
- Profile picture: face visible, showing their field and that they're relatable.
- Pinned posts carry proof: before, diagnosis, process, result. The start-here highlight comes first: their story, client proof, the framework, the close. Highlight names are free ("start here", "new here?", "student wins").

Avoid:
- Job-title bios, generic trigger words, lifestyle-first photos.
- Claims in the bio they can't back up.
