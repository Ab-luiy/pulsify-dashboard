# Content funnel — TOF, MOF, BOF

Every piece of content Pulsify produces for a client maps to a funnel layer: Top of Funnel (attract), Middle of Funnel (nurture), or Bottom of Funnel (convert). The funnel layers determine what the content is designed to do — not just what it covers.

## Why this matters for Pulsify

Most coaches produce content randomly — they post whatever they feel like and hope it converts. Pulsify produces content with intent. Each video has a specific job in a specific funnel position. This is why a 400-view video can book more calls than a 2,000-view video: the 400-view video was built for BOF; the 2,000-view video was built for TOF.

The customer journey is not linear in practice but it is linear in design. Pulsify builds the ecosystem so that a new viewer can enter at TOF and arrive at a sales call through a logical progression of content.

## Core mechanics

### Top of Funnel — Attract

**Goal:** Reach new audiences, spark engagement, shift beliefs, create awareness that this creator and this mechanism exist.

**What TOF content does:**
- Creates attention-driven content optimized for searchability and shareability
- Qualifies viewers through retention loops (who keeps watching self-identifies as the ICP)
- Breaks limiting beliefs (e.g., "YouTube doesn't work for high-ticket")
- Creates an open loop that pulls the viewer toward MOF content

**What TOF content does NOT do:**
- Over-explain the how (gives the "wow" not the "how")
- Make a hard sales pitch
- Assume any prior relationship with the creator

**Content types:**
- Evergreen "how to" searchable videos (per Ayman Arab: "how to find a product," "how to run ads" — each step of the niche process becomes a video)
- Belief-shifting videos ("Why YouTube actually works for high-ticket")
- Trend-adjacent videos adapted from proven formats in other niches

**Viewer signal they need:** A clear "this is for me" signal + a broken assumption + a fast win or insight + an open loop

### Middle of Funnel — Nurture

**Goal:** Build trust, establish authority, begin qualifying the viewer as a real prospect.

**What MOF content does:**
- Delivers contradicting content (challenges conventional thinking in the niche)
- Provides in-depth breakdowns of real results and processes
- Shows social proof: client results, case studies, before/after
- Begins booking sales calls — MOF is where purchases start to happen
- Removes fears: "this won't waste my time or money"

**Content types:**
- Niche-specific deep dives and process breakdowns
- Case study breakdowns (viewer self-identifies: "that's my situation")
- Behind-the-scenes of the delivery model
- The unique mechanism explained in depth

**Viewer signal they need:** Personal relevance + emotional certainty + momentum + fear removal

### Bottom of Funnel — Convert

**Goal:** Push high-intent viewers to book a call.

**What BOF content does:**
- Functions as a video sales letter — every BOF video is treated like a mini-VSL
- Uses client interviews where the student states transformation in first 30 seconds
- Provides behind-the-scenes proof the solution works
- Contains intentional, explicit CTAs: book a call, fill the form, DM the trigger word

**Content types:**
- Client interviews (transformation first, then backstory)
- Behind-the-scenes of implementation with a client
- Direct comparison videos ("Why my approach vs. the generic approach")
- "What you get" breakdown videos

**Viewer signal they need:** Final proof + direct instruction on what to do next

## The ideal customer journey

```
1 TOF video (discovers creator via search or recommendation)
  → 2-3 MOF videos (builds enough trust to take next step seriously)
  → 1 BOF video (ready to book, just needs the push)
  → Landing page / Profile Funnel
  → Booked call
```

When someone books a call having watched 3–5 videos, they arrive pre-sold. The salesperson closes, not persuades.

## Content ratios

Ayman Arab recommends 50% TOF, with MOF and BOF making up the rest. Alexei recommends building the funnel structure first (TOF → MOF → BOF) and measuring which layer is bottlenecked before adjusting ratios.

In practice, Pulsify's weekly content system defaults to:
- Reels (daily): mix of TOF hooks and MOF proof
- YouTube (1–2/week): TOF how-to videos early, MOF breakdowns as the library grows, BOF client interviews and case studies
- Stories (daily): primarily MOF/BOF — testimonials, backstories, behind-the-scenes

## Tracking which layer is bottlenecked

Per Ayman: use Trakyo (trakyo.io; transcribed as "Trackio") to track calls booked per video and the full customer journey before each booking. Common patterns:

- **Offer clicks dropping** → post more MOF/BOF content
- **Clicks high but calls not booking** → BOF content is missing or weak
- **Calls booking but not converting** → offer or funnel needs attention (not a content problem)
- **No tracking at all** → you're flying blind; this is the default state for most creators
- Pulsify is building this tracking into the client portal: [[synthesis/cash-by-video-spec]] (ticket #36) ranks every video by cash collected, not views.

## Inside one video: the horizontal funnel (added 2026-10-15)

Founder-directed insight, to be used whenever we break down content funnel stages. Source: a reel by Kobey Nguyen ([[raw-sources/transcripts/kobey-nguyen-horizontal-funnel-reel]]).

The idea: the three stages don't have to be three separate kinds of video. They can run left to right inside a single video:

- **First ~20% is TOF.** The opening speaks to a mass audience, anyone at all, to win the view.
- **The middle is MOF.** It speaks to the dream follower: a problem that's still broad, but your ICP sits inside it.
- **The end is BOF.** It speaks only to the ICP: their exact pains and wants, the things that move them toward buying.

Why it matters: a micro-niched value video no longer has to give up reach. Open it wide, narrow it in the middle and land it on the buyer at the end, and the same video can pull TOF-level views and still sell.

How it fits what we already have:
- It's the same overlap as SooWei's ICP vs dream follower split ([[synthesis/soowei-goh-extractions/01-3m-yr-organic-content-strategy]]), applied to time inside one video instead of to topic choice.
- It doesn't clash with the founder's 2026-09-28 rule of no dedicated BOF videos (BOF is the landing page and VSL). The BOF here is the closing section of a video, not a separate video.
- It partly answers the open question below about Ayman's "free course video" doing all three stages at once: that's this pattern at full length.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: the reel says "top, middle and bottom of funnel is completely dead". This page and the rest of the vault still use the three stages to plan *what* content to make (the ratios and journey above). Resolution: keep both. Use the stages across the content mix, and use the horizontal pattern inside each video. The reel's "dead" claim is a hook, not a reason to drop the stages.

How Pulsify uses it: the YouTube "build the video" step should outline videos in this order (wide opening, dream-follower middle, ICP ending), and the client onboarding uses it to teach TOF, MOF and BOF ([[synthesis/onboarding-v2-spec-2026-10-15]], 10.1). Untested on client videos so far; judge it by cash per video ([[synthesis/cash-by-video-spec]]).

## Outside view: Dustin Varano's layer model (added 2026-09-29)

Logged as extra context, not adopted. Dustin's layers: TOF = lifestyle vlogs (most views, weakest leads), MOF = one multi-hour "flywheel" video that does the pre-selling ([[concepts/flywheel-vsl]]), BOF = in-depth breakdowns for people who already understand the model, plus a separate proof layer (testimonials, case studies).

> ⚠️ Contradiction: this page defines TOF as searchable how-tos and belief shifts and puts in-depth breakdowns in MOF. Dustin puts lifestyle vlogs in TOF and in-depth breakdowns in BOF. Resolution: keep this page's definitions; Pulsify does not post lifestyle content. The useful parts of his model are the linking rule (every supporting video points to one anchor video) and his agreement with the 400-vs-2,000-view point above: niche, low-view breakdowns bring the highest-intent viewers. Source: [[synthesis/dustin-varano-the-keys]].
