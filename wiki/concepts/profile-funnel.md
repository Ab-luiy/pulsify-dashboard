# Profile funnel

The Profile Funnel turns an Instagram profile into the conversion mechanism itself: bio, pinned posts, highlights, story sequences, and a DM process replace (or precede) a separate funnel page. It is Pulsify's default build for clients without the audience or budget for a full landing-page funnel — and, since the 2026-07 ingestion, the vault holds the complete slide-level mechanics from its originator's own material (Niksetting, who claims the mechanism's invention and credits it for his Forbes feature).

## Why this matters for Pulsify

Most Pulsify clients are Marcus-stage creators: 5K-20K followers, no ad budget, no funnel infrastructure. The Profile Funnel captures inbound from content, qualifies in the DMs, and books calls with near-zero build cost. It is also pull marketing in its purest form: every asset is self-selling and permanent, so effort compounds instead of resetting the way ads do ("if I spend time on building a profile funnel... it's still existing tomorrow"). The economics at maturity: conversion cycles of 24 hours to 7 days, calls arriving pre-sold, and a sales layer of one setter plus one closer.

## The funnel flow

```
Content (reels, YouTube, stories) -> profile visit
  -> Bio passes the 3-second test, triggers curiosity
  -> Two paths, always both:
       DM trigger word -> automation/setter -> qualification -> booked call
       YouTube long form -> deep trust -> DM or call
  -> Story sequences close daily in the background (+1%/watched story)
  -> Email (via lead magnet) owns the relationship off-platform
  -> The web, not a line: unclosed leads cycle back through
     email -> YouTube -> stories -> call, never out
```

## What gets built (in order)

1. **Bio.** A stranger decides in under 3 seconds. Three questions must be answerable instantly: what's in it for me, is this person credible, what do I do next. Rules: state who you help without boxing into a number or identity label; name the craft so uniquely it can't be compared (category of one — generic titles get brain-skipped); include backtrackable proof (verifiable, e.g. a press feature or a checkable result); polarize — the bio should attract and repel equally, because the lukewarm middle produces followers who never buy. If off-ICP followers accumulate, the filter is broken.
2. **Profile picture.** Two questions: what is my field of expertise, and how am I relatable to my audience. Face visible, authority posture.
3. **Bio CTAs — exactly two.** One DM trigger word, one to YouTube long form. The trigger word is named uniquely ("elevate"), never generically ("learn", "start", "grow"). The word routes to automation or a setter (ManyChat where automated; human takeover when warm).
4. **Proof pin posts.** The first thing on the grid: pinned transformation reels structured before -> diagnose -> process -> result. The one format that does both jobs (views and buyers).
5. **Start-here highlight.** The permanent first highlight, tapped first by every new visitor — the silent sales pitch. 10 slides: story (3), client proof (3), framework (2), close (2). Full spec in [[frameworks/story-sequence-system]]. **Founder-ratified:** whether start-here or a landing page carries conversion for a given audience is a test, measured by where that audience interacts; the landing page is the never-fails fallback, and cold outreach always keeps pages.
6. **Daily story sequences.** The closing surface. Five types, 6-step slide structure, 3/week floor and 5-7 target — all in [[frameworks/story-sequence-system]].
7. **Grid and reels.** Reels make strangers problem-aware and redirect (they don't close); carousels and posts carry congruence — what is shown must match what is claimed (verbal-visual sync). Full format ladder in the content-engine pages.

## The DM layer (conversion-cycle rules)

- Respond in minutes, not hours: speed preserves the emotional state the content created. Stack small yeses.
- Never end an open conversation. Three acceptable endings only: yes, no, or a next step with a time attached. Anything else donates the lead to the paradox of choice.
- Real scarcity in offers, provably real (rotating or capped offers), never fake countdowns.
- The window is short: a lead warm today rarely re-warms next week. The system's KPI is interest-to-decision time; benchmark 24h-7d.

## Paid ads integration

Ads point at the profile, not a landing page ("profile ads"): low cost per follower, and the qualified follower base converts over 30-60 days through content and story sequences. The CTA is "follow me to learn everything for free." Ads feed the self-selling ecosystem rather than replacing it, which is why stopping them doesn't zero the machine.

## When to escalate beyond the profile funnel

- Large audience + budget: webinar.
- Inbound exceeding DM capacity: landing page + VSL + Typeform/Calendly.
- Scaling with paid: ads still enter through the profile first.
- Per the ratified test doctrine, escalation is measured, not assumed: if page interaction beats story interaction for that audience, the page leads earlier.

## Proven results (source-claimed)

- Amer: debt (October) to $200K/month in ~5 months; 3 -> 37K qualified followers; the full rebuild documented in [[synthesis/niksetting-extractions/06-200k-months-amer-case-study]].
- The originator: $5.2M profit over 3.5 years on 322 total posts, ~$500K/month at 174K followers, one setter + closer.
- SooWei (independent, same architecture): 30K -> 408K/month, 10K -> 120K followers, no ads; clients at $38/follower.
- These are source claims, not Pulsify-verified numbers. Pulsify's own deployments are the validation path.
