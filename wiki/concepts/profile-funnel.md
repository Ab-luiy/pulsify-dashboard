# Profile funnel

The Profile Funnel is Pulsify's default conversion mechanism for clients who don't yet have the audience size or budget to justify a full landing page funnel. It turns a creator's Instagram profile into a functional landing page — no separate website required.

## Why this matters for Pulsify

Most Pulsify clients are Marcus-stage creators: 5K–20K followers, no ad budget, no existing funnel infrastructure. They can't run a $50K webinar launch. The Profile Funnel is what Pulsify builds first, before anything else. It captures inbound leads from content, routes them through a qualification flow, and gets them on a sales call — all without requiring a landing page, VSL, or paid traffic.

It also costs almost nothing to implement and starts generating leads immediately, which builds trust with the client early in the engagement.

## Core mechanics

**The funnel flow:**
```
Content (reels, YouTube, stories) → profile visit
  → Bio triggers curiosity (trigger word)
  → DM the trigger word → ManyChat automation fires
  → Automated qualification sequence
  → Human setter takes over
  → Booking call
```

**What gets built:**
1. **Bio** — Rewritten to clearly speak to the ICP. States who you help, what result you produce, and contains the trigger word or CTA
2. **Profile picture** — Clean, authoritative, face visible
3. **Story highlights** — Act as mini-case studies and social proof. Testimonials, results, behind-the-scenes, FAQs
4. **Main grid** — Carousels (educational value + authority) and clips from YouTube when relevant
5. **Trigger word automation** — A keyword in the bio (e.g., "SBT") that viewers are instructed to DM. ManyChat fires an automated sequence, qualifies the lead, and transitions to a human setter when the lead is warm

**The Niksetting reference:** Pulsify explicitly models this approach on "Niksetting style" — an appointment-setting framework that uses story sequences, grid content, and DM automation to convert Instagram traffic into booked calls without a separate funnel page.

## How Pulsify uses this

This is step 5 in the [[pulsify-order-of-operations]]. It is built after:
1. Profile cleanup (bio, highlights, pfp)
2. Unique mechanism created
3. Offer built (high-ticket or mid-ticket)
4. Content pillars established

The Profile Funnel is the default until the client gets big enough for webinars or has the budget for a full VSL landing page. It works for the majority of Pulsify's early-stage client engagements.

**When to escalate beyond the Profile Funnel:**
- Client has a large following and budget → move to webinar
- Client's inbound volume exceeds what DM-based conversion can handle → build a landing page + VSL + Typeform/Calendly
- Client wants to run paid ads to scale → ads still point to the profile funnel first (low CPL, builds qualified followers)

## Content on the profile

- Reels: 1 per day. Value-driven, authority-building, with a soft or hard CTA to DM the trigger word
- Grid: Carousels and educational content. Repurpose YouTube clips when relevant
- Stories: Student testimonials, backstories, storytelling sequences. Daily. Keeps the "red ring" active on the profile.

## Paid ads integration

When Pulsify runs paid ads for a client, ads typically point to the profile funnel, not a separate landing page. The CTA is "follow me to learn everything I know for free." This generates low cost per lead, low cost per profile view, and builds a qualified follower base that converts over 30–60 days through content and story sequences.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: The Lead Sourcing SOP instructs Pulsify's own outreach operators to "post lifestyle content (cars, status symbols)" as Instagram story content to maintain authority while doing outreach. This contradicts Pulsify's stated positioning against lifestyle flexing. This likely reflects two different operating modes: Pulsify's own early-stage operator playbook (pre-brand, using social proof shortcuts) vs. the brand philosophy for established clients. As Pulsify matures, the lifestyle shortcut should be phased out entirely in favor of value-based authority.
