# Lead sourcing and outreach

Outreach generates immediate revenue. It is a bridge channel — it creates leads today while the content ecosystem (YouTube long-form, profile funnel, story sequences) is being built and compounded. Inbound content is Pulsify's primary revenue engine at scale: a prospect who books a call after watching 3–5 videos is warmer, more pre-sold, and easier to close than any cold DM lead. Outreach is the stopgap that funds the business while content compounds. It is not the long-term model.

## Why this matters for Pulsify

Pulsify's primary revenue model is inbound — YouTube long-form content that attracts prospects who are already pre-sold by the time they book a call. Outreach matters because the content ecosystem takes 60–90 days to produce meaningful compounding volume. A client doing 50–100 DMs per day from day one generates revenue while YouTube and the Profile Funnel are being built. Outreach funds the operation. Content scales it. These are not interchangeable — one is a short-term cash mechanism, the other is the long-term revenue engine.

## Core mechanics

### The sourcing platforms

Pulsify sources outreach targets from six primary platforms:

**TikTok**
Search the niche keyword + "coach" or "mentorship." Identify creators in the right niche who are monetizing or trying to monetize. Filter by engagement quality, not follower count.

**Kajabi**
Search for active course creators and membership site operators in biz-op niches. Kajabi users have already invested in infrastructure — they are further along than someone who has not started monetizing.

**YouTube**
Search niche-specific keywords. Any creator posting content about coaching, dropshipping, trading, or any biz-op topic who does not yet have a polished funnel is a qualified prospect.

**Twitter/X**
Biz-op operators frequently post about revenue, lifestyle, and business topics. Twitter's search and list features allow rapid identification of prospects actively talking about the problem Pulsify solves.

**Whop**
Platform for digital product and community creators. Prospects here are monetizing but typically at lower volume — exactly the Marcus-stage profile.

**School (Skool)**
Community platform where coaches run paid communities. Operators with 100–500 paying community members are often plateaued and looking for the next layer of scaling — exactly the Pulsify proposition.

### Volume and daily targets

- **Minimum daily volume:** 50–100 DM outreach attempts
- **Response-to-follow-up ratio:** 5–10 personalized DMs per day to responded leads
- **Loom videos:** when a lead shows interest, send a Loom — one video, specific to their situation, short (2–3 minutes)

The first 60 seconds of a conversation define 391% higher close rates when a response comes back — the research cited in the SOP specifies this explicitly. Speed of first contact matters. Do not let leads go cold for 24+ hours after they respond.

### The outreach sequence

**Day 1 — Initial DM**
Short, specific, not a pitch. References something real about their content or situation. Does not open with a compliment about their account. Opens with a specific observation or a direct question about where they are stuck.

**Day 3 — First follow-up**
If no response. Short. Does not repeat the Day 1 message. Approaches from a different angle.

**Day 5 — Second follow-up**
Brief. Gives them an easy response option (a direct yes/no question).

**Day 7 — Final follow-up**
Last attempt. Closes the loop. Either invites a response or signals this is the last message.

After Day 7 with no response: move on. Do not continue following up beyond 7 days — it damages the account and the sender's credibility.

### The Loom approach

When a prospect responds with interest (or even mild curiosity), the Loom is the most effective conversion tool:

- **Length:** 2–3 minutes. Not longer.
- **Structure:** Their specific situation first (named, referenced) → the specific problem you see → what Pulsify does about it → one clear CTA (usually "book a call" or "reply to this to chat more")
- **Tone:** Matter-of-fact, not sales-y. The Loom is a diagnosis, not a pitch.

The Loom is effective because it is personal and effort-signals. A video made specifically for one person communicates investment before a single dollar has changed hands.

### Proven niches

The lead sourcing SOP specifies the niches with documented conversion performance for Pulsify's offer:

- Trading and investing
- Dropshipping and e-commerce
- Amazon FBA
- Airbnb hosting and short-term rental
- TikTok Shop
- Agency owners (SMMA, media buying, copywriting)
- Course creators and coaches in any biz-op adjacent niche

Niches to avoid or approach carefully: life coaching, wellness, spiritual coaching, fitness — these have different financial profiles and different expectations about what "scaling" means.

### Setter hiring and training

When daily outreach volume + inbound DMs exceed what the operator can manage (typically when response volume exceeds 2 hours/day of DM management), hire a setter.

**Setter responsibilities:**
- Handle initial DM responses on the operator's account or a team account
- Qualify leads against the ICP criteria (niche, revenue range, intent)
- Send Looms or schedule Looms for the operator to record
- Book calls into the calendar
- Execute the Day 1/3/5/7 follow-up sequence

**Setter training:**
- Full ICP profile (see [[marcus-avatar]])
- The outreach sequence word-for-word
- The qualification criteria (when to book vs. when to disqualify)
- The objection-handling scripts for common responses ("I'm not interested," "tell me more," "how much does it cost")

The setter does not close. The closer handles all calls. Setters who try to close calls before they land in the calendar reduce close rates.

## How Pulsify uses this

Outreach runs from day one of every client engagement as a bridge revenue mechanism. In the first 30–60 days it is often the primary source of booked calls, because the content ecosystem has not yet had time to compound. This is a temporary condition, not a permanent operating model.

As inbound grows (through YouTube long-form, profile funnel, story sequences), outreach volume is progressively replaced by inbound volume. By month 3–4 of a properly executed engagement, the majority of booked calls should be coming from content-driven inbound — these prospects are warmer, more pre-sold, and convert at higher rates than cold DM leads. Outreach is never eliminated entirely, but it is no longer the primary engine once content is working. Even at $100K/month, a setter running 30–50 targeted DMs per day remains a useful supplementary CAC channel — not the core of the business.
