# Outreach video and voice touches

Three touches that cannot be mass-faked: a 90 second video in the opener, voice notes for the follow-ups, and a 60 second phone video sent to view once. Each one shows the prospect a real person doing real work on their stuff, which is the thing a templated text DM can never prove. All of it is proposed and untested. Nothing here is doctrine until reply data exists.

## Why this matters for Pulsify

A text DM now reads as mass outreach by default, and the data agrees: the 2026-08-22 batch returned one reply on 33 sends. The one prospect who clearly responded to a recorded walkthrough was `bergtrades`: the 2026-08-11 Loom went out, he watched it, and he answered "send the doc over sure." That is one thread, not a result, and the recording and its transcript are not in the vault, so what exactly it said is not verifiable from here.

The thesis is narrow. The opener and the follow-ups have to prove two things at once: a person looked at this specific creator, and Pulsify actually does what it says. Video proves the second one by showing the work. Voice proves the first one because nobody records a voice note to a list.

## Capacity, and who gets what

Same constraint as [[playbooks/ig-followup-sequence]]: 80 people cannot each get a custom hour. So the media is tiered.

| Touch | Goes to | Cost per prospect (estimate, time the first five) |
|---|---|---|
| 90 second video in the opener | Every lead the founder vets and picks | Research plus a recording |
| Voice note, 20 to 30 seconds | Anyone who opened or reacted, and everyone who gets a delivered asset | A couple of minutes |
| 60 second phone video, view once | Replied-and-warm, or the best leads still silent | One take |

## The sequence

| Day | Touch | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Opener text plus the 90 second video, sent as a file, no link | A lead the founder picked |
| 3 | Voice note: the small built thing | Five minutes of profile research done |
| 7 | 60 second phone video, view once: the small question, spoken | They reacted, opened, or replied |
| 10 | The asset, per [[playbooks/reply-to-asset-sop]] | They answered the question |
| 21 | Clean exit, text | Still silent |

> ⚠️ Contradiction: [[content-engine/cold-dm-formula]] settled on 2026-07-06 that the opener carries a link to the asset. This sequence moves the link out of the first message: the video shows the built thing, and the link comes when they reply. Reason: a bare link in an IG DM is the weakest container we have evidence on, the video plays inline and shows the work in 90 seconds, and an unrequested link to a fresh domain reads as a phishing risk. Only one batch ever shipped links in the first message and it was four sends, so neither side is proven. Resolution: run the video opener on the next 20 and compare. The text-and-link opener stays the control.

Everything in [[playbooks/ig-followup-sequence]] about voice still binds: no pitch, no call ask, no price at any touch, never reference their silence, one specific detail per message, a wrong detail is worse than none.

## Move 1: the 90 second video

The video does one job. It is the opener's proof, and it is also the answer to "how do you actually do what you do." So it has to show the method, not describe it: i went through your channel, i found the one thing, i built it before we ever spoke, here it is.

### The opener that carries it

> ngl your channel kinda took over my week. [one specific nod]. also noticed [soft gap], so i went ahead and built you something, recorded a quick walkthrough so you can see it. it's 100% yours

Then the video, sent as a file. Same five beats as the cold DM formula, same 30 to 45 words, with the link swapped for "recorded a walkthrough."

### The script

About 210 spoken words. Speak it, do not read it. Face bubble on, because the person is the proof. Screen choreography in brackets.

**0:00 to 0:08, who and why**
[their channel open on screen]
> hey [name], it's [your name]. i'm the one who messaged you. i recorded this so you can see exactly what i meant instead of reading it.

**0:08 to 0:30, what i saw**
[the specific thing on screen: the video, the number, the page]
> i went through [channel]. [one specific observation with a real number from their public stats]. that's the part that stuck with me. and then [the soft gap, stated as an opening, never a flaw].

**0:30 to 1:05, what i built**
[scroll the actual asset live: the page, the rebuilt bio, the titles]
> so i built this for you. [piece one, what changed]. [piece two, what changed]. [piece three]. it's yours, there's nothing to buy.

**1:05 to 1:20, how we do what we do**
[five seconds on the dashboard, no more]
> this is how i work with everyone. i go through your stuff, find the one thing that's costing you the most, and build it before we ever speak. then it runs inside a dashboard that counts which video made which sale, so what to fix next is never a guess.

**1:20 to 1:30, the close**
> that's it. which of those would you actually use first? one word is fine.

The close is a one-word question, not a permission request. The full asset page goes out when they answer, per [[playbooks/reply-to-asset-sop]].

### Worked example, written from the Berg intel (not the 08-11 recording)

> hey matthew, it's [your name]. i'm the one who messaged you. i recorded this so you can see exactly what i meant.
>
> i went through your channel. the forex video with the $18,530 did 558 views, and the last four did 62, 181, 373 and 107. same you, same model. the title changed. and your bio is pointing at a second account, so the 10k on your main doesn't have anywhere to go.
>
> so i built this. six titles in the same shape as the one that worked, a rebuilt bio with youtube where the second account was, and a vsl written off your own students. it's yours, there's nothing to buy.
>
> this is how i work with everyone. i go through your stuff, find the one thing that's costing you the most, and build it before we ever speak. then it runs inside a dashboard that counts which video made which sale.
>
> that's it. which of the six titles would you actually film first? one word is fine.

Every figure in it is public and from [[outreach/intel/bergtrades]]. That is the standard: a number they can check and that matches.

### Rules for the video

- No "hey guys", no "so basically", no apology for the recording, no reading off a script.
- No pitch, no price, no call ask, no mention of their silence.
- One specific observation, with a number off their public stats. Never a revenue or enrolment figure we cannot see.
- On screen at phone size: zoom the browser to 125 percent so text is readable on a 6 inch screen.
- Under 90 seconds. Cut anything that is a second thing.
- Do not credit the framework source by name, per the founder ruling in [[playbooks/prospect-asset-visual-standard]].

### Delivery

Record in Loom or any screen recorder, face bubble on, download the file, and send it from your phone as a video in the DM so it plays inline. Two things to check on your own phone before the rhythm is built around this, because they are not verified: the video length and size Instagram accepts in a DM, and whether Loom allows the download on your plan. If either blocks, record with Windows Game Bar or OBS and trim to size.

Trade-off, accepted on purpose: a downloaded file loses Loom's view analytics. The only signal is Instagram's "Seen" and whether they reply. The inline play is worth more than the watch percentage here.

## Move 2: voice notes

Voice replaces the text version of touch 2 and the "just sent it" message after an asset lands. It does not replace the opener. Twenty to thirty seconds, spoken like to a friend, never read.

Why voice: it is the hardest thing to automate and the most expensive-looking thing to send for how little it costs. A prospect who hears a real voice naming a specific detail from their own channel cannot confuse it with a mass send.

### Touch 2, the small built thing, spoken

> yo [name], it's [your name]. quick one. i was back on your [channel/profile] and [the specific thing and the reason it matters, in one breath]. no reply needed, just thought you'd want it.

The content is identical to the text version in [[playbooks/ig-followup-sequence]]. Same three tests: they can do it in under a minute, it is a replacement and not a verdict, the reason is the gift.

### After an asset lands

> yo [name], just sent it over. start with [the one part to open first], that's the bit i'd look at. it's yours either way.

### Rules for voice

- Say their name and one specific thing in the first ten seconds, or it sounds like a list.
- No pitch, no ask. Optional one-word question at the end, never "let me know."
- One note, never two in a row. Re-record a flubbed take; a stumble is fine, a rambling one is not.
- Check Instagram's voice message length limit on your phone before scripting to a time. Not verified.

## Move 3: the 60 second view-once video

A video shot on your phone, sent in the DM so it can be opened one time. It is for the touch where a text would say "i'm putting these together for a few people," and it does the same job with a face: i am not spamming a list, i sat down and made this for you.

### The take

Selfie camera, natural light, no editing. Their profile or channel open on a second screen or in your other hand only if it makes the point, otherwise just talk. One breath of context, one thing, one question.

> hey [name], it's [your name], i messaged you a few days ago. i was back on [the specific thing] and noticed [one observation that is new, not the one in the opener]. [what it means, in a sentence]. did [the thing from your last message] land, or was that too much at once? one word is fine.

That is touch 3 from [[playbooks/ig-followup-sequence]], spoken. The question is the one with a live reply on record: it asks for a word and not a decision.

### Rules

- View once is for the human touch only. Never put a link, a number they have to remember or an instruction they have to act on inside it, because they cannot replay it. Anything they need goes in text after.
- Use it only on people who reacted, opened or replied, and on your best silent leads. Spending the scarce version on a cold lead wastes the one signal it gives you.
- The "opened" status on a view-once video is itself a signal. Log it.
- Check on your phone how Instagram handles view once and replay settings, and whether it notifies on screenshot. Not verified here.
- Never say you are sending it because they did not reply.

## Logging and the test

Log every touch in `outreach/outreach-log.md` with its type: `video-opener`, `voice-note`, `view-once-video`. State and next date live in `outreach/followup-tracker.md`.

The test that makes this doctrine instead of a hunch: send the video opener to the next 20 vetted leads and keep the text-and-link opener as the control on the batch after. Compare reply rate and, separately, how many replies mention the video. Prior batches returned at most 18% and 3%, so those are the numbers to beat. Twenty sends is the minimum before any of this gets rewritten as a rule.
