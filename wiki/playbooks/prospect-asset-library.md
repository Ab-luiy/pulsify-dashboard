# Prospect asset library

Every asset Pulsify has built for a prospect, what wound it treats, and what it takes to build another one. Founder directive 2026-07-28: log every asset as it ships, then propose from this list when the next lead asks for something. The point is that the menu compounds. Nothing gets re-derived from scratch, and the founder's judgment on each build gets written down instead of living in one conversation.

## How to use it

When a lead replies and an asset is owed:

1. Diagnose first. Read their profile, their captions, their bio destination, their offer. The wound decides the asset, never the other way round.
2. Find the wound in the table below and check what has already been built for it.
3. If a proven asset fits, copy the build and refit it. If nothing fits, build the new one and add a row here.
4. Log the ship in the build log at the bottom, including the founder's steer, because the steer is the part worth keeping.

The doctor rule applies. Diagnose before treating, and do not treat an illness we do not specialise in.

## The menu

### Proven

Built, shipped, and rendered at least once.

**Content packaging teardown**
Their existing titles and thumbnails, rebuilt. Wound: they publish long form and the packaging wastes it, usually auto-generated thumbnail frames and titles with no pair. Needs: their live uploads. Built for FITFO, July 2026. Canonical build at `output/fitfo-doc-build-short.js`.

**Funnel and door teardown**
Where their CTA says one thing and their link does another. Wound: positioning is selective, plumbing is open, or the offer has no qualification layer. Needs: bio, link destination, and their own caption CTAs quoted verbatim. Built for acp1re, July 2026.

**Content ladder and YouTube launch**
A long-form channel proposed from scratch, with two or three title and thumbnail pairs, plus how shorts get cut from the long form. Wound: audience buys the lifestyle rather than the expertise, because the expertise is never shown. Needs: their current content style, their niche vocabulary, and any real number they have already published. Built for acp1re, July 2026. Uses the titles-thumbnails skill.

**Cold DM opener**
The gift-first casual-peer DM. Wound: no relationship yet. Needs: one specific reference from their content. Standard at [[content-engine/cold-dm-formula]].

**Story sequence**
Slide-by-slide Instagram story copy with photo direction. Wound: no narrative path from profile to offer. Uses the story-sequence-generator skill.

### Proposed, not yet built

Named in the pipeline and ready to build when a wound calls for it. Do not present these as things we have already done.

**Rebuilt landing page plus pre-call email sequence** — for backend stall, where proof exists but nothing catches the lead. Landing pages are Codex-owned; Claude specs, Codex builds.
**Application or qualification form** — for an offer with no filter. The Lead Studio Typeform generator exists and is parked.
**Pre-call objection page** — short objection videos on one page ahead of a call, for sales-infra stall.
**Offer architecture and unique mechanism** — naming the mechanism a room or mentorship is sold on. Recurring wound: the operator alludes to "my framework" and never names it.

## What every asset must hold

- Their own words quoted back, verbatim, wherever the evidence is verbal. It is the difference between a diagnosis and an opinion.
- Real numbers only. If we do not have their figure, leave the slot marked for them to fill rather than inventing one.
- No CTA. A close offering free labour positions us as a vendor auditioning. Per [[playbooks/prospect-asset-doc-standard]].
- Suggestions, not instructions. Founder directive 2026-07-28: the register is "you could" rather than "do this, and I want this from you." A prospect who feels managed before they have paid anything does not reply.
- Nothing shipped unrendered. Build, export, read the pages, then send.
