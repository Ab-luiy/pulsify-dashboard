# Carousel SOP

## Current shared production workflow, 2026-10-08

Start with the question, purpose, audience, fitting offer, available proof and desired action; write the complete copy/payoff map before styling. Then run [[playbooks/reference-replication-sop]] with [[content-engine/reference-replication-profile]], its JSON, selected actual reference images and registered operator assets. The [[synthesis/carousel-reference-forensic-audit-2026-10-08|76-image examination]] supplies nine visual families and individual source anchors. Stories inherit the same craft through a separate 9:16 layout.

The three structures below remain useful content options, not the complete menu or universal design templates. Add feature demonstration, case-mechanism, result archive and sourced personal chronicle where the job warrants them. Serif covers, mandatory ghost numbers, three chips, proof walls and alternating left/right alignment do not apply to every family. Choose the reference/photo relationship deliberately. Keep recognizable environments, intentional subject-over-text/cards, genuine glyphs and meaningful figures. Never manufacture space with blanket blur or mirrored extensions.

The inspected app carousel sanitizer currently accepts short layout hints without mask/layer/graph geometry. The new scene schema/preflight and app integration contract are specified in the shared SOP; their existence does not mean the live renderer has been upgraded. Older builder steps below must be read with this current scope/precedence.

How a Pulsify carousel gets made, from the idea to the posted set. Founder ask 2026-10-03: carousels get real work behind them and a full SOP, not a quick template. This SOP is also the order of the carousel builder in the client app (Instagram, Carousels).

## Why this matters for Pulsify

A carousel is the save format. It gets bookmarked and reopened, which is a buyer signal, and it can carry a whole method in one swipe. A carousel that looks typed into a template tells the reader the creator is cheap. The design is half the asset ([[content-engine/sequence-carousel-design-system]]).

## Core mechanics

### Step 1. Pick the job and the structure

One carousel, one job. Pick the structure from [[frameworks/carousel-system]]:

- **Methodology.** Your process as a document: cover, one slide per step, close. Use it to sell how you work.
- **Taxonomy.** "The N types of X": one slide per type, each with a definition, three colour-coded chips and a real example. Use it to teach a framework for free and sell the deeper version.
- **Philosophy.** N one-word beliefs, one per slide, each a single shift. Use it to move beliefs.

Cadence: one to two carousels a week, mostly proof or breakdowns.

### Step 2. Write the cover first

The cover is the thumbnail. It gets the most work.

- One hook line: a specific number ("How 14 students passed funded challenges"), a "pov:" frame, or one provocative word.
- Pick one emphasis word. It gets the marker treatment (a red hand-drawn underline).
- Type: a display serif italic woven with a heavy sans word or number.
- Visual: proof (a wall of results, a real dashboard) or the medium itself. Never lifestyle.

### Step 3. Build the inside slides

- A giant light ghost number behind each slide drives the swipe.
- Bold headline, one lighter sentence. No paragraphs.
- Every slide carries a proof artifact where possible: a student dashboard, a chat, a notification, a whiteboard. Shown, not claimed.
- Taxonomy slides add the chip row: three metadata axes, one colour each, the same mapping on every slide.
- Annotations: one to three per slide at most. Red is pain or the wrong path, green is the right move or the win, blue is a pointer.

### Step 4. Close

- Last slide: one ask. Comment a word for reach, DM a word for intent.
- The caption repeats the spine of the carousel and the trigger word.

### Step 5. Check before it ships

- The cover reads at feed size and earns the first swipe.
- One idea per slide, slides numbered or promised ("7 lessons").
- Every number and result is real and permitted. No invented stats, no lifestyle flex.
- Send it for review, then post. Log saves and reach.

## How Pulsify uses this

- The client app's carousel builder follows these five steps and draws a live designed preview as you type (editorial cover, ghost numbers, chips, marker underline, close slide).
