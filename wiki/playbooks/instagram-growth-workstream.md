# Instagram growth workstream — scope and parking notes

The dedicated workstream for Pulsify's Instagram presence. Parked at the profile-renovation level for now (per founder, 2026-05-11: the profile itself is acceptable; further visual polish is gated on growth and money for better photography). Active scope is the content/growth side, not the bio/highlights aesthetic.

## Why this matters for Pulsify

Instagram is the destination every DM points to. Outreach can run on the current profile, but the lever that compounds — without paid traffic and without studio-grade photography — is content cadence and content quality. Story sequences are the warm-up for inbound DMs. Reels are the only IG surface that pulls cold reach. Posting frequency and topical discipline decide whether the profile feels alive when a prospect clicks through.

## What is parked (do not push on this)

- IG bio rewrite
- Highlights cover redesign and reordering
- Profile-grid visual overhaul
- Photo shoot / personal brand photography

Trigger to un-park: a paying client closes, cash exists, growth justifies investing in higher-end visual identity. Until then, the profile is "good enough for outreach to convert against."

## Active scope (what to plan and execute)

### 1. Story sequences

Story is the highest-trust surface on IG. Marcus already follows; story is how he stays warm or decides to DM. Pulsify needs:

- A story sequence template (hook → context → tension → micro-proof → soft CTA → ask).
- Daily story rhythm (target frequency TBD — likely 3–6 frames/day, 5 days/week).
- Sequence types to develop: founder POV, mechanism demo, client wins (once they exist), behind-the-scenes Pulse Brain build, content breakdowns of other operators.
- Highlight allocation: every well-performing story sequence gets saved into the appropriate highlight bucket.

### 2. Highlights — kinds, not redesign

The aesthetic is parked; the *categorization* is active. Highlights are saved story sequences, so the bucket structure governs what to record. Target buckets:

- **My story / New here?** — founder bio, what Pulsify is, the personal arc.
- **Start here** — the entry funnel: what the prospect should read/watch first.
- **Results** — wins, framed conservatively, source-traced.
- **Results 2 / Results 3** — split as volume grows; multiple highlights beat one overstuffed one.
- **Pulse System** — mechanism demo highlights, modular by Pulse Engine / Pulse Brain / Pulse Loop.
- **Apply** — call-booking, qualification, what happens after the DM.

Order to confirm once content exists. New highlights only get created when there are 3+ story sequences worth saving into them.

### 3. Reels

Reels are the only IG surface that reaches non-followers. Without reels, Pulsify's IG is a closed loop. Workstream items:

- Reel topic pool sourced from the same Pulse Engine pillars as YouTube long-form.
- Repurpose long-form YouTube into 3–5 reels per video.
- Native reels: founder-face vertical, 30–60s, single tight idea per reel.
- Hook-first editing rules (first 1.5 seconds decides retention).

### 4. Posting frequency

To define. Working hypothesis until tested:

- Stories: 3–6 frames/day, 5 days/week.
- Reels: 3–5/week.
- Static posts: 1–2/week, mostly proof or carousel breakdowns.
- Lives: when a guest or moment justifies it, not on schedule.

Confirm against actual founder capacity once Phase 1 of the 90-day plan ships.

### 5. Topic discipline

What to talk about is governed by the existing pillars in [[content-engine/content-pillars]]:

- Breaking down a coaching content concept.
- Arbitrary case studies of someone achieving X using Miro/Gamma/Google Docs as the demo surface.
- Breaking down common beliefs around coaching.

IG-specific overlay: more founder-POV, more reaction-style content, more behind-the-scenes than long-form. The IG surface tolerates rawer ideas than YouTube does.

### 6. Scraping successful operators

A system, not a vibe. Needed:

- A watchlist of IG operators whose content patterns work (Marcus-niche-adjacent: trading coaches, info-offer operators, AI-for-coaches voices).
- A scraping cadence (weekly sweep of top-performing reels and story patterns).
- Pattern-extraction rules: capture the *structure* and *authority signal*, never the surface language or the lifestyle frame.
- Storage: a `wiki/content-engine/ig-pattern-log.md` (pending) that mirrors [[synthesis/vsl-pattern-lab]] for IG.
- Integration with the Marcus five-wound diagnostic from [[people/marcus-avatar]] — every captured pattern gets tagged with which wound it speaks to.

## How Pulsify uses this

This workstream is owned by founder execution + agent drafting. The agent generates story sequence scripts, reel hooks, caption drafts, and maintains the operator scraping log. Founder records on phone, edits lightly, ships.

The workstream is currently sequenced *after* the Phase 1 funnel deliverables in [[synthesis/pulsify-90-day-plan]]. It activates in Phase 2 (Days 15–45) once VSL, Typeform, Cal.com, and the hit list are live and outreach is generating call traffic. Posting on a starved profile burns ideas; posting on a profile that DMs are funneling traffic into compounds.

- Exact posting frequencies are hypotheses, not measured. First 30 days of cadence are an experiment, not a rule.

- Reel-from-long-form repurposing workflow is undocumented.
- Live strategy is undefined.
