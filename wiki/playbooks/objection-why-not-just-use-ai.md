# "Why not just use ChatGPT or Claude and do it myself?"

Use it before they say it. In 2026 every prospect has thought it, so the pre-empt goes into every sales call, the VSL and the follow-up sequence. If you wait for them to raise it, they have already half-decided.

## The core idea (memorise this, the scripts are just versions of it)

AI is an engine. It does not know where to drive.

ChatGPT gives you the average of the whole internet. The average creator in trading or ecom makes nothing from their content, so the average answer gets you the average result. What we bring is what AI cannot give you: what has actually worked for creators like you, which option to pick out of the 50 it spits out, and someone who does the work and answers for the result.

We use AI every day. That is why we move fast. The difference is what we feed it and what we do with what comes out.

## Script 1: the pre-empt (sales call, before price)

> "Quick thing before we go further. A lot of people at this point think, why don't I just do this with ChatGPT. Fair question, and honestly you could try.
>
> Here's what happens though. You ask it for video titles, it gives you 50. Which one do you post? It doesn't know. It's never seen what actually gets a trading creator paid, it's seen the whole internet, and most of the internet makes zero from content.
>
> We use AI every single day, that's why we're fast. But we feed it thousands of hours of my own work, over a year of it, some days 14 hours straight: what's worked across 1,490 creators in your space, 225 playbooks we've written, and real client numbers. Nick went from 10-20k a month to 50k. The titles we packaged for him did 6 times his normal views and they're still his best videos 10 months later.
>
> ChatGPT can't pick the winner. We can. And we don't hand you a doc and leave, we build it, run it, and get paid when you get paid."

## Script 2: they say it directly

> "Honestly, you could. The tools are free. So why haven't you yet?"

Then stop and let them answer. Nearly always it is one of three things, and each one is the sale:

- **"I haven't had time."** → "Right. AI makes the writing faster, it doesn't post, follow up, track who came in or close the call. That's the part that eats your time, and that's what we take off you."
- **"I tried, it didn't really work."** → "Because it's guessing. It's giving you what works on average, and you're not average, you're in trading. We've already done the guessing for you."
- **"I don't know where to start."** → "That's the whole thing. AI answers questions. It doesn't tell you which questions to ask. Starting from zero with a blank chat is months of trial and error. We start you where our clients are now."

## Script 3: the maths (when it is really about the $5k)

> "Look at it this way. Doing it yourself costs you zero dollars and months of figuring out what we already know. What's a month of your content not selling worth to you right now?
>
> We charge $5k plus 30% of what we bring in after you join. The 30% means we only make real money if you do. If ChatGPT could get you there, you'd be paying for nothing. We're betting it can't, and we're putting our cut on it."

Never move off the upfront fee here. If the "I'll use AI" line comes back after this, it is a price smokescreen ([[feedback_price_objection_smokescreen]]). Ask straight: "If it wasn't about the money, would you start today?"

## Script 4: short DM version

> "you could 100% try it with chatgpt. the thing is it'll give you 50 ideas and no clue which one actually sells in trading. that's the bit we've already figured out across 1,490 creators. nick went from 10-20k to 50k/mo doing it this way"

## Script 5: for people who barely use AI

Some prospects have never touched it. Do not lecture them. Flip it:

> "You don't need to learn any of this. We already use the AI tools, we already have the playbooks, you just make the content you're good at and we handle the rest."

## Do not

- Do not trash AI. They use it, so you sound out of date. We are the people who use it best.
- Do not claim a custom-trained Pulsify AI. The app's own AI layer is still being built ([[project_brain_in_app_vision_2026_10_15]]).
- Do not name our private sources (creators, courses we studied). Say "what's worked across creators in your space".
- Do not drop the upfront fee to win the argument.
