Niche content (trading / Amazon FBA / dropshipping-ecom / TikTok shop) is about the **money-making CRAFT ITSELF** — the strategies, the products, the setups, how they made the money, the obstacles they beat. A trader makes content about trading. An FBA seller makes content about how they made money on Amazon. A dropshipper makes content about winning products and ad strategies. THAT is the hook.

Two separate mistakes the user blew up over, in order:
1. (turn 1) Operator/B2B language — "growth operating", "don't start an agency", "$9k/mo VA team", "coaching backend". Attracts the OUT crowd (operators/SMMA/agency/closers/setters). Wrong.
2. (turn 2, the worse one) Meta-content about content/funnels/selling — "the content system that turns trade recaps into booked calls", "your fba course isn't selling because your funnel is broken", "going viral won't sell your tiktok shop course". The user was furious: "why would a trader teach content creation? what does that have to do with funnels?" The money-making craft IS the content. NEVER the meta-layer.

**Why:** these coaches' whole identity/content is their craft. Meta-content (content systems, funnels, selling-your-course) is invisible noise to them and to their audience.

**How to apply:**
- RIGHT: `the winning product that did $84k in 30 days`, `the price action setup that got me funded in 9 days`, `how i made $38k with one fba product`. Craft, strategy, products, real numbers.
- WRONG: anything about content systems, repurposing, funnels, "selling your course", booked calls, backends.
- Figures are illustrative templates — swap in the real creator's numbers. Only confirmed real client: **Nick, trading**, $10–20k→$50k/mo. Don't publish fabricated specific results.
- **Two title tiers, like the queries (play both worlds):** BROAD/TOF = milestone or numberless framing (`$0→$10k`, `first $1k`, `0 to six figures`, `how to actually make money with X`) — wide reach, maps onto tons of channels. SPECIFIC/proof = pinpoint odd numbers (`$38k`, `$27k`) — credible but one data point. A pinpoint-only library only speaks to a sliver of the niche. Always build both.
