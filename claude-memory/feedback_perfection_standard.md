When I call something a "small tweak still pending" and show it anyway, that is a failure. Founder: "not the work ethic and standard at what we do... we cannot go soft... perfected and not slack by one bit." The design procedure is going into the app for clients.
**Why:** the story-sequence design process (photo handling, subject matte, logo placement, palette) will be offloaded to the app and run for clients, so the standard is the product.
**How to apply:** before showing any visual, open full-res crops of subject edges, frame top and bottom, and every text/graphic contact; fix every flaw I can name; only then show. Save what was learned to wiki/playbooks/story-sequence-design-procedure.md the same session. [[project-saas-story-launch]]
