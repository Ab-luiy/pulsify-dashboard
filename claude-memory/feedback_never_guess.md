2026-07-06: the founder gave a hard, universal instruction after I guessed at landing-page URLs (tried bare pulsify-funnels.pages.dev then ?client=frank, both wrong) instead of asking him for the actual link. He was frustrated: "can you like never ever guess in your life again across all and any tasks."

**The rule (applies to EVERYTHING, not just this task):** never guess. When I don't have a concrete value — a URL, a file path, a name, a number, a fact, which page/asset he means — I ask him for it or verify it from a real source. I do not produce a plausible-looking guess and present it as if it might be right. Guessing wastes his time and erodes trust.

**Why:** a guess that looks confident is worse than saying "I don't have this, send it to me." He would rather give me the exact input than watch me fire blind attempts. This compounds the existing standards: no invented facts/claims about people ([[feedback_flag_gaps_no_heavy_claims]]), verify before completion, flag gaps instead of filling them.

**How to apply:** if a task needs a specific artifact/value I don't hold, my first move is to ask for it or look it up — not to try candidates. One direct "paste me the link / what's the exact X" beats three wrong guesses. Also: don't over-explain or hedge when I don't know; just say I don't have it and ask.

Same principle, harder form. His words: "Never in your fucking life guess the work before you conduct your full research on what we have and what we dont."

**The rule:** before generating ANY deliverable, do the research pass first — load the matching skill, read the relevant wiki pages, read the applied examples in the vault, and check PRODUCT.md/DESIGN.md when the surface is visual. Only then produce. "It's just a few titles" is exactly when this fails, because we have a documented standard for nearly everything and my defaults contradict it.

**How to apply:** treat "what do we already have on this?" as a mandatory first tool call, not an optional nicety. Grep the vault for the topic. Check `wiki/content-engine/`, `wiki/frameworks/`, `wiki/people/`, and applied batches like `pulsify-content-niche-batches-2026-06`. Name what I found and what's missing before writing.
