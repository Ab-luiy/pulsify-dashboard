When I lack the solid source info a task really needs, **flag it and ask — do not fill the gap by deriving/assuming and then presenting it as authoritative.**

**Why:** On the IG tab (2026-06-14) I built a prescriptive "Action Engine" with heavy claims (cadence standards, profile-funnel rules) by deriving them from the **long-form / YouTube** brain and applying them to Instagram. The founder flagged this: validated **short-form IG** strategy doesn't exist in the brain yet, so those claims are premature. I should have flagged the missing short-form grounding instead of shipping confident prescriptions.

**How to apply:** Before making prescriptive/benchmark claims, check whether I actually have validated source material for *that specific domain*. If not, say "I don't have X — here's what I'd need" rather than extrapolating from an adjacent domain and labeling it as standard. Neutral observations are fine; authoritative "you should do X because that's the standard" needs real grounding. See [[project_shortform_ig]], [[feedback_speed_is_the_motto]] (speed ≠ fabricating confidence).

## 2026-09-12: Framework fidelity; no unsupported strategic exceptions

Founder correction during outreach planning: "We're following frameworks of Niksetting and Soo Wei" and "You're only allowed to work based on Data and information you have." Trigger: Codex said travel, "Me," and results highlights were not inherently wrong and personal content could belong there, without tying that assessment to the adopted framework and its required structure. The founder rejects this kind of unsupported leniency in a competitive market.

Standing rule for future Pulsify work:

- Apply the documented Niksetting/SooWei frameworks and the founder's explicit adopted rules. Do not substitute personal taste, generic marketing opinion, or plausible exceptions for the specified system.
- Before judging a profile, content format, bio, highlight rail, funnel, VSL or outreach asset, identify the relevant source rule and compare the observed item against it. State which requirement is met or missing.
- Do not soften a deviation with "that can work too" unless a documented exception, explicit founder decision, or relevant measured evidence supports it. If support is missing, say so; do not improvise permission.
- Distinguish founder operating rules, attributed source teachings, measured Pulsify results, and untested proposals. A framework requirement is not proof of a prospect's conversion performance. Never fabricate data or claim both creators teach a rule that only one source establishes.
- When sources or founder decisions conflict, surface the exact conflict and respect the latest applicable explicit decision. Do not silently erase documented distinctions to manufacture agreement or universal bans.
- Source-defined flexibility must be attributed precisely, not expanded into general leniency. The canonical story-sequence page specifies a first start-here asset, proof/mechanism/apply functions and naming flexibility; it separately specifies personal-story content. Do not conflate story types with an unstructured permanent highlight rail.

References: wiki/synthesis/cross-creator-synthesis.md; wiki/frameworks/story-sequence-system.md; wiki/concepts/profile-funnel.md. These pages must be read fully for relevant work. This instruction reinforces feedback_never_guess.md and applies to future strategy, reviews, copy and implementation decisions. It does not convert prior generic drafts into validated frameworks.
