Founder directive 2026-08-02, during the `_acetrades_` build: every prospect value asset now closes with a **horizon note**. One short paragraph that names the belief the creator has not voiced yet (you do not have the followers you want), reframes the constraint as foundation rather than volume, and sets the time expectation honestly (once the foundation is right, compounding takes time). No promised timeline or numbers, no flattery, no strings, no ask.

**Why:** the creators Pulsify targets sit roughly between 500 and 5,000 followers and almost all of them read the follower count as the reason nothing converts. That wrong diagnosis is what makes them quit a working system three weeks in. Naming it inside the asset pre-empts the objection before it kills the engagement. It also fills the space at the close where a CTA is banned, so the document can end warm without becoming a pitch.

**How to apply:** it is structure part 9 in [[project-prospect-asset-folders]]'s doc standard (`wiki/playbooks/prospect-asset-doc-standard.md`, section "The horizon note"), and a required item in `wiki/playbooks/prospect-asset-library.md`. Anchor it in real evidence from their own account where possible, audience quality being the strongest anchor, because that turns encouragement into an observation. Same session recorded five unratified upgrades to the standard: page one stands alone, falsifiability test on the finding, name what we left alone, send behind the live `/a/{token}` tracked link, outcome columns in the build log.
