## The packaging case study, founder-confirmed 2026-08-13

Channel is at **764 subscribers** now. Founder says it was around 200 when this work was done. Thumbnails saved to `Desktop\Prospect Assets\niicksfx\`.

**The three videos Pulsify packaged**, confirmed by the founder from screenshots, all 10-11 months old:

| ID | Title | Views | Thumbnail |
|---|---|---|---|
| `3QDKzi4nFl8` | watch us breakdown our 6 figure trading systems live. (step-by-step) | **814** | "TRADING IS THIS EASY" on a yellow highlight bar, two men at a desk, arrows labelling each "6-FIGURE TRADER" |
| `KnbqLjGzD3k` | trading mentor exposes trading mentorships | **737** | Two men at a desk with speech bubbles: "Is a mentorship really important to succeed in trading?" / "Do you really need a mentor?" |
| `enVq8W8N6mU` | my $30k/mo secret that stopped me from blowing accounts (step by step) | **314** | Sunglasses, beside a red blueprint diagram "STOP BLOWING FUNDED ACCOUNTS / Full Blueprint" with six named boxes |

**The before.** Uploads immediately preceding them (the "1 year ago" block) ran 68, 70, 114, 119, 122, 125, 141, 204, 238, 351, 416. Median roughly 125.

**The honest read.** 814 against a ~125 median is about 6.5x, and the top two are *still* the channel's best videos 10 months later. But views settled back after that batch (53, 113, 157 followed) before climbing again, so the packaging spiked those specific uploads rather than permanently resetting the channel. Say it that way. Do not claim a permanent transformation.

**What makes all three work, and it generalises:** something concrete and legible carries the claim inside the frame. Annotation and arrows, a speech bubble posing the viewer's real question, a named blueprint. Not a single styled word.

This matters because it sharpens the bergtrades thesis. Berg's winners had prop-firm certificates; Nick's had arrows, bubbles and a diagram. The shared principle is not "certificates," it is **a legible artifact in the frame instead of one big word.**
