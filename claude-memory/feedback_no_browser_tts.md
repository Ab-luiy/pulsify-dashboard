**Why:** the free browser speech voice sounded bad and he judged the feature not worth building at all.

**How to apply:** do not add read-aloud or text-to-speech anywhere in the app with the browser's voice. Only reconsider with ElevenLabs, and only if he asks. Speech-to-text (the mic that types for them) stays. Related: [[project_brain_in_app_vision_2026_10_15]].
