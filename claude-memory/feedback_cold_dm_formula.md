2026-07-06: the founder built and pressure-tested (full iteration pass) the standard cold-DM voice and had me codify it at [[content-engine/cold-dm-formula]] so it auto-generates on command. It SUPERSEDES the old diagnostic DM voice in [[playbooks/pulse-system-outreach]] Step 4 (which banned casual openers/compliments).

**The formula (five beats):** 1) casual lowercase opener ("yo been deep in your vids this week"), 2) one genuine specific reference to something they actually teach, dropped in the MIDDLE not the opener, plus a plain "really like it" (never glaze), 3) soft gap framed as opportunity not flaw ("also noticed you don't really have a page that captures people and educates them on what you can help with"), 4) the gift ("so i just went ahead and put one together for you"), 5) ownership + soft reply-inviting close ("it's 100% yours, take a look and lmk what you think [link]").

**Why:** the posture is high-authority peer doing them a favor, zero ego, zero begging — I noticed something, built you something, it's yours. The first 3-4 words are all the IG request preview shows, so they must read like a real person, not a pitch. The old audit voice triggered sales resistance.

**Cold email variant (added 2026-07-06):** same voice + five beats, plus a subject line and a lowercase-first-name sign-off; body is the DM with line breaks. Subject has two lanes — curiosity ("made you something") or a customizable witty situation-metaphor: shape = [their impressive result/model] + [missing/broken container], e.g. "million dollar model, no front door", "ferrari engine, bicycle brakes", "you built the engine, forgot the keys", "great model, nowhere to send people" (founder-approved). One-joke rule: witty subject pairs with a straight body open, or curiosity subject + one joke in body — never two jokes. No cold-email asset existed before this (Ben Bader = warm nurture, hammer-them = post-booking). DEPLOY RISK is deliverability not copy: bare fresh-domain link + no SPF/DKIM/DMARC spam-filters on batch sends; single hand-sent from a normal inbox is fine.

**How to apply:** lowercase casual frat-adjacent (yo/bro/ngl fine, not street/hood); NO em-dashes, no emojis, no exclamation marks; reference in the middle never the opener (never open with a quote of their words); one specific detail max; NEVER name the gap as a flaw or hit ego (no "your IG is dead"); drop the link but still invite a reply so they don't lurk (founder chose link-in-first-msg for IG); stay safe-generic on their setup unless a fact is confirmed; trim to 30-45 words; vary opener + reaction verb across a batch. Grading: base ~85, real least-obvious reference gets to 95, generic placeholder caps ~85. Founder vets/picks the lead himself then hands me the creator + a transcript; I pull the reference and produce a 95. Frank worked example uses his break-even/let-the-MRR-carry-it take. Ben Bader is the wit reference the founder relates to but heavy wit read "too generated" for cold DMs — wit is light seasoning, not the structure.
