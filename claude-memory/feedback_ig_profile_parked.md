The Instagram profile itself (bio copy, highlight cover redesign, grid aesthetic, brand photography) is parked. Founder said on 2026-05-11: "the Instagram it's already done well there's nothing much I can do until I grow and get money and start taking better pictures."

**Why:** Visual polish at this stage is wasted effort without growth and budget. Better photography and identity work are gated on a paying client closing first. Pushing on profile aesthetic burns time that should go to content/growth and the funnel plumbing.

**How to apply:**
- Do not recommend IG bio rewrites, highlight cover redesigns, grid overhauls, or photo shoots as priority work.
- The active IG workstream is content/growth: story sequences, highlight *kinds* (categorization, not design), reels, posting frequency, topic discipline, scraping operators.
- Full scope lives in [wiki/playbooks/instagram-growth-workstream.md](wiki/playbooks/instagram-growth-workstream.md).
- Un-park trigger: a paying client closes and cash exists. Then revisit visual identity.
