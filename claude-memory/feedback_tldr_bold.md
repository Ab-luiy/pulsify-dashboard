After ANY response with numbered steps, multiple sections, or 1) 2) 3) structure,
finish with a **bold TLDR** line (or two) stating ONLY what the user needs to do on
their end, or my suggestion/recommendation. Keep the detail above it for reference,
but the bold TLDR is the part they actually read.

**Why:** the user genuinely gets lost in long, paragraph-heavy replies — it causes real
friction when all they want is the next action. They'll scroll up to the detail only if
they need to double-check.

**How to apply:** keep necessary detail but always cap a structured reply with a concise
**bold TLDR** at the END — what's needed from them or what I recommend. Don't bury the
ask. Lean shorter overall. Relates to [[feedback_speed_is_the_motto]].
