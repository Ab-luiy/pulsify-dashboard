## The 7 frames

Each frame: one idea, one line of text, 4–6 seconds. Read top to bottom it should feel like
one argument, not seven posts.

### 1 — Hook / pattern interrupt
- **On screen:** "You checked my profile. Good. Here's the 60-second version."
- **Why:** names what they're doing. Kills the "who is this random DM" reflex instantly.
- **Format:** talking head, no edit, straight to camera.

### 2 — Who I am + what I do
- **On screen:** "I'm AB. I build content systems for coaches and consultants that turn
  views into booked calls."
- **Why:** positioning before proof. They need the category before the number means anything.
- **Format:** talking head.

### 3 — Proof #1 (the money)
- **On screen:** "$94,000 generated with a trading coach." + subline "YouTube content system"
- **Why:** biggest number we own. Leads the proof block.
- **Format:** designed frame, big number, dark red on black. Screenshot underneath if we have one.

### 4 — Proof #2 (the growth)
- **On screen:** "200k → 500k followers for a fitness creator." + subline "and a high-ticket
  offer sold off the back of it"
- **Why:** second modality — proves it isn't a one-niche fluke.
- **Format:** designed frame, same template as Frame 3. Consistency = system, not luck.

### 5 — The mechanism
- **On screen:** 3 steps, one line each:
  1. Find the outlier — what's already working in your market
  2. Build the exceptional video — angle, story, unique mechanism
  3. Funnel it — 3 CTAs on every video, calls tracked back to source
- **Why:** turns proof into *repeatable*. This is the "how", and it's what separates us from
  everyone posting screenshots.
- **Format:** designed frame, 3 stacked rows. Could be 3 separate frames if it feels cramped.

### 6 — Who it's for / who it's not for
- **On screen:** "For coaches and consultants already selling. / Not for you if you've got no
  offer yet."
- **Why:** disqualification builds more trust than a pitch. Also filters the call calendar.
- **Format:** talking head.

### 7 — CTA
- **On screen:** "If that sounds like your problem — reply to my DM, or the link's in bio."
- **Why:** one action, low friction. They're already in the DM thread.
- **Format:** talking head + sticker/link.

## Format rules

- **Frames 1, 2, 6, 7 = talking head.** Face builds trust; designed frames build credibility.
  Alternating keeps it from feeling like a slideshow ad.
- **Frames 3, 4, 5 = designed.** Same template, same type scale, dark red / black per brand.
- Captions burned in on every frame — most views are muted.
- **Do NOT show own-channel stats.** 19 YouTube subs and 519 IG followers undercut the $94k.
  Client results only. This is the single most important rule in the file.
- Keep it to 7. Highlights lose people after ~8 frames.

1. Clear the pre-flight blockers above.
2. Write the 4 talking-head scripts (frames 1, 2, 6, 7) — 4–6s each, one take, no teleprompter feel.
3. Design frames 3, 4, 5 in the Pulsify template.
4. Film all 4 talking heads in one sitting, same shirt, same light.
5. Post to story in order 1 → 7, same day, then save to a highlight named **"Start here"**.
6. Screenshot the finished highlight into `assets/` as the version of record.

## Keeping it current

This is the part that kills these highlights. Numbers rot, the highlight stays up, and a
prospect sees a stale claim.

- **Monthly check** — reread every frame against `WINS.md`. Any number that moved gets refilmed.
- **On every new win** — add the row to `WINS.md` first, then decide if it replaces a proof frame.
  Proof frames cap at 2. A new win must beat an existing one to get in.
- **On any offer change** — frames 2, 5, 6 get rewritten same week.
