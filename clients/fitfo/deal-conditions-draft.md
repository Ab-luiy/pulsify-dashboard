# FITFO deal conditions, working draft

Carlos asked for this at 19:30 on 2026-07-28: "Let's write down the conditions so we're clear with everything." He asked again, more directly, on the 2026-07-29 call: "Send me everything that we have agreed upon... send a contract... Once we both agree on that, we can both sign on it." This draft is still not sendable. Two things now need Abdullah's decision, marked `[DECIDE]` below, down from six after the 07-29 calls resolved the rest.

The point of writing it down is not formality. A performance deal with no defined trigger and no time limit is the version that ends in a disagreement neither side wanted, and Carlos flagged the same instinct himself when he said he would need it in a contract for both of their safeties.

## What is already agreed

Both sides said these out loud in the thread. They go in as written.

- Nothing is paid up front.
- The first $3,000 of revenue Carlos generates goes entirely to Abdullah.
- Carlos takes nothing from that first $3,000.
- The arrangement continues past that point on the same system.
- Neither side is in the more favourable position if it does not work. Carlos's framing, and the reason he proposed this structure.

## What has to be decided before this can be sent

**1. ~~`[DECIDE]` What "times 1.5" multiplies.~~ RESOLVED 2026-07-28.** It multiplies the original $2,000 quote. $2,000 x 1.5 = $3,000, which is exactly the figure Carlos named. He was being precise rather than vague. Nothing further to decide, but the base should be written out explicitly in the final version so it cannot be reread later.

**2. `[DECIDE]` What counts as revenue. STILL OPEN after the 07-29 call.** Cash collected or contracted value. Gross or net of refunds, chargebacks, and payment-plan defaults. If a client signs a $3,000 plan and pays $1,000 before dropping off, the answer has to already exist.

Carlos's worked example on 07-29 was cash in his pocket ("two seats at $2,000 each. That means $4,000 in my pocket"), which implies collected rather than contracted. He never stated it as a rule and refunds were never mentioned. Write it as cash collected, net of refunds and chargebacks, and it matches how he already describes it.

**3. ~~`[DECIDE]` What triggers payment, and the base.~~ FULLY RESOLVED 2026-07-29.** The trigger is revenue collected, and Pulsify is paid first out of the earliest money in. Carlos, on call 1, unprompted: "There's not a single dollar that is going to my wallet until your 3K has been paid off." No delivery milestone carries any payment.

The base ambiguity flagged after call 1 (first-dollars versus "$3k out of the first $20k," per Fathom's garbled AI summary) is closed. On call 2, at 31:12, Carlos restated the deal in his own words: "we sign on the 7030 and then the 3k upfront before I get any money. That's been agreed upon." Abdullah: "Perfect." Both parties have now independently confirmed first-dollars priority. The $20k reading never reflected what either side meant and should not appear in the agreement.

Recommended wording, now confirmed rather than merely recommended: the first $3,000 of cash collected goes to Pulsify before Carlos draws anything, and the 70/30 split applies to every dollar collected after that $3,000.

The build still stays fully on spec until Carlos has an offer that closes. That exposure did not close, it moved into item 4.

**4. `[DECIDE]` Termination and exit terms. NOW THE SINGLE LARGEST OPEN ITEM, raised directly by Carlos on the 07-29 call and never answered.**

Carlos asked outright, call 2, 29:02: "I need to also understand what are termination clauses in the case that this simply just does is not working for either of us... What are the termination clauses in that case?" He named a concrete trigger: three months in, results nowhere near what he expects, he walks.

He then specified three things he does not want, at 31:21, and they compound into an unbounded exposure for Pulsify if left as stated:

- He wants to **keep the systems** on exit.
- He does not want the contract to be **time-bound**.
- He does not want to be **bound to pay the $3,000** as a condition of leaving: "I don't want it to be money binded either... what I don't want is for you to say, oh, no, we're going to work until you give me my money back."

Abdullah's answer was a story about how the Nick engagement ended (Nick kept the systems, work stopped, no hard feelings) plus an appeal to good faith. That is not a clause. Carlos is still waiting on one, and he explicitly told Abdullah he expects a contract "set in stone with signatures... not just word of mouth."

**This item absorbs the old "time limit" question and is bigger than it was.** Combined with the call-1 scope expansion (portal, Slack, calendar, bio audit, YouTube structure, payment-processor setup), Pulsify now holds: zero upfront, no time floor from Carlos, no lapse date, an expanding build, and a counterparty on record asking not to be bound to the only payment in the deal. Every category of risk sits on one side.

**The fix does not require refusing what Carlos asked for.** He objected to being compelled to keep working until Pulsify is paid back, not to owing money he actually collects. Recommended structure: the $3,000 obligation survives termination on revenue Carlos collects within a defined window after exit (his idea of a launch timeframe, not indefinite); if he never launches or collects nothing in that window, he owes nothing and keeps the systems, matching what he asked for. That satisfies all three of his stated conditions while still closing the hole.

> ⚠️ Pick the window number carefully. Carlos said both "I don't need anything from here to a year from now" (patience) and, in the same call, gave three months as his own walk-away scenario. Those cannot both be the operative number. Recommend using his three-month figure as the earliest review point and stating explicitly what happens at that point, rather than defaulting to his year-long patience by omission.

**5. ~~`[DECIDE]` Get the 30% onto the record.~~ RESOLVED 2026-07-29.** Abdullah recapped it and asked "Afterwards, 7030 applies. We cool?" Carlos: "Yeah, makes sense." Abdullah then said he would put it in writing. Carlos separately asked for the recording, so once it is sent he has received the record himself.

The 5/10/15 internal split is also on the record, and Carlos demonstrated he understands it by working the math out loud and correcting his own misreading. Still write the breakdown into the agreement, but the evidentiary hole is closed.

Founder confirmation 2026-07-28: the 30% split survives the restructure at 5% setter, 10% closer, 15% Abdullah. The $3,000 is a first tranche, not a ceiling. On that basis the economics barely moved:

| | 07-24 structure | Current structure |
|---|---|---|
| Guaranteed up front | $2,000 | $0 |
| On the first $3,000 of revenue | 30% ($900) plus the $2,000 = $2,900 | 100% = $3,000 |
| Everything after | 30% | 30% |

Against revenue these are the same deal. The whole delta is $2,000 moving from certain to contingent, which makes the build fee in decision 3 the only economic argument left rather than one of several.

**The real exposure here is evidentiary, not commercial.** The 30% appears nowhere in the WhatsApp thread. Carlos's only words on continuation are that they "continue working together with the same system making more and more money from more clients." No percentage, no split, no mechanism. It is agreed on the call and nowhere else.

That leaves it open to an honest misunderstanding rather than bad faith: once the $3,000 clears, Carlos can reasonably read the arrangement as returning to open terms. Writing the split out, with the 5/10/15 breakdown and what revenue it applies to, is the highest-value line in this document.

**6. `[DECIDE]` Scope of the build. MOSTLY RESOLVED 2026-07-29 on the boundaries. Still open on the ceiling.**

Carlos set the boundaries himself and Abdullah agreed to both:

- **Instagram is his.** No scripted reels, his frequency, his voice, his call on what gets posted. Pulsify gets profile picture, bio, visuals, suggestions, and a veto on content actively harming the brand (Carlos granted that veto unprompted).
- **YouTube is Pulsify's to structure.** Titles, topics, length caps, required beats. Carlos records.

What is now in scope by agreement: YouTube structure and packaging, IG bio audit, grid direction, client portal, Slack workspace with daily briefing, shared calendar, WAP/Fanbases payment setup, and the funnel once a platform proves out.

What is still undefined is the ceiling. There is no cap on revisions, no cap on portal build-out, no stated volume of YouTube packaging per month, and no cadence Carlos owes in return. On a $0-upfront deal that is the part that bites. Write the deliverable volumes as numbers.

## Structure to fill in once those are answered

**Parties and dates.** Names, start date, review date.

**What Abdullah delivers.** The systems build, itemised. Content structure, ideation and consistency layer, backend. Explicitly out of scope: anything not listed.

**What Carlos delivers.** Partly established on the 07-29 calls: he keeps a full-time job and gets priority when it conflicts, he recovers final say over what he actually records and posts, and he committed to radical honesty and no passive-aggression. What is still missing is anything numeric: no stated posting cadence, no turnaround time on YouTube recording, no defined availability window for calls. A performance deal makes his execution the payment trigger, so this still needs numbers, not just tone.

**Payment.** Fully resolved by decision 3: $3,000 of cash collected to Pulsify before Carlos draws anything, then 70/30 on everything after. Revenue definition (item 2) still needs writing as an explicit clause even though his own examples imply cash collected.

**After the first $3,000.** Per decision 5. Resolved and confirmed twice, on the WhatsApp thread and again verbally on the 07-29 call.

**Time limit and exit.** Per decision 4. Now the most important open section in the whole document: what each side keeps if it ends, whether the $3,000 obligation survives an exit, and what window governs that survival.

**Proof obligations.** Carlos asked for accounts backing the fitness-creator and trader-creator results on 07-24 and said he would check the handles and the YouTube breakdown. Still never confirmed as checked, including across both 07-29 calls.

## Where this actually stands, 2026-07-30

Both calls Carlos asked for have happened. He does not need another call before signing; he needs the document itself. His own words on call 2, verbatim: "Send me everything that we have agreed upon... send a contract... Once we both agree on that, we can both sign on it and start working from there."

Two things stand between here and a sendable contract:

A performance-only variant has to be built before anything gets sent, and it should not go out with the jurisdiction and signer placeholders the v1 template still carries unresolved.

Carlos intends to redline whatever arrives and asked clarifying questions before signing, so a draft with an honest gap flagged in it is safer to send than a polished one that quietly omits the termination clause he already asked for by name.
