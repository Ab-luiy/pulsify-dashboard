# FITFO deal conditions, working draft

Carlos asked for this at 19:30: "Let's write down the conditions so we're clear with everything." This is the draft to work from. It is not sendable yet. Six things need Abdullah's decision first, marked `[DECIDE]` below.

The point of writing it down is not formality. A performance deal with no defined trigger and no time limit is the version that ends in a disagreement neither side wanted, and Carlos flagged the same instinct himself when he said he would need it in a contract for both of their safeties.

## What is already agreed

Both sides said these out loud in the thread. They go in as written.

- Nothing is paid up front.
- The first $3,000 of revenue Carlos generates goes entirely to Abdullah.
- Carlos takes nothing from that first $3,000.
- The arrangement continues past that point on the same system.
- Neither side is in the more favourable position if it does not work. Carlos's framing, and the reason he proposed this structure.

## What has to be decided before this can be sent

**1. `[DECIDE]` What "times 1.5" multiplies.** Carlos said "not a dollar to me until you get yours back times 1.5." That number has no base attached to it. If it means 1.5x the $2,000 originally quoted, the first tranche is $3,000 and the maths already lines up with the $3,000 he named. If it means something else, it needs saying now, because Carlos will read it his way otherwise.

**2. `[DECIDE]` What counts as revenue.** Cash collected or contracted value. Gross or net of refunds, chargebacks, and payment-plan defaults. If a client signs a $3,000 plan and pays $1,000 before dropping off, the answer has to already exist.

**3. `[DECIDE]` What triggers payment.** This is the one raised at 18:04 and never answered. Three options were named:
   - On delivery of the systems, regardless of launch outcome.
   - On launch.
   - On metrics other than revenue.

   Carlos's answer defined it purely as revenue. That is the version that leaves the build fully on spec. Worth deciding whether a delivery milestone carries any payment at all, because as written the trigger depends on Carlos having an offer, and he does not have one yet.

**4. `[DECIDE]` A time limit.** If no offer has launched by a given date, what happens. Options: the arrangement lapses, it converts to a fee, or it continues indefinitely. Right now it is indefinite by default, which is the exposure.

**5. `[DECIDE]` What happens after the first $3,000.** Both sides assume continuation. Nobody has said on what terms. The obvious candidate is the 30% split from the 07-24 structure, at 5% setter, 10% closer, 15% Abdullah, but that was attached to a deal that no longer exists.

**6. `[DECIDE]` Scope of the build.** What Carlos gets for the $0 up front, and what he does not. Without this, scope creep is unbounded and there is no upfront payment sitting there to justify drawing a line.

## Structure to fill in once those are answered

**Parties and dates.** Names, start date, review date.

**What Abdullah delivers.** The systems build, itemised. Content structure, ideation and consistency layer, backend. Explicitly out of scope: anything not listed.

**What Carlos delivers.** This one matters and has not been discussed at all. A performance deal makes Carlos's execution the payment trigger, so his obligations are as load-bearing as the build. Posting cadence, turnaround on assets, availability for calls, and the decision to actually launch.

**Payment.** The first $3,000 as agreed, with the base for 1.5x named, revenue defined, and the trigger stated.

**After the first $3,000.** Per decision 5.

**Time limit and exit.** Per decision 4. What each side keeps if it ends: assets, access, accounts.

**Proof obligations.** Carlos asked for accounts backing the fitness-creator and trader-creator results and said he would check the handles and the YouTube breakdown. Confirm he has actually looked before the call, because an unverified proof claim sitting in the background of a signed agreement is the thing that unravels later.
