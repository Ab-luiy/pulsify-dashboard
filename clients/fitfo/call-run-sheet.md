# FITFO conditions call, run sheet

For the call where the documentation gets written and the gameplan gets set. Carlos asked for this at 19:30 on 07-27 and has been waiting since.

## Decide these before dialling, not on the call

Carlos has moved the terms twice already. Both times it happened because a number was open at the moment he asked about it. Walk in with these fixed and the call becomes confirmation rather than round three.

- **The build fee number.** Pick it. It is credited against the first $3,000, so Carlos is out nothing if the work lands.
- **The trigger.** Delivery, launch, or metrics. Pick one.
- **The time limit.** A date after which the arrangement lapses or converts.
- **Build scope.** What is in, what is explicitly out.

## Order to run it

Sequence is deliberate. The uncontested items bank first, obligations get established before the ask, and the fee lands as symmetry rather than as price.

**1. Close his open question first.** He asked for accounts backing the fitness-creator and trader-creator results and said he would check the handles and the video. Ask whether he did. Leaving it unanswered lets it resurface later as leverage exactly when the fee comes up.

**2. Read back what is already agreed and bank it.** Nothing up front, first $3,000 of revenue at 100%, then 30% at 5% setter, 10% closer, 15% Abdullah. Get an explicit yes on the 30% with the breakdown said out loud. This is the highest-value minute of the call and it is uncontroversial, which is exactly why it goes early. He has never seen a percentage in writing.

**3. Define revenue and the trigger.** Cash collected or contracted. Gross or net of refunds and payment-plan defaults. What happens if a $3,000 client pays $1,000 and disappears. Technical questions, not adversarial ones, and easier to settle while the tone is still collaborative.

**4. His obligations.** Posting cadence, turnaround on assets, availability, and the decision to actually launch. Frame it as what is needed from him to make the $3,000 happen. This has never been discussed and it is the step that makes the next one land.

**5. Then the build fee.** By this point the call has established that both sides carry obligations. Ask it as a symmetry correction rather than a price change, using his own principle: he proposed this structure so that neither side sits in the more favourable position, and as written he risks nothing while the full build is carried on spec. Credited against the first $3,000, so it costs him nothing if it works.

**6. Time limit and exit.** What each side keeps if it ends: assets, access, accounts.

## Gameplan half

Phase it. He has no offer, roughly 1,000 followers, six weeks of posting, and [[wiki/concepts/pulsify-order-of-operations]] puts content and offer ahead of funnel.

- **Phase 1, now.** Content structure, ideation and consistency layer, positioning. This is real, he needs it, and it is what he is ready for.
- **Phase 2, gated on an offer existing.** Offer construction and the unique mechanism.
- **Phase 3, gated on Phase 2.** Backend, funnel, sequences, sales infra.

Say the gates out loud on the call. Two reasons. It is what was already agreed on 07-24, that structure comes before launch and nothing gets rushed. And it protects the build from being delivered in full against a payment trigger that cannot fire until he has something to sell.

## Traps

- **Do not reopen price as price.** It gets reframed as scope and symmetry or it does not get raised.
- **Do not agree to deliver backend before an offer exists.** It is the single fastest way to do the whole build unpaid.
- **Do not leave "we continue working together" as the wording for the 30%.** That phrase is Carlos's, and it is the only thing he has ever seen in writing about what happens after the first $3,000.
- **Do not discover a number live.** Anything not decided beforehand gets decided at his pace.
