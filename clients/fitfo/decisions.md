## [2026-07-24] Deal structure: $2,000 up front, 30% after the first launch

**Decided by:** Abdullah, on the discovery call, agreed in principle by Carlos.

**The terms:**

- **$2,000 up front** for the systems build. Includes his own AI, the ideation and consistency layer, and the backend built out.
- **30% after the first launch only.** Not on current revenue, not on his trading, not on his job. Profits split 70/30 in his favour once there is a launch to split.
- **The 30% breaks down** as 5% to the setter, 10% to the closer, 15% to Abdullah. It funds the team that runs the launch rather than being a flat operator cut.
- **Price anchor used on the call:** Abdullah's usual fee is $10,000 with consultancy and a full team, cited against prior work with Mamba FX and associates. $2,000 was positioned as fitting Carlos's current stage.

**Why it matters:** the timing of the 30% is the part most likely to be misread. It is a launch-triggered profit split, not a revenue share starting now. The first version of the scope doc had this wrong and was corrected the same day.

> Note against [[playbooks/pulsify-offer-v1]]: the documented floor is $5,000 base + 30%. This deal is below that floor deliberately, because Carlos has no offer yet and the standard engagement assumes a working one. Treat as a stage-appropriate variant, not as a new floor.

## [2026-07-24] Structure before launch. No rush to a mentorship.

**Decided by:** Abdullah and Carlos jointly on the call.

Carlos asked how long until he can realistically launch and sustain a mentorship. Both agreed it cannot be built overnight. The sequence is to put the right structure and frameworks in place first, build authenticity, and launch from that base rather than launching early off a thin audience.

Abdullah also advised explicitly against building the funnel yet, preferring content and foundation first. This matches [[concepts/pulsify-order-of-operations]]: nothing downstream gets built until what is upstream exists.

**Why:** he is at roughly 1,000 Instagram followers, about six weeks into posting seriously. Launching into that is the failure mode, not the goal.

## [2026-07-24] Authenticity is the positioning spine

**Source:** Carlos, unprompted and repeatedly, on the call.

He stated he disagrees with people who sell a mentorship without trading, using his own past as a professional tennis player: a coach who never played is worthless. He does not want to be a BSer. He is also clear-eyed that mentorship earns more reliably than trading, and that trading is educated gambling that will never stop being that.

**Why it matters:** this is a direct match with Pulsify's [[concepts/trust-recession]] thesis and the proof-currency rule. He is not being asked to change how he presents. Everything built for him should lean on this rather than work around it, and it is the strongest available answer to his own hesitation about selling.

**Carlos's stated goal:** roughly 10,000 Instagram followers in six months.

**Abdullah's read:** 10,000 in six months is low if the method is right. 20,000 to 30,000 is achievable.

This gap is useful on the follow-up. His own target is conservative, and the honest version of future-pacing here is showing him what the ceiling actually looks like rather than agreeing with the floor he set.

## [2026-07-24] Objection: NOT price. Verified against the recording.

**Initial read (Abdullah, from recall):** "not ready to take this step" is a smokescreen over price, plus a need for more proof the method works.

> ⚠️ Corrected against the full recording. The objection is not price, and Carlos ruled it out explicitly when asked directly. Building the follow-up around price would miss him entirely, and would contradict something he stated plainly.

**What he actually said, in sequence:**

- Timing is his stated worry. He is unsure he has enough today to justify turning this into a business yet.
- He frames the real question as whether he is ready to give it everything: if he pays and works with Abdullah, he will not half-ass it, and he does not want to waste the money by being the reason it fails.
- Asked directly whether he was scared, he said money does not scare him. That closes the price theory.
- He describes a part of his brain going to what happens if it works far better than expected and his life changes completely. He calls it a yield sign rather than a stop sign.
- Asked why, he explains he met many successful people through professional tennis, understands how they think, and finds it hard to picture himself there because he has failed many times.
- His clearest formulation: what if he does not live up to his own expectation, and what if he crashes twice as hard as he rises.

**The actual objection: fear of success and self-belief, not cost and not doubt about the method.** He believes the work would help. He is unsure he is the person who can carry it, and he is wary of a version of his life he cannot picture.

**Why this matters for the follow-up.** Evidence that the method works does not answer this, because he already accepts the method. What answers it is lowering the identity stakes: this is a structure that runs whether or not he feels ready, the first 90 to 120 days are process rather than transformation, and failure at this scale costs him a defined amount rather than his self-image. Abdullah's own line on the call was the right instinct, that the money exists to make him take it seriously rather than because it matters.

**Also on the record:** he asks his father for advice, not his girlfriend or his friends, and he stressed the decision is his own. Any follow-up is written for him alone.
