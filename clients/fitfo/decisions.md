## [2026-07-24] Deal structure: $2,000 up front, 30% after the first launch

**Decided by:** Abdullah, on the discovery call, agreed in principle by Carlos.

**The terms:**

- **$2,000 up front** for the systems build. Includes his own AI, the ideation and consistency layer, and the backend built out.
- **30% after the first launch only.** Not on current revenue, not on his trading, not on his job. Profits split 70/30 in his favour once there is a launch to split.
- **The 30% breaks down** as 5% to the setter, 10% to the closer, 15% to Abdullah. It funds the team that runs the launch rather than being a flat operator cut.
- **Price anchor used on the call:** Abdullah's usual fee is $10,000 with consultancy and a full team, cited against prior work with Mamba FX and associates. $2,000 was positioned as fitting Carlos's current stage.

**Why it matters:** the timing of the 30% is the part most likely to be misread. It is a launch-triggered profit split, not a revenue share starting now. The first version of the scope doc had this wrong and was corrected the same day.

> Note against [[playbooks/pulsify-offer-v1]]: the documented floor is $5,000 base + 30%. This deal is below that floor deliberately, because Carlos has no offer yet and the standard engagement assumes a working one. Treat as a stage-appropriate variant, not as a new floor.
