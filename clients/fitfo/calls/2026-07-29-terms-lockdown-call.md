# FITFO terms lockdown call, 2026-07-29 (call 1 of 2)

Participants: **AB** (Abdullah, Pulsify) and **CARLOS** (FITFO, labelled `F1TFO` by Fathom from his Meet display name). Recorded via Fathom, 57 minutes. The Google Meet link expired mid-sentence at roughly 56:00 while Abdullah was starting the Instagram algorithm explanation, so the session continues in call 2. There is also a 15-minute dead stretch from 26:54 to 42:09 where Carlos stepped away for a work call.

## Three-bullet summary

- **The 30% is finally on the record.** Abdullah recapped the full structure at 25:5x and asked "Afterwards, 7030 applies. We cool?" Carlos answered "Yeah, makes sense" at 26:07. This closes the single biggest exposure flagged in [[clients/fitfo/deal-conditions-draft]], which was that the 30% existed only on a call and nowhere Carlos had seen.
- **Carlos tried to defer the split to a later negotiation and Abdullah refused.** Carlos proposed discussing "70/30, 80/20, 60/40, whatever makes more sense in that moment" once the $3,000 cleared. Abdullah's answer: "I prefer for everything to be locked down right now... I hate renegotiating things." Carlos accepted. That refusal is the most valuable thing Abdullah did on this call.
- **Carlos set two hard delivery terms and they are both compatible with Pulsify doctrine.** Instagram stays his: no scripted reels, he posts what he wants, and Abdullah gets visuals, bio, and suggestions only. YouTube is fully open to structure: topics, titles, length caps, and required beats, with Carlos recording it himself. This resolves the scope question in decision 6 of the conditions draft almost entirely.

## The terms as actually stated

Quoting the mechanics rather than paraphrasing, because this is the material that goes into the agreement.

**The upfront is gone and the $3,000 replaces it.** Carlos at 17:00: "2K upfront, gone. The 3K is just like a kind of like a, call it like an interest fee, like the extra $1,000 of like, as a thank you for not, you know, taking the down payment originally."

**Payment priority.** Carlos gave his own worked example at 17:38: two seats at $2,000 each is $4,000 collected, and "out of those 4K, 3K is immediately going to you. The second I can pay you 3K, immediately going to you. There's not a single dollar that is going to my wallet until your 3K has been paid off."

That is the answer to `[DECIDE]` item 3 on the payment trigger. The trigger is cash collected, and Abdullah is paid first out of the earliest revenue.

**The 30% is fixed and its internal split is agreed.** 15% funds the sales team when one exists (10% closer, 5% setter) and Abdullah keeps 15%. Until then Abdullah does the setting and closing himself and takes the full 30%. Abdullah at 21:28: "right now, we launch, but we only get like six people in. I'll handle the setting. I'll handle the closing and I'll just take all of you to myself." And at 22:21: "the 30% is a fixed fee, but it will come to a point where it's like, which is very soon, I'm going to go down to 15%. Cause then I'll take the money and pay these guys out."

Carlos walked through the math out loud at 19:37 to 21:13, initially misread 15% as sitting on top of the 30%, and confirmed his understanding once corrected. He is not going to claim later that he misunderstood the split.

**Carlos's own justification for paying above the original quote**, at 6:17: if Abdullah reached out to a small profile and genuinely saw money on both sides, then offering more than was asked, but only on realised revenue, is what proves the outreach was not "reaching out to small accounts, selling them some [thing], saying, oh, I see so much potential in you, cashing out from them and just giving them like a program."

> ⚠️ Contradiction against [[clients/fitfo/decisions]] and [[clients/fitfo/deal-conditions-draft]]: both currently record the trigger as "the first $3,000 of revenue Carlos generates goes entirely to Abdullah." Abdullah's own verbal recap at 25:5x is garbled in the transcript and reads "the two K off three K when you get the first three... 20k generated, and I'll take it." Fathom's AI summary resolved that to "$3k paid to AB Loui from the first $20k in revenue." Carlos's two explicit statements support the first-dollars reading, not a $20k gate. Resolution: do not treat either as settled. This is the one line that must be written unambiguously in the agreement, because the two readings change when Carlos starts taking money. Recommended wording: the first $3,000 of cash collected goes to Pulsify before Carlos draws anything, and the 70/30 split begins on every dollar collected after that $3,000.

## Delivery terms Carlos set

**Instagram is his.** At 9:39: "what I don't want is scripted reels that I have to like read off of. I want to still have my page on Instagram be me and who I am and be able post whatever the [thing] I want." He explicitly allowed profile picture, text, and visual changes. Abdullah agreed immediately and hard ("hell no, you're not scripting"), and reserved only a suggestion role plus a veto on content actively harming the brand. Carlos granted that veto unprompted at 15:45: "you have all the rights to tell me, yo, like the reel you posted is really stupid. It's going to harm your brand. Take it down."

**YouTube is open to full structure.** At 10:53: "YouTube should be more structured, the titles, the topics of the videos, but still, the videos, I want to do them myself... where you say, okay, here's what you need to do, make a video about the strategy, try not to make it any longer than 15 minutes, and try to cover these five bases, perfect, that's all I need, I can do that."

That is a direct commission for the packaging and scripting layer Pulsify already builds. It is also the reverse of the usual client friction, where the client wants control of the thing that needs structure.

**Production formats discussed for YouTube:** Miro board breakdown with a small circular facecam, in-person case study interviews with successful students, and recorded live mastermind sessions. Abdullah's framing was that production quality and angle now carry more weight than the raw idea, because the space has moved into high production.

## Funnel and scaling, as sketched

The funnel choice is deliberately deferred to data. Abdullah at 0:00: "I'll only be able to judge which funnel would be the best for you, depending on which platform favors you the most. If it was YouTube, then it would be a landing page funnel. If it was Instagram, then it's definitely going to be a profile funnel." This matches [[concepts/pulsify-order-of-operations]] and keeps the funnel gated behind the content pillar rather than built on spec.

Scaling is paid traffic, and explicitly not direct-to-mentorship. Ads funnel to the profile, where a "start here" highlight runs a story sequence narrating his story. That is the exact surface [[wiki/synthesis/instagram-project-fable-handoff]] and the story-sequence-generator skill exist to produce.

Abdullah's stated ad track record on the call: over $300,000 to $400,000 in spend across all accounts, averaging 4x to 12x ROAS.

## Systems and tooling agreed

- **Client portal.** Abdullah screen-shared the live design preview. Reel tracking, click-off rate, viewer data, a brainstorming surface, planning, and to-do lists. Carlos's read: "this would be like a page for like performance."
- **Cloud subscription.** Carlos needs his own paid AI subscription, around $20/month. He confirmed he already has it. Abdullah will teach him to integrate it with the portal.
- **Payment processor.** WAP / Fanbases rather than Stripe, on Abdullah's claim of better rates plus customer financing. Access normally requires roughly $30k/month recurring, and Abdullah says he has a connection to get around that gate.
- **Communication.** Slack, chosen over Carlos's Discord and WhatsApp. Carlos has never used Slack and said he can adapt.
- **Shared calendar.** Agreed, with future closer and setter availability visible in the same place.

## Risks and things to watch

**Abdullah answered a direct earnings question with a yes.** Carlos at 43:13, prefacing it as full honesty: "do you really see this working for more than $10,000 a month?" by end of year. Abdullah: "Yeah, yeah, I do." That is a verbal forward-looking projection on a performance deal with no written performance floor. It is not the $100k guarantee from [[playbooks/pulsify-offer-v1]], and it should not quietly become one. Worth deciding whether the agreement states explicitly that no revenue outcome is guaranteed.

**The authority-photo direction brushes against the proof-currency rule.** Abdullah anchored on Iman Gadzhi and was careful to rule out the obvious flex ("you don't need a Porsche", "I don't need to have like that Birkin"). But the concrete examples he showed were a dinner, walking to Rolex, a fancy mall with good lighting, and a hotel. The ratified dial in [[wiki/synthesis/instagram-project-fable-handoff]] is student-results-never-lifestyle, and [[CLAUDE.md]] positioning is explicitly against selling on lifestyle. "More pictures of who I am and where I am" is defensible. Rolex and fancy malls are the start of the drift. Flagging rather than resolving, because Carlos's own positioning spine is anti-BS authenticity and that is his strongest asset.

**Carlos is time-constrained and said so twice.** At 6:02: "I can't take infinite time. This is my priority, number one. I don't want you to think that I'm putting work first. It's just where I am today." He also cut the call short once for a work call and lost 15 minutes. Any cadence commitment has to be built around an investment-bank job, which is consistent with the async constraint recorded on [2026-07-24].

**Abdullah's own disqualification tripwire is now stated.** At 22:45: the moment he watches for is the first big launch, and specifically $50k/month, because "that's literally when people relent" and slide into feast-and-famine posting. Worth writing into the agreement as the client-side cadence obligation, since a performance deal makes Carlos's consistency the payment trigger.
