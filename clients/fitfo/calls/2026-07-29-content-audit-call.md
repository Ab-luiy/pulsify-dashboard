## Three-bullet summary

- **The $3,000 trigger ambiguity is resolved, and it resolves toward first-dollars.** At 31:12 Carlos restated the deal himself: "we sign on the 7030 and then the 3k upfront before I get any money. That's been agreed upon." Abdullah answered "Perfect." The $20,000 figure in Fathom's summary of call 1 was an artefact of a garbled recap. Both parties have now stated first-dollars priority in their own words.
- **Carlos asked directly for termination clauses and did not get an answer.** He raised it deliberately, framed it as protecting both sides, and named a concrete scenario: three months in, nothing close to expectation, he walks. Abdullah answered with a story about how Nick's exit went and an appeal to good faith. No clause, no notice period, no exit terms. This is now the largest open exposure in the deal and it replaces the one that closed on call 1.
- **Carlos wants the contract before any work starts, and he intends to redline it.** He asked for everything in writing, said he will change what he thinks needs changing and ask for clarification, and only then sign. He also stated plainly that work begins after signature, not before. That sequencing is favourable and should be honoured rather than worked around.

## The termination problem, in his words

This is the section to read before drafting anything.

Carlos at 29:02: "I need to also understand what are termination clauses in the case that this simply just does is not working for either of us... if I say like, in three months, I'm not doing honestly anything close to what I expect. I simply just don't see things working out between us."

Then at 31:21 he named three things he does not want, and they compound:

1. **He wants to keep the systems on exit.** "I still want to keep the systems."
2. **He does not want a time commitment.** "I don't want if we have a contract to be time-based binded."
3. **He does not want to be bound to pay the $3,000.** "I don't want it to be money binded either... what I don't want is for you to say, oh, no, we're going to work until you give me my money back or not money back, like until you pay me."

Read together, that is an arrangement where Carlos can walk at any point, keep everything built, and owe nothing. His reasoning is sincere rather than adversarial: he believes he will make the $3,000 regardless, so being bound to it feels unnecessary to him. That belief is not the same as an obligation.

Abdullah's response at 32:15 did not address it. He said he operates in good faith, then described how the Nick engagement ended: Nick kept the systems, the work stopped, the friendship survived. Good faith is how the relationship should run. It is not a termination clause.

> ⚠️ This is the deal's structural gap and it is worse than the one it replaces. Pulsify now has: no upfront, no time floor, no lapse date, an expanded build scope agreed on call 1, and a counterparty who has explicitly asked not to be bound to the only payment in the deal. Every risk sits on one side.
>
> The fix does not require taking anything he objected to. He objected to being *forced to keep working* until Pulsify is paid. He did not object to owing the $3,000 if he launches and collects. Those are separable. A clause that says the $3,000 survives termination on revenue he collects within a defined window after exit, while he keeps the systems and owes nothing if he never launches, gives him everything he asked for and still closes the hole.

## Content decisions

**The whole strategy in one line, Abdullah at 6:11:** "the whole movement is just taking people from IG to YouTube." Instagram builds reach, YouTube builds the depth Instagram cannot. From YouTube, the description carries the landing page first and the IG link second.

**Instagram:**
- Kill the Linktree. Replace with the YouTube channel direct, plus a CTA. Carlos agreed and called the Linktree "a lazy attempt."
- Remove the word "free" from anything mentioning mentorship. Abdullah: "Mentorships are never free."
- Bio to be optimised off-call rather than live.
- URL Genius named as the click tracker.
- Highlights get a "start here" structure built from story sequences, using Nick's build as the reference.
- Reels stay untouched. Story sequences and carousels are the work.

**YouTube:**
- The channel is underbuilt and unpackaged. Consistency is already there.
- One video did well on an eye-catching net-worth angle. Same-angle videos can be reposted with different packaging.
- Scope is titles, thumbnails, and what to say.
- **Body gets bullet points only, never a script.** Hooks and CTAs can be scripted. Abdullah's reasoning: a rant needs a few seconds to find the genuine feeling. Carlos: "You don't want to sound like a robot." This is a cleaner rule than the one recorded on call 1 and should carry into delivery.
- Hyper-niche videos are gated behind a subscriber and view threshold. Until then, follow competitor packaging with his own twist.

**Deprioritised, both by agreement:**
- **YouTube Shorts.** Abdullah: "it gets a lot of views, but it has no impact whatsoever." Carlos had already noticed the same thing independently.
- **Twitter.** Abdullah declined to advise at all: "I really have no much information on Twitter, so I'm not going to yap about it." Carlos keeps posting trade recaps with a small CTA. Worth noting as the right call rather than a gap.

**Funnel and scaling stay deferred by mutual agreement.** Carlos: "I don't think it's worth talking too much about the funnel right now... just Instagram, YouTube, packaging properly." Scaling is three to four months out. The VSL will be scripted when it comes, and Carlos accepted that without friction.
