## The one diagnostic worth tracking going forward

A 4M-plus-view reel produced an estimated 500-600 followers. Reach is not the bottleneck on his account; conversion of reach into followers, and followers into relationship, is. This is the concrete evidence behind moving depth-building to YouTube. Worth re-measuring the reach-to-follower ratio each time a video breaks out, since it is the number that will show whether the IG-to-YouTube funnel is actually working.
