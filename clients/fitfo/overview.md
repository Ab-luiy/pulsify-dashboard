# FITFO overview

Carlos, 25, trading creator in the FX and markets niche. Signed in principle on 2026-07-29 on a zero-upfront performance deal. He has an audience forming and no offer, which makes him a program stall rather than a content stall, and it means the entire deal is currently gated on building something for him to sell.

## Who he is

Former professional tennis player. Works at an investment bank and cannot quit it. Roughly $10k/month total income, split about half job and half his own trading. Zero revenue from mentorship, courses, or any info product as of 2026-07-29.

He asks his father for advice, not his girlfriend or friends, and stressed the decision to work with Pulsify was his own. Any written follow-up is addressed to him alone.

## Positioning spine: authenticity, stated unprompted and repeatedly

He disagrees with people who sell a trading mentorship without trading, and uses his tennis background as the analogy: a coach who never played is worthless. He does not want to be a BSer. He is also clear-eyed that mentorship earns more reliably than trading, and that trading is "educated gambling" that never stops being that.

This is a direct match with [[concepts/trust-recession]] and Pulsify's proof-currency rule. Everything built for him leans on this rather than working around it. It is also the strongest available answer to his own hesitation about selling.

## Hard constraints on any offer built for him

- **He cannot run anything live during work hours.** Stated plainly on 2026-07-24. Any product has to be async or framework-based, not live-trading sessions.
- **He is time-constrained and says so.** On 2026-07-29: "I can't take infinite time. This is my priority, number one... it's just where I am today." He lost 15 minutes of that call to a work interruption.
- **Instagram is not available for scripting.** His term, agreed by Abdullah. See [[clients/fitfo/decisions]].

## The offer: does not exist yet

This is the central fact about the engagement. He has no mentorship, no course, no paid Discord, nothing priced. The 2026-07-24 call established that both sides agreed not to rush one, and [[concepts/pulsify-order-of-operations]] says nothing downstream gets built until what is upstream exists.

The consequence, which belongs in the agreement rather than in a later conversation: the payment trigger is revenue, revenue requires an offer, and the offer does not exist. Pulsify's build is fully on spec until it does.

Directions discussed but not decided: one-on-one mentorship seats around $2,000 (used as the worked example on 07-29), or a premium Discord. Nothing chosen.

## Commercial terms

Current structure, agreed 2026-07-29. Full detail and the one unresolved number in [[clients/fitfo/decisions]] and [[clients/fitfo/deal-conditions-draft]].

| Item | Term |
|---|---|
| Upfront | $0. The original $2,000 was waived. |
| First tranche | $3,000 to Pulsify, paid out of the earliest revenue, before Carlos draws anything. Carlos calls it an "interest fee" for waiving the deposit. |
| After that | 70/30 split, Carlos/Pulsify. On the record as of 26:07 on the 07-29 call. |
| Inside the 30% | 15% funds the sales team when one exists (10% closer, 5% setter). Pulsify keeps 15%. Until then Abdullah sets and closes himself and takes the full 30%. |
| Time limit | None. Open exposure. |
| Signed paper | None yet. Contract is action item one from the 07-29 call. |

> ⚠️ This sits below the documented floor in [[playbooks/pulsify-offer-v1]] ($5,000 base plus 30%) and carries no floor at all. Treat as a stage-appropriate variant, not a new floor. [[playbooks/pulsify-contract-template-v1]] has no branch for a zero-base performance deal, so the agreement needs a performance-only variant rather than the v1 template.

## Scope of work

Agreed on 2026-07-29.

**Pulsify owns:** YouTube structure and packaging (titles, topics, length, required beats), IG bio, grid direction, the client portal, Slack workspace with daily briefing, shared calendar, WAP/Fanbases payment setup, and the funnel once a platform proves out.

**Carlos owns:** all recording and posting, Instagram creative entirely, and the decision to launch.

**Pulsify explicitly does not:** script his reels, or post as him.

Deliverable volumes and revision caps are still undefined, which is the live scope risk on a zero-upfront deal.

## Goals

- **His stated six-month target:** roughly 10,000 Instagram followers.
- **Abdullah's read:** 10,000 in six months is the floor if the method is right; 20,000 to 30,000 is achievable.
- **His stated one-year ambition, on 07-29:** "$500k a month in a year." He pre-empted the word delusional himself.
- **The number he actually tested Abdullah against:** more than $10,000/month by end of year. Abdullah said yes. Recorded as a risk in [[clients/fitfo/calls/2026-07-29-terms-lockdown-call]] because it is a verbal projection on a deal with no written performance floor.

## Channels

- **Instagram** is the working channel and the one he protects. Roughly 1,000 followers and about six weeks into posting seriously as of 2026-07-24. Growing at a rate he described as decent.
- **YouTube** exists and is underbuilt. He volunteered that he could do "a lot better" there, and handed it over for structuring. This is the lead-generation engine in the plan.
- **Paid ads** are the later scaling layer, funnelling to the profile rather than direct to an offer.
- TikTok and X were raised as optional and not discussed further.
