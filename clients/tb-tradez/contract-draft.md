## I. The Services

The Operator is engaged to install and operate the Pulse System inside the Client's business across the Term defined in Section III. The Pulse System comprises five components, each with defined deliverables and human checkpoints:

1. **Brand Brain** - a client-owned knowledge vault built from past calls, past content, structured intake, and competitor research. Delivered Week 1-2.
2. **Content Engine** - weekly research, scripting, packaging, and pipeline management for the Client's primary content channel (YouTube long-form by default). Delivered from Week 3 onward.
3. **Funnel Stack** - the conversion path from content view to sales call, including VSL, application form, booking calendar, and Instagram profile-funnel wiring. Delivered Week 4-5.
4. **Sales Co-pilot** - pre-call briefs, drafted proposals, drafted follow-ups, automatic CRM stage transitions. Delivered Week 6+.
5. **Ops Replacement** - comment triage, DM triage, dashboard refresh, weekly metric reports. Delivered Week 9+.

A full Client Scope Document confirming exact deliverables, channel choices, and integrations will be produced by the Operator in Week 1 of the engagement and shared with the Client for sign-off.

The engagement also includes:
- Weekly reporting and optimization meetings.
- A dedicated Slack or Discord workspace.
- Direct asynchronous communication channels with the Operator.

Deliverables are subject to adjustment due to unforeseen circumstances or changes in the Client's needs, by mutual written agreement.

## II. Fees

**Base Fee: None.** The Client pays no fixed fee, no setup fee, and no retainer. The Operator funds the entire build at its own cost and risk. The Parties expressly acknowledge that this places the whole of the Operator's commercial risk on the Performance Share defined below, and that the provisions of Sections III, IV and VI are material terms without which the Operator would not have entered into this Agreement on a no-fee basis.

**Performance Share:** The Operator shall receive thirty-five percent (35%) of all attributable revenue collected by the Client, payable on a weekly settlement cadence within five (5) business days of the close of each calendar week.

**Attribution Rule:** All revenue collected by the Client from the date the Operator is integrated into the Client's CRM and payment processor is attributable to the Operator, with the sole exception of leads that were already in active negotiation with a scheduled close date prior to integration. Such excluded leads shall be listed by name in the Client Scope Document in Week 1; any lead not listed there is attributable. After integration, every revenue event collected counts toward the performance share because the Operator owns the systems generating, processing, and tracking them.

**Refunds and chargebacks:** Where the Client refunds a customer or suffers a chargeback on a revenue event on which a Performance Share has already been paid, the corresponding Performance Share is credited against the next weekly settlement. The Operator does not return cash for refunded events; the credit mechanism is the sole remedy.

**Settlement Source of Truth:** The weekly setter/closer report in the shared workspace and the payment-processor export are the joint source of truth. Both Parties have read access. Disputes are resolved against those records.

All amounts are quoted in USD. Late payment or failure of payment shall result in immediate cessation of all work carried out by the Operator until payment is resolved, and shall suspend the Operator's obligation to maintain any installed system. All work product remains the sole property of the Operator until the amounts due in respect of it are paid.

## III. Term, Minimum Commitment, and Termination

**Initial Term:** Ninety (90) days from 2026-08-26 (the "Initial Term"), continuing thereafter on a rolling basis until terminated in accordance with this Section.

**Minimum Commitment.** In consideration of the Operator providing the Services with no Base Fee, the Client agrees not to terminate for convenience during the Initial Term. The Client may terminate during the Initial Term only for cause under the paragraph below.

**Termination by Client after the Initial Term:** the Client may terminate on thirty (30) days' written notice.

**Termination by Client for cause:** the Client may terminate at any time, on fourteen (14) days' written notice, if the Operator materially fails to deliver the components listed in Section I in accordance with the Client Scope Document and fails to cure within that notice period.

**Termination by Operator:** the Operator may terminate at any time on fourteen (14) days' written notice, and may terminate immediately for cause if the Client (a) fails to provide or withdraws the CRM or payment-processor access required for attribution, (b) fails to attend two or more consecutive scheduled meetings without rescheduling, (c) fails to pay a weekly settlement when due, or (d) materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within fourteen (14) days of written notice.

**Survival of the Performance Share.** The Performance Share shall continue to apply, and shall be paid on the same weekly cadence, for six (6) months following the effective date of termination, in respect of revenue collected by the Client from:

(a) any lead that entered the Client's pipeline during the engagement; and

(b) any revenue generated through or attributable to content, funnels, sequences, or systems built by the Operator during the engagement, for so long as the Client continues to use them.

The Client's reporting and access obligations in Section VI survive termination for the duration of this six-month period, limited to what is reasonably necessary to compute the sums due. If the Client ceases all use of the Operator's work product and confirms this in writing, obligation (b) ends on that date; obligation (a) continues for the full six-month period regardless.

**Removal option.** The Client may at any time after termination elect to cease using the Operator's work product entirely, in which case the Operator will on request provide reasonable assistance to decommission it. This election ends obligation (b) prospectively but does not refund or reverse any Performance Share already accrued.

## IV. Performance Guarantee

The Parties acknowledge that this engagement carries no Base Fee and that the Operator is therefore compensated only when the Client collects revenue. The Parties agree that this structure replaces, and is given in lieu of, the Operator's standard revenue guarantee. No separate monetary guarantee is given and none is owed.

The Operator commits to deliver the components of the Pulse System described in Section I in accordance with the Client Scope Document. That delivery obligation is enforceable under Section III.

---

## V. Payment Scheme

The Client agrees to settle all Performance Share payments in USD via the following preferred rails:

1. **Cryptocurrency** (USDT or USDC on a major chain) - preferred.
2. **International SWIFT bank transfer** to the Operator's nominated account.

Alternative rails (Wise, Payoneer, or equivalent) may be arranged upon mutual agreement before the first settlement. Stripe is not available for this engagement due to the Operator's payment-rail configuration.

The Client is responsible for any fees, taxes, or withholdings imposed by their jurisdiction. Quoted amounts are net to the Operator.

---

## VI. Client Integration Requirement

This Section is a condition precedent. The Operator's obligation to commence the Services does not arise until the access below is granted.

To enable the Services and the attribution rule in Section II, the Client agrees to grant the Operator read or admin access (as appropriate) to the following systems within five (5) business days of the Effective Date:

- The Client's CRM (or, where none exists, agreement to install one within the engagement).
- The Client's payment processor dashboard or equivalent revenue-collection record.
- The Client's content publishing accounts for the channels in scope.
- The Client's calendar booking system.
- The Client's communication channels with any existing setters or closers.

The Client shall maintain this access for the duration of the engagement and, to the limited extent set out in Section III, for the survival period following termination. Withdrawal of access without the Operator's written agreement is a material breach.

Without integration the Operator cannot perform the Services or compute the Performance Share. Failure to provide integration within fourteen (14) days of the Effective Date is grounds for termination under Section III.

## VII. Non-Disclosure and Intellectual Property

Both Parties mutually agree that certain confidential information, including but not limited to funnel strategies, revenue data, client lists, and internal frameworks, may be exchanged during this engagement. Both Parties agree not to disclose or share this information with any third party unless required by law or with prior written consent.

a. **Confidential Materials:** Includes technical materials, models, strategies, and intellectual property created during this engagement.

b. **Sales Materials:** Includes pricing strategies, sales funnels, and client acquisition methods provided by either Party.

c. **Intellectual Property Rights:**
- The Client retains ownership of the Brand Brain populated with their data, the per-client AI agent installed in their accounts, all content produced under the engagement, and any creative output bearing the Client's likeness or voice.
- The Operator retains ownership of all underlying templates, frameworks, prompt packs, the Pulse System methodology, and any tooling not specific to the Client's account.
- Pre-existing intellectual property of either Party remains the sole property of its original owner.
- The licences granted to the Client in this Section are conditional on the Client remaining current on all Performance Share payments due, and suspend automatically during any period in which a settlement is overdue.

d. **Additional Confidential Information:** Any other information explicitly marked or identified as confidential by either Party.

**Case Studies and Marketing Use:** The Client agrees that the Operator may share case studies, screenshots, testimonials, and quantitative results achieved through the engagement for marketing purposes. Personal or brand-identifiable information will be excluded upon the Client's written request.
