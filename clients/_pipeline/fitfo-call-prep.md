## The one question that decides the whole call

**Does he sell anything?**

Nothing in five uploads points to an offer, a link, a lead magnet, or a mentorship. He may be a pure creator with no monetisation beyond prop payouts.

This is not a detail. [[playbooks/pulsify-offer-v1]] lists "no offer at all (I just post about trading)" as an explicit **disqualifier** for the standard engagement, and prescribes a separate $5k offer-creation scope with no performance fee. The $100k-in-90-days guarantee cannot be put in front of someone with nothing to sell, because the guarantee is carried by the qualification gate.

So the call forks at minute five:

- **Has an offer** -> standard engagement, open $5k + 30%, the packaging work is already proof of competence.
- **No offer** -> offer creation first, scoped separately. Bigger conversation, longer runway, and honestly the better business for him.

Ask it early and plainly: *what happens when someone messages you after a video?* His answer sorts the fork without him feeling sold.

## Call shape

Per [[frameworks/call-funnel-framework]] and the Grant close:

1. Rapport, short. He is time-poor.
2. Diagnose. Fork question first, then the lead-flow and DM questions.
3. Present what he already has, and what the next layer would be.
4. Project conservatively off his real numbers, never invented ones. **Note: his subscriber count and view figures are not in the vault. Pull them before the call or do not project at all.**
5. Structure the deal against the fork.
