## The video

**Idea:** the one concept he teaches every student first, shown live on a real chart. Location (premium and discount, where the probabilities sit) matters more than which entry model you use. It sells the mentorship because it is his mechanism, and the live-breakdown format is the one that already works on his channel ("watch us breakdown our 6 figure trading systems live" is his best video at 814 views against a usual ~125).

**Funnel stage:** BOF. People who watch this and like it are the ones who apply.

### Outline (his words where we have them)

1. **Hook, first 30 seconds.** "I started trading at 17. I was trading the 6:30 open because that's what my friend told me. I bought a course like anyone else, and I still wasn't making money." Then the promise: the one concept that changed it, the same one he teaches every student first.
2. **The problem.** Most struggling traders keep switching entry models: ICT, SMC, breakouts. The entry isn't what's failing them. They don't know where the probabilities are.
3. **The concept.** Price moves between premium and discount. Draw the range on the chart, mark the middle, and show where you're more likely to win and where you're more likely to get run over. In his words: "it's just the understanding of where the probabilities lie in each and every situation."
4. **Live, step by step.** Mark up a real recent day from the open. Show where he'd take a trade and where he'd sit on his hands, and show that it works the same with an ICT entry, an SMC entry or a breakout entry.
5. **Students.** Two or three student results from his proof screenshots, with permission. For each one: what changed when they added the concept to the entry they already had.
6. **The offer.** "I work with every student myself. I don't hand you off to someone else." Apply link in the description, or DM "EDGE".

**Two other openers for slide 1**
- "i bought a course at 17 / still wasn't making money / here's what i was missing"
- "every trader i've taught had the same problem / and it wasn't their entry"

**Why this order:** it opens with his own start so it feels like a story, not an ad. Slides 3 and 4 make the viewer see themselves. Slide 5 is the one reframe (location over entry), repeated from the video. The student proof lands right after that reframe, so it reads as proof of it. The video comes before the ask, so the people who DM have already seen him teach.

**Voice check done:** no "it's not X, it's Y" except the quoted law on slide 3, no em dashes in the slides, straight quotes, every number his. It is written lowercase to match his account.
