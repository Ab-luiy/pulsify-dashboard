## The story

Alex is a 21-year-old guy stuck in that confusing, frustrating phase of life where he’s old enough to know he doesn’t want the typical 9-to-5 grind, but young enough to feel like he still hasn’t figured out how to break free.

Every morning, he wakes up with this restless energy inside — a hunger to do more, to make his mark, to prove to himself and the people around him that he’s not just “average.” But at the same time, he feels a heavy weight of anxiety pushing down on him. What if he fails? What if all this trying is just a waste of time?

For months, he’s been chasing that dream of financial freedom, picturing himself living on his own terms — trading from a laptop in some cool city or beach town, driving nice cars, and having the freedom to take care of his family without stress. He’s seen the Instagram reels, TikToks, YouTube videos — young millionaires flashing their Lambos, cash stacks, and private jets. Deep down, he wants that too. But those images also make him question himself. Why am I not there yet? Am I just missing something?

He’s tried to learn trading on his own. Countless late nights watching free videos, trying to copy strategies, downloading random PDFs, lurking in online forums, and reading posts from traders who sound like they’ve “made it.” Sometimes he thinks he’s getting it — a few small wins here and there — but then the market turns, he loses more than he wants, and the hope crumbles again.

Some days he’s full of motivation. He sets alarms early, opens his charts, takes notes, and feels like he’s got a plan. But inevitably, the doubt creeps in. The fear that maybe he’s not cut out for this, or maybe the success stories online are all fake. He tries to fight it with more hustle — grinding harder, watching more tutorials, jumping on more live streams.

At times, Alex has even bought a few courses. Maybe one or two that promised the secret formula to trading success. But after paying hundreds or thousands of dollars, he’s left feeling more confused than before. The content feels overwhelming, or too complicated, or just not realistic for someone like him. Sometimes the “mentors” vanish after the sale, leaving him to figure it all out alone. Other times, the community is toxic or unhelpful, which just chips away at his confidence.

For those who never took the plunge to buy, it’s mostly because trust is a huge problem for Alex. The internet is flooded with scammers and “gurus” who promise everything but deliver nothing. Every glowing testimonial could be fake. Every flashy ad might just be an illusion. And so he hesitates, caught between wanting to change and not knowing who to believe.

This lack of trust feeds his anxiety. It feels like every choice is a gamble — and if he bets wrong, it could cost him time, money, and his already fragile confidence. He’s scared to take the leap, but also terrified of staying stuck forever.

Outside of trading, Alex’s life feels a bit chaotic too. He’s juggling school or maybe a part-time job, trying to manage relationships with family and friends who don’t fully get why he’s so obsessed with “this trading thing.” They tell him to get a real job, “be realistic,” or just settle down. But he can’t settle — not when there’s this voice inside him screaming that there’s more.

Some nights, Alex stays up late scrolling through social media, watching stories of people his age making bank trading, launching side hustles, or traveling the world. It’s inspiring, but also painful. It reminds him of how far behind he feels, and it makes him question his own progress. That mix of envy and hope is exhausting.

Alex battles with his own emotions daily. His confidence is a rollercoaster — soaring high when he thinks he’s cracked the code, and crashing hard when a losing streak hits. He can get frustrated and impatient with himself. Sometimes he gets so overwhelmed he zones out or procrastinates, telling himself he’ll start fresh tomorrow.

Despite all this, he’s desperately determined. He’s not ready to give up. He tells himself over and over, “Just one more try,” or “I’m one strategy away from making it work.” That relentless hope is what keeps him going.

What Alex really wants is clarity and guidance — a mentor who understands his struggles, can show him exactly what to do step-by-step, and can hold him accountable through the messy, frustrating parts. He wants proof that success is possible — not just flashy cars or big numbers, but real, steady progress that builds his confidence and changes his life.

Alex doesn’t want to be sold to. He wants real talk, no fluff, no hype. He wants to be part of a community that supports him without judgment.

Because if he can find that — if he can find someone who gets him and can guide him — Alex believes he can finally break the cycle of doubt, frustration, and burnout. He can finally build the kind of life where he wakes up with excitement instead of dread, and trading becomes not just a dream, but his reality.

- Lack of clear guidance in trading — overwhelmed by misinformation.
- Trust issues due to past course scams and shady “gurus.”
- Emotional rollercoaster of hope and doubt, causing inconsistent progress.
- Pressure from family/friends to choose “stable” paths.
- Difficulty balancing trading learning with work/school/family.

### 3. Age
