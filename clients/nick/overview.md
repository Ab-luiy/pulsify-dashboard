# Nick — trading coach

## Quick profile

Niche: trading + info offer (selling knowledge around trading)
Stage when Pulsify started: $10K-$20K/month from trading alone, no consistent info offer revenue
Result: $50K/month (trading + info offer combined)
Timeframe: approximately 3 months to consistent qualified leads

## What was done

Rebuilt positioning. Built outreach motion leveraging existing connections in the trading space (including Mamba FX). Got Nick in frame with names that mattered in his niche. Gave cold traffic a reason to take him seriously before they got on a call.
