# Off-boarding pack template

Fires when an engagement closes — target hit, mutual exit, or termination. Covers the final-call agenda, the final email, and the asset handover checklist the delivery SOP listed as undrafted. The goal of off-boarding is a clean exit that produces a case study and a referral, not just an ended contract.

---

## Final call agenda (30 min, recorded)

1. **The numbers, start to finish (5 min).** Attributable revenue total, what was collected when, performance-fee final position. Same processor view as always.
2. **Handover walkthrough (15 min).** Screen-share through everything they keep (list below): where each asset lives, how to run it, what breaks if ignored.
3. **Maintain or retire (5 min).** For each running routine: keep running it themselves, hire it out, or switch it off cleanly. Name what dies first without maintenance (usually the research cadence, then the pre-call sequence freshness).
4. **Case study and referral (5 min).** If the target was hit: testimonial ask (recorded now, on this call, while it's warm — a 2-minute raw answer beats a polished one later), case-study permission and what numbers can be public, and the referral question: "who else in [niche] should be running like this?"

Recording filed at `clients/{name}/calls/offboarding.md`.

## What the client keeps

- [ ] Brand Brain (their Claude project — ownership confirmed/transferred)
- [ ] The custom agent setup and its instructions
- [ ] Funnel: landing page, VSL files and hosting access, Typeform, Cal.com
- [ ] Pre-call email sequence in their ESP
- [ ] All SOPs Pulsify wrote for their operation
- [ ] Their dashboard view

## What Pulsify retains

- Templates, frameworks, prompt packs, Pulse System methodology
- Case-study rights as agreed on the final call
- Nothing that requires the client's credentials — all access returned

## Final email

**Subject:** [First name] — handover complete. Everything you keep, in one place.

[First name],

Everything is handed over as of today. One email to bookmark:

**Your assets:** [links — Brand Brain project, funnel pages, Typeform, Cal.com, SOP folder, dashboard]

**Access returned:** we're out of [list: IG, YouTube, ESP, processor, calendar]. Rotate any shared passwords this week.

**Performance fee settlement:** [final schedule — what's settled, what settles when, against which processor export]

**If you maintain one thing,** maintain [the single highest-leverage routine for this client]. The walkthrough recording covers how: [link].

It was a serious 90 days. [One specific, true sentence about what this client did well — their execution, not flattery.]

[Founder name]
