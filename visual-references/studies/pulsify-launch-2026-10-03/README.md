# Pulsify founder and product launch reference study

Reviewed 2026-10-03 at the founder's explicit request to inspect and file five X references. This is a reference study and an original proposed production direction, not a finished film or an approved commercial offer.

## Evidence and access

All five posts were opened in the browser; their accessible written material and linked guides were read. X returned 403 through the web reader, but browser access worked. Four embedded demos from the first guide were downloaded and sampled every 0.5 seconds. Metadata records dimensions, durations, frame rates and contact sheets. This is frame sampling, not a frame-by-frame reconstruction or an audio analysis.

The two dawood46 players and Raphael's linked Howseen film streamed but did not export through the browser media downloader. Course samples with correctly framed full-page captures are saved as `notdwd-course-verified-*`, with observed playback times in JSON. Comparison evidence is limited to the opening side-by-side view and its detailed written specification. Raphael screenshots show selected product scenes, with timestamps unknown. Playback sound, exact easing curves and all cuts of these streamed films are not verified.

Early cropped captures of the dawood46 players caught surrounding page text because the screenshot crop did not match the visible player. They are invalid evidence and excluded. Corrected full-page captures were inspected. Original videos are reference-only assets; do not incorporate them into Pulsify deliverables.

## 1. zero / twoclipping — shared elements carry the story

[Post and full guide](https://x.com/twoclipping/status/2104446706741825818). Four exports: overview, food, photo, UI. Overview and UI are visually similar demos, not independent taste signals.

Observed: the UI demo transforms a button into a loader, music player, volume control, toggle, date selector, chart, command menu and confirmation. Few objects, large readable scale, cursor-led actions. Food moves from request to choice to order. Photography keeps one image recognizable through editing, devices, print configuration and a room on a wall. The image is the narrative carrier.

Useful for Pulsify: keep one content idea recognizable through experience, angle, title, plan and review. Preserve its wording or thumbnail so transitions communicate progress. These exports are UI/product films; the founder-talking-head hybrid is our proposed adaptation.

The guide describes deterministic HTML motion, still review, frame rendering and sound sync. Its cost and speed claims are unverified marketing claims. Taste, direction and revisions remain work.

## 2. dawood46 / notdwd — a tightly specified product sting

[Comparison post](https://x.com/notdwd/status/2104401208958230764). The opening shows original and remake side by side; the post credits @thednyx. Detailed written specification describes a roughly 12-second sequence: header, icon, prompt, answer, cards and wordmark. Blue-white glass, cursor emphasis, scale changes and blur tied to movement are source direction, not measured parameters from our capture.

Useful for Pulsify: a short reveal cutdown with one action and an unmistakable ending. Build a beat map and settle objects long enough to read. The source includes hard cuts and blur transitions; continuous morphing is not a universal rule.

Do not lift the entire shot order, Apple-like blue palette, Whop identity, frame timing or music into our film. Its 15-minute and zero-cost claims are not production estimates for Pulsify.

## 3. dawood46 / notdwd — direct the process, then show examples

[Film](https://x.com/notdwd/status/2104694573067120934) · [Linked guide](https://x.com/notdwd/status/2104684539142648062). Browser duration about 36.7 seconds. Verified samples show a goat-led prompt opening, a goggles-wearing monkey landing-page example, overlapping tilted project cards, large type, a glass composer and a blue grid background. Selected later scenes demonstrate editing direction and film categories.

The guide advocates studying references, deciding what to preserve, a coherent visual system, reviewing stills, specific critique and sound alignment. Useful for Pulsify: show actual work and a visible decision, rather than only announcing capabilities. Cards can briefly preview the range, then return to one workflow. The source's mascots and technical grid are not Pulsify identity.

## 4. Bakers Studio — ambient movement and material

[Post](https://x.com/studiobakers/status/2102349193881006288). Reuses the existing [full local study](../bakers-liquid-gradients/README.md), including three 60 fps originals and six timestamped frames each. All three timeline sheets were inspected again.

Observed: broad, softly deforming ribbons; light seams; grain; coral/cream, blue/yellow and mint/aqua variations. Useful as a quiet transition or background around solid product surfaces. Adapt into Pulsify's existing low-saturation graphite, coral, violet and slate treatment. Do not replace the approved app palette. Original shader, exact easing and seamless looping remain unknown. The older study's fixed-sidebar instruction is historical; capture the current app navigation.

## 5. Raphael Aubry — prove a product workflow

[Guide](https://x.com/RaphaelAubryy/status/2104502744010629269) · [Linked Howseen film](https://x.com/RaphaelAubryy/status/2104300246268571733) · [Public implementation](https://github.com/howseen-ai/claude-motion-design).

The guide describes input collection, beat maps, stills, render review, deterministic animation and sound. It asks for truthful product screens and labelled example numbers. Although the promotion says one prompt, the guide describes multiple revision passes. No software was installed or run from this repository.

Selected screenshots show buyer questions, prompt tracking, source lists, CMS compatibility and a final product CTA. Useful for Pulsify: connect a buyer problem to a visible action and the next useful state. Do not borrow Howseen's AI visibility capabilities or integration logos. Sound and full transition timing are not verified here.

## Original Pulsify direction

Working concept: **I built the way I work into an app.** Founder presence supplies motive, the app supplies evidence, consultation supplies judgment. The film follows one grounded example, with intentional cuts back to the founder. Dense personal copy belongs in stories and carousel pages; the moving film uses short captions and spoken context.

See [[wiki/synthesis/pulsify-product-launch-direction]] for the 65-second beat map, draft voiceover, nine-story sequence, eight-page carousel, footage list and production checks. This synthesis also uses existing Niksetting and SooWei research, not an assumed characterization of either person.
