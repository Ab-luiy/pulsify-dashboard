## How to add a win

Append a section under **Clients / partners** with: name, niche, status, what we did,
the number, and the date range. If a number isn't verified, put ⚠️ on it. Small wins
count — a single closed offer, a follower milestone, a launch that worked. The point is
that nothing gets lost between sessions.
