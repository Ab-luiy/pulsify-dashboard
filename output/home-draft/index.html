    Questions

    Who is this for?

    Creators who already do the thing they teach: traders, ecom and Amazon sellers, Airbnb hosts and other skill niches, who want to sell a high-ticket mentorship off the back of their content.

    Do I need a big audience first?

    No. Your follower count isn't what's holding you back. The foundation is: the right videos, a clear offer and a funnel that tracks every step. It compounds, so it takes time.

    Do I have to be on camera?

    Yes. We don't do faceless content. People buy mentorship from someone they've watched.

    Will you make me look rich?

    No. We never use cars, watches or lifestyle shots. Proof means your results and your students' results.

    What does it cost?

    It depends on where you are and what we'd build. We go through it on a call once we both know it fits.

    What do I have to do?

    Film the videos, take your own sales calls and show up for the calls we set. We handle the planning, scripting, packaging, funnel and tracking.

    How is this different from an agency?

    You get the work and the dashboard it runs on. It shows every video ranked by the cash it made and the next thing to fix, so you always know where the money is coming from.
