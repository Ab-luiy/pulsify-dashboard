## Where the packaging breaks

**Four-word compression test.** Titles 2, 3 and 5 pass (`survive losing weeks`, `tripled net worth`, `bounce back -$20,000`). Title 1 fails: the plus sign welds two promises together, so no four words carry it. Title 4 fails: `trading harder than necessary` names no concrete thing the viewer receives.

**Complete vs repeat.** All five are complete titles, which is the right call for an authority channel. Nothing to fix here.

**One clear focal point.** All five fail. Three are a room, and the two screen-recordings carry browser chrome, sidebars, a taskbar and a weather widget competing with the actual subject.

**Reads at 120px.** All five fail. No text, no crop, no deliberate contrast.

**Title and thumbnail connected but not redundant.** Not applicable, because there is no thumbnail text on any of them. The entire second half of the pair is missing, so the title is doing all the work alone.

**Face on camera.** Technically present in three, but as an accident of frame selection rather than a chosen expression. Two have no face at all.

**Topic overlap.** Videos 1, 2 and 5 are the same subject. Two of them make the same promise with different wording, so they compete with each other and drag the channel average down.

**What is already right.** His titles are written in practitioner language, not beginner language. "How to survive losing weeks" is what a funded trader says in his own head on a red afternoon. Most of the niche writes for people who want to get rich; he writes for someone already in the seat and already down. That instinct is the hard part and he has it.

## Contradiction to resolve before we send anything

> ⚠️ Contradiction: his 48.8x outlier is a payout-page thumbnail. [[content-engine/packaging-rules]] rule 5 says **no income screenshots as thumbnails**, and [[concepts/trust-recession]] lists income screenshots as an actively harmful signal. His single best-performing video is the one that breaks our rule.

I am not resolving this unilaterally. The two readings:

1. **Hold the rule.** The 48.8x came from the topic (consistent payouts), not from the screenshot itself. Keep payout proof inside the video and in the thumbnail *text*, never as a pasted income screenshot.
2. **Narrow the rule.** A prop-firm payout ledger with account IDs, dates and a third-party platform behind it is a verifiable record rather than a flex screenshot, which is the thing trust-recession actually targets.

Every rebuild below is written to reading 1, because that is the rule as it currently stands. If you take reading 2, the backgrounds change and the wiki page needs updating.

## Production notes

Trading audiences on funded accounts are money-motivated and time-poor, so run the B2B editing standard from [[content-engine/packaging-rules]]: no jump cuts, no heavy music, clear speech, minimal effects. Production flair reads as a content farm to someone deciding whether to trust a stranger with risk advice.

Every rebuilt title needs a hook that confirms the promise inside the first thirty seconds, not a setup. For P1 that means opening on the loss, not on how his week started.

Backgrounds move off the bed. Rule 4 of the thumbnail set wants a background that signals authority, and a bedroom with a closet in shot is the single fastest way to read as an amateur at 120px.
