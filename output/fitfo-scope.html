The first 30 days

Everything below is built and handed over in the first month. You keep all of it whether we carry on or not.

01
Packaging, built and systemised Your five existing videos repackaged, titles and thumbnails, files delivered ready to upload. Then the system behind it, so every video after this one gets packaged the same way without you guessing.
02
The content bank A pillar map plus 30 titles banked against it, drawn from what you already do every week. No more sitting blank at your desk deciding what to film, and no more making the same video three times.
03
The offer, named and structured What you sell, to who, at what price, and what they actually get. Built around the fact that you trade at a bank and cannot run anything live during the day, so the format has to work asynchronously from the start.
04
The path from video to conversation Where a viewer goes when a video ends. Link, page, form, and the wording that gets them to use it. Built and live, not a recommendation.
05
Tracking So both of us can see what came from where, and neither of us is guessing later about what the content actually produced.

The 30 is a team, not a fee. You are not paying me to keep advising you, you are paying for the people who run a launch so you do not have to take sales calls between market sessions. If nothing sells, the 30 is zero.

What I need from you

  • Your real numbers. Payout figures and the drawdown screenshot, so the packaging uses what actually happened rather than approximations.
  • A cadence you will hold. Five posts a day on Instagram, one video a week. One a week you keep beats four in a burst.
  • Visibility at launch. So the split is calculated off real numbers.

Not included

  • Video editing. You keep whoever you use, or we sort it separately.
  • Being on camera. Yours, and the part nobody can do for you.
  • Ad spend. All of this is organic. Paid comes later, if at all.

Everything above is a floor, not a forecast. The 120 days assumes you hold the cadence. If you do not, no structure fixes that, which is the one part of this I cannot do for you.
