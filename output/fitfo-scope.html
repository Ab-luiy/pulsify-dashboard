What I actually do

Onboarding is week one. After that the build runs alongside your posting rather than holding it up, and it keeps running for as long as we work together.

01
Intake and brand brain Week one. Voice notes and a structured interview until I have your story end to end, how you actually trade, your voice, and what you would teach. It goes into a knowledge base that everything else gets built out of, and you keep it.
02
Instagram rebuilt to convert Bio, highlights, pinned proof, and the profile itself, so the account turns attention into conversations instead of collecting followers. Your reels barely change. What changes is everything around them.
03
The content engine Weekly angles researched and ranked against your positioning, packaging on every upload, scripts you talk from rather than read. Plus a standing bank so you never sit at your desk deciding what to film.
04
YouTube built out properly Channel structure, the packaging system, and your existing videos repackaged and handed back ready to upload. One a week, built to compound rather than to chase a spike.
05
Your offer and your mechanism What you sell, to who, at what price, and the named method behind it. Built around the fact that you trade at a bank and cannot run anything live during the day, so it has to work asynchronously from day one.
06
Numbers and a weekly review What worked, what to cut, what to do more of, every week. This is the part that separates a channel that compounds from one that plateaus, and it is the part almost nobody does.

What I need from you

  • Your real numbers. Payout figures and the drawdown screenshot, so the packaging uses what actually happened rather than approximations.
  • A cadence you will hold. Five posts a day on Instagram, one video a week. One a week you keep beats four in a burst.
  • Visibility at launch. So the split is calculated off real numbers.

Not included

  • Video editing. You keep whoever you use, or we sort it separately.
  • Being on camera. Yours, and the part nobody can do for you.
  • Ad spend. All of this is organic. Paid comes later, if at all.
  • Software and program fees. Anything the build runs on that carries its own subscription is yours. I will keep the stack as small as it can be.

Everything above is a floor, not a forecast. The 120 days assumes you hold the cadence. If you do not, no structure fixes that, which is the one part of this I cannot do for you.
