## Cut entirely

- The `+120.4R` observation. Nobody can even see it. It is a detail masquerading as a finding.
- `PACKAGED UPLOAD / 01`, `04 / 06` and similar counter labels. Reads as AI slop.
- The repeated GBP 99 x 2 arithmetic. State the money situation **once**, briefly, never three times.
- Section headings and body copy that run long. Cut the fat hard.

## The spine, restated

The whole asset answers one question: **how does he perform better on YouTube so he gets more clients.** Everything that does not serve that comes out.

He already has a good landing page. It needs marketing, a slight redesign, and his existing recorded testimonials on it. It does not need rebuilding.

## Give built assets, do not lecture

Shape of every section: here is the thing we made you, here is what you currently have, here is the difference, here is how it should perform.

- Six titles, and they must not read as AI-written. Humanise them.
- The thumbnail insight, with compare and contrast against his current ones.
- Projected performance where it can be honestly stated.

Three examples, then **one block of well-written prose** that ties them together. Not five separate lectures.

## Description: three links maximum

1. His landing page
2. His Instagram
3. Optional third

No Discord link. Kill the `linktw.in` shortener, it reads as suspicious. The current "Join the Best Trading Team" arrangement goes entirely.

## Bio

Must not sound AI-written. `Day trader, UK. Mechanical, not discretionary.` is dead on arrival because nobody talks like that.

Keep it plain and human. The one idea worth keeping is that his students take the same trades he does, with the YouTube link beneath it. Source the approach from the Niksetting bio extraction, not from instinct.

## Highlights: PERMANENT STANDARD, applies to every creator from here

Founder directive, explicitly for Berg **and everybody after him**:

1. **Start here** (or **New here?**) — orients a first-time visitor
2. **My Story**
3. **RESULTS** — and it is spammed with nothing but results

Highlights named `YouTube`, `Testimonials`, `Few Certs`, `Few Trades` get removed. They are not jobs, they are folders.

## Order of sections: easiest fixes first

Attention span is the constraint. Open with what he can fix in ten minutes, then move to the heavier work. Do not lead with the biggest lift.

## The two accounts

- The 10K account is **him**.
- The second is for the **mentorship / offer**.
- There is a third account with ~3.5K members which is the community and offer.

Do not write flowery lines like "every payout, every JJ". State the split plainly.

**Open question for Berg, not for us to answer:** why three accounts exist at all, and whether one is shadowbanned. Ask it, do not assume it.

## The VSL, rebuilt properly

The current version is wrong. "Say what you'll do for them in one line" is not how these are written.

It must be:
- His **story and transformation**, told properly
- Written to the **viewer's pain points**, because the viewer arrives after consuming hours of his content and needs one more step
- A smooth transition into the body, then the CTA
- The CTA's only job is booking the call
- Tangible and real, never invented on the spot

Write it from `client-vsl-writer-prompt.md` plus the dissected real VSLs, not from the six-step skeleton alone.

## The Typeform defect, new finding worth including

His application form asks for contact details at the **end**, around question 8, after seven questions. Anyone can answer everything and walk before handing over details, and that loss is invisible to him.

Contact fields belong at **question 2 or 3**, required.
