Pick your path

  1. 1Record once: your long-form video, a workshop or a 20 minute talk to camera.
  2. 2Mark the moments people stayed for: a sharp claim, a result, a mistake named.
  3. 3Cut each moment into a 20 to 45 second reel with the claim in the first line.
  4. 4Post one a day. Plan the recording day and the clip days on your calendar.

What to make

  • Clips from your long-form
  • Raw talking head on one strategy
  • A result explained

Every thought goes down one of these paths before it becomes a piece.

  • A winCredibility sequence or proof reel

Reframe rotator

One belief, five ways to say it. Change the shape, keep the message.

  • reelA 20-second talking head with the belief stated plainly.
  • storyA value-driven sequence that walks through one profile fix.
  • carouselA saveable philosophy or taxonomy set.
  • long-formA deeper YouTube segment with the full mechanism.
  • hookOne bare hook to test the belief without building the full asset yet.
