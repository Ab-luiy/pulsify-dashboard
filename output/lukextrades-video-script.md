# Luke, DM video script

90 seconds to 2 minutes. Screen recording of **his** account with your voice over it. Record in Loom if that is convenient, then download the mp4 and send the file directly in the Instagram DM. Do not send a Loom link; Instagram plays an uploaded video inline, and a link is a click out of the app to a stranger's domain.

No slides, no intro card, no face cam needed. Start recording with his channel already open.

## Rules for the recording

- Talk over his screen the entire time. The moment you cut to slides or a face cam it becomes a pitch.
- Never say a number about how badly something is doing without immediately saying why. "Forty views" alone is an insult; "forty views because nobody searches a date" is a diagnosis.
- No compliment at the top. "Love your content" is what everyone opens with and it reads as setup.
- No ask at the end. The doc link carries whatever comes next, same as the written version.
- One take. A clean second take sounds rehearsed, and rehearsed sounds like sales.
- Under two minutes. If it runs over, cut the landing page section and leave it for the doc.

## The message it goes in

> yo made you a quick video, 90 seconds. mostly about why that alpha futures one did 5k and the rest didn't. full thing's written up here if you want it [link]
