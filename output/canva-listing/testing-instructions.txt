How to test Pulsify with Canva

1. Go to https://pulsify-ai.com/portal/login and sign in with the reviewer account.
   Email: (from CANVA_REVIEW_EMAIL in the Brain .env)
   Password: (from CANVA_REVIEW_PASSWORD in the Brain .env)
2. Open Create, choose Carousel, type any topic, press Continue through the steps, then Make it.
   (If generation is slow, wait about a minute. The carousel opens in our editor.)
3. In the editor, press "Edit in Canva" (top right). The first time, Canva asks you to allow Pulsify.
   Pulsify asks for two permissions only: design:content:read and design:content:write.
4. Your carousel opens as a new design in Canva with editable text. Edit anything, then press Return in Canva.
5. You land back in Pulsify and your edited slides appear in the editor. "Undo" returns the earlier version.

Notes: the reviewer account has no real client data. Posting to Instagram is not part of the Canva flow.
