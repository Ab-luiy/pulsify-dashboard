No slides, no intro card, no face cam. Start recording with his YouTube channel already open on the videos tab.

**Have these open in tabs before you hit record:** his channel, one of the concept thumbnails at full size (the jpgs are already in `Desktop\Prospect Assets\tb-tradez\`), his Instagram grid, and the doc.

## The 1:89 call

The doc mentions it in one clause and moves on. On camera with a cursor on it, it stops being an observation and becomes "I caught you," and a nineteen year old who feels caught doesn't reply.

My read: say it once, flat, no zoom, after the argument about the number rather than before it — so the point is already made and this is just a detail. If you're recording and it feels sharp in your mouth, cut it. The number argument does the whole job without it, and nothing else in the video depends on it.

Never say fabricated, fake, or edited. Not in the video, not in the DM, not if he asks.

## Rules for the recording

- Talk over his screen the whole time. The moment you cut to slides or a face cam it's a pitch.
- Never say a number about something doing badly without saying why in the same breath. "Eleven hundred views" on its own is an insult. "Eleven hundred views because nothing connects it to the reel that did a hundred thousand" is a diagnosis.
- No compliment at the top. Everyone opens with one and it reads as setup.
- No ask at the end. The doc carries whatever comes next.
- One take. A clean second take sounds rehearsed and rehearsed sounds like sales.
- Two minutes. If it runs long, cut the objectives section to two sentences — do not cut the thumbnails, that's the part he can see.

## The message it goes in

> yo made you a quick video, 2 mins. mostly about the four thumbnails and why that 101k one didn't turn into followers. whole thing's written up here if you want it [link]
