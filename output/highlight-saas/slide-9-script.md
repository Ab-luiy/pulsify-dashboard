# Slide 9: founder video script (phone, one take, 25 to 35 seconds)

Slide 9 sits between slide 8 ("you don't have to be a guru, you just have to sound like you") and slide 10 ("now you sit down and you already know what to make"). No motion graphic. Just you, to camera.

## Read-it-straight version (about 28 seconds)

I've spent this year inside creators' businesses.

Every one of them was great at what they do. And every one of them got stuck in the same place: turning that skill into something that actually runs.

So I built the thing that runs it.

It writes in your voice. It shows you which post made you money. It shows you who's closing and who's not.

One place. Everything I've seen work, behind you.

You keep doing what you're good at.

Now watch.

## Delivery notes

- Lead with the lens, not the phone. Phone at eye level, window light in front of you, not behind.
- One take, no cuts needed. Pause a full second after "So I built the thing that runs it." Slow down on "Now watch."
- Short sentences. Heroic comes from slowing down at the turn, not from getting louder.
- Keep it under 35 seconds. If you ramble, restart from beat 3, which is the pivot.

## Do not say

- No income, results or client numbers. None are approved for this.
- No "guaranteed", "changes everything", "never seen before".
- No "years" of anything beyond what you can stand behind ("this year" is true).
- No call to action. The ask is just watch.

## Long version, "the wrong metric" (about 70 seconds, SooWei-style flip translated to the app)

SooWei's pitch moves used (from the vault extractions, his claims, structure only): flip the metric from views to attributed booked calls; show instead of tell; cash per follower over follower count. Product claims limited to what slides 10 to 12 show. No transcript objection mining (not confirmed live). No income figures. Ends on a statement, no "watch".

1. The flip. Views are the wrong way to judge your content. Everyone checks views, likes, followers; none say if a post made a dollar. A 5K-view post can bring in more booked calls than a 500K one, and you never find out because nothing connects the two.
2. The gap. You post, a call books, someone pays, and you don't know which post started it. Next week you guess and make more of what got attention, which isn't what pays you.
3. The turn. So I built the thing that connects them.
4. What it does. Every post tracked from first view to payment: who watched, how far, who clicked, who booked, who paid, down to the video. It writes in your voice from how you talk. On the sales side you see who's closing and who's not, every setter and closer, one place.
5. The reframe. You stop asking what's trending and ask what's paying. A small audience that buys beats a big one that watches; cash per follower matters more than follower count.
6. The close. Built from everything I've seen work, every framework tested before it went in. You keep doing what you're good at. The system tells you where it's paying.

Anchors: views are the wrong metric / you can't tell which post did it / so I built the thing that connects them / post to payment, your voice, who's closing / make more of what pays / you keep doing what you're good at. Minimum cut: beats 3, 4, 6.

## "What happens when you work with me" version (modeled on SooWei's IG walkthrough, founder-supplied 2026-10-08)

Structure taken from the founder's pasted SooWei transcript: one hook line promising the exact process, then three numbered steps (get in the app and answer onboarding questions, then a roadmap call; analyze KPIs and fix the lowest-hanging fruit before touching top-of-funnel content; amplify with the content system), then a short close. His specifics (named clients, $200K a month, a weekly account manager, 150 posts a month) are NOT used. Pulsify steps map to what is live: onboarding form into the client brain (17 questions, 7 parts), tracking post to payment, closer and setter numbers, voice-matched writing, "we work through it with you".

Script (about 75 seconds):

Here's exactly what happens when you work with me.

Step one. You get access to the app and answer a set of questions: who you are, what you sell, who you sell to, how you talk. Everything after this is built from your answers, including the voice. Then we get on a call and I go through it with you, so you know exactly how to attract the right customer and what makes you different from everyone else in your niche.

Step two. We look at your numbers. Your offer, your funnel, your sales calls, where people drop off. We fix the cheapest leak first, because there's no point pouring more content into a business that loses people further down. In the app, every post is tracked from the first view to the payment, and you can see who's closing and who's not.

Step three. We turn it up. With the foundation right, the app writes in your voice and tells you what to make next. You sit down already knowing what to say, and you record it. And every week we get on a call, look at what's paying, and make more of that.

Three steps. You keep doing what you're good at, and the system does the rest.

Anchors: here's exactly what happens / step one: app, questions, sit down together / step two: numbers, cheapest leak first, tracked to payment / step three: writes in your voice, make more of what pays / you keep doing what you're good at.

Founder confirmed 2026-10-08: he goes through onboarding on a call and reviews weekly. Unconfirmed: that step two (offer, funnel, calls, cheapest leak first) is what he does on every client.
