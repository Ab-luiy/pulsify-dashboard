02 / Two businesses

Copy and design notes

Existing copy you're already good at what you do. trading. tiktok shop. whatever it is. and you know a mentorship can make you more than the thing itself. but info is a whole business on its own. so now you're learning two businesses at once.

Mockup copy you're already good at what you do. trading. tiktok shop. whatever it is. now you want to teach it. but a mentorship is a business of its own: content, an offer, a way to sell it. so now you're learning two businesses at once.

Proposed rewrite: removes the unsupported comparison of mentorship earnings with craft earnings. Whole café photo; type supplies the comparison.

03 / The whole chain

Copy and design notes

Existing copy content. traffic. a page. sales calls. a team. cash in the bank. that's the whole chain. most people have never run it. some don't even know what a kpi is. and that's fine. nobody teaches you this part.

Mockup copy content. traffic. a page. sales calls. a team. cash in the bank. that's the whole chain. knowing your craft doesn't teach you how to run all of this. that's the part you have to build.

Proposed rewrite: retains the chain, removes the KPI put-down; follows Claude’s numbered-list composition.

04 / Where it breaks

Copy and design notes

Existing copy it breaks somewhere: - you want to post, but you don't know what to say - you know what to say, but nobody's watching - people are watching, but you don't know where to send them - calls are booked, but you can't tell who's closing and who isn't

Mockup copy it breaks somewhere: you want to post, but don't know what to say. you post, but nobody's watching. people watch, but you don't know where to send them. calls get booked, but you can't see what's happening after. one missing step is enough to stop the next one.

Proposed concise diagnosis; no additional numerical claims. Keeps the founder work-photo background.

05 / The conversation gets lost

Copy and design notes

Existing copy you post something. a couple of people dm you. you lose half of them in the chat. the ones who book don't close. and you have no idea which post brought any of them in.

Mockup copy you post something. a couple of people dm you. some conversations go nowhere. others never make it onto a call. and when someone does buy, you can't tell which post brought them in.

Proposed rewrite: removes “lose half” and the invented 40/20/8 chart. A conversation path explains the leak without posing as measured data.

06 / Try harder

Copy and design notes

Existing copy so you try harder. more posts. more tools. another chatgpt tab at 2am. you start copying what the big names do. and you end up promoting their format instead of building your own. meanwhile the thing that actually pays you gets less of your time.

Mockup copy so you try harder. more posts. more tools. another chatgpt tab at 2am. you start copying what the big names do. their format takes over. your own voice gets harder to hear. meanwhile, the thing that pays you gets less of your time.

Illustrative browser, not a real chat or testimonial. Carries Claude’s late-night artifact direction without the pile of tool labels.

07 / The cost

Copy and design notes

Existing copy that's where most people plateau. then they pick the main business, because that's what keeps food on the table. and the info side dies quietly. none of that is your fault.

Mockup copy eventually, you go back to the business that pays the bills. the mentorship gets pushed to next week. then next month. you need a way to build it alongside what you already do.

Proposed rewrite: replaces the generic plateau graph and blanket absolution with the practical two-business cost. Deliberately quiet type-led pause.

08 / Your own voice

Copy and design notes

Existing copy here's what most people miss. the top of every niche is 3 or 4 names. but new people break in every single month. not because they're bigger. because they bring something new. people always want a new face with a new idea. "you don't need to be first. you need to sound like you."

Mockup copy you can still bring something new. your experience. your way of explaining it. the problems you see every day. you don't need to be first. you need to sound like you. that gives the content a direction.

Proposed rewrite: drops universal niche rankings/monthly entrant claims. Keeps the approved quote as the dominant visual, with the walking photo whole.

11 / The sales side

Copy and design notes

Existing copy then the sales side. you see who's closing. who's not. who's hitting their calls. who's hitting your numbers. no more guessing what happened on a call. no scripts to write yourself.

Mockup copy then the sales side. you can see what happens after a call gets booked. calls, closes, cash collected. the numbers your team works against.

Story-scale illustrative arrangement of shipped sales metric fields, explicitly labelled; no invented performance values or fake conversations.

12 / See what converts

Copy and design notes

Existing copy and every post is tracked. who watched, who clicked, who booked, who paid. "you never post blind again."

Mockup copy tracked links connect content to leads and matched payments. you can see what turns into a sale. so the next post starts with something to learn from.

Actual saved funnel UI, cropped into readable stage groups. Proposed copy separates tracked attribution from identifying every anonymous viewer. Removes sample $63,000 headline.

Copy and design notes
