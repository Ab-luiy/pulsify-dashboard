# Pre-launch v2 — proposed founder copy

Nine-frame draft responding to the founder’s October 8 feedback. Existing locked copy preserved.

## 1. All year

i've been working on this this whole year, right up until today. with the creators i've worked with, i kept seeing the same problem. i couldn't find what i wanted out there. so i started building it.

Design: Founder-led opening; earlier behind-subject logo retained, new words replace the elapsed-year claim.

## 2. Two businesses

being good at your thing is only half of it. a mentorship is a whole business of its own. i watched people try to build the second while keeping the first one running.

Design: Full-bleed work photo, natural cover crop. Constructed craft-versus-business visual, no mirrored photo extension or numerical performance claim.

## 3. The whole chain

the whole chain. personal branding: people start buying into you because you solve their niche problems. traffic. a landing page: VSL, webinar, profile funnel, ascension or challenge funnel. talented setters and closers. cash in the bank.

Design: The requested detailed square-board scene. Payment marks show category examples, not a claim that all three are integrated. Avatar crowd is conceptual. Descriptions demonstrate depth without asserting measured results.

## 4. Why I built it

the pieces exist. they don't connect. what do i post? where do i send them? who follows up? what actually sold? that's what i wanted to fix: the gaps between all of it.

Design: Every floating object names a practical missing handoff. Native diagram replaces a longer run of pain narration.

## 5. Keep your voice

i don't want you sounding like another copy of someone else. you've got your own experience. i want the content to sound like you.

Design: Shorter replacement for the prior crowded slide8. Closing phrase fits one line; person head remains clear, natural full-bleed crop.

## 6. Content that comes from you

your experience, turned into content. this is the part i care about most: getting what's in your head into something the right person wants to watch.

Design: Advertising-scale redesigned Voice and Content surfaces. The example angle is authored illustrative content, not a claim of a generated client asset. The waveform encodes voice as a conceptual relationship. Mark is a product object.

## 7. A sales side you can see

the call ends. the work keeps moving. i wanted you to see what happens after someone books: who takes the call, what happened, what comes next.

Design: Sales Co-pilot mechanisms rebuilt as a cinematic brief-to-follow-up composition. Five-touch dates reflect shipped logic. Review requirement is visible; no autonomous sending or invented rep results.

## 8. Follow the money back

the post. the person. the payment. i wanted to know which content actually brought someone in, so the next post isn't a blind guess.

Design: Exploded content-to-person-to-matched-payment scene. No revenue amount, synthetic performance graph or claim of identifying anonymous viewers. Semantics follow first-touch cash-by-video attribution.

## 9. Why this matters to me

i'm putting everything into this because i want you to keep doing the thing you're good at. we work through the business with you. pulsify is where it all comes together. it's still getting sharper, and i'm not stopping.

Design: Personal service-led close. Proposed passion copy reflects founder direction without inventing users, measured results, scarcity or a particular personal event. Recording remains separate.
