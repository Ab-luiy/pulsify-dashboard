# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Trading, ecom/dropshipping, Amazon FBA, and TikTok Shop content creators — Pulsify's
outreach prospects. Early-stage creators with a real audience but a broken or missing
backend (no VSL, no application flow, no email follow-up). They land on this page from a
cold DM, often on mobile, with zero prior relationship with Pulsify. The job: recognize
themselves and their offer instantly, watch one short private-feeling video, and apply.

## Product Purpose

This is Pulsify's outreach asset, not a generic template: a free, fully-built landing
page sent unsolicited to a prospect as proof of capability (the "build before you DM"
method). Success means the prospect notices Pulsify actually studied their content,
replies, and eventually becomes a client. It's also the reusable STANDARD every future
prospect/client landing page is built from (see `wiki/synthesis/landing-page-standard.md`).

## Brand Personality

Premium, controlled, proof-led. Expensive because it's restrained, not because it's
decorated. No hype voice, no lifestyle flexing, no invented claims — proof does the
selling. Confident and direct, closer to an operator's own tone than a marketing agency's.

## Anti-references

- Canva-style result-stat callouts (loud colored boxes wrapped around a number) — cheap,
  templated, avoid.
- Big colored "card" treatment on the proof/results section — should blend into the
  page, not shout.
- AI-slop placeholder copy: explanatory meta-text sitting on proof tiles ("Drop Frank's
  original payment screenshot here", "Nothing below invents a testimonial") — reads as
  generated boilerplate, not a real page.
- Generic throwaway headline copy with no specific claim (flagged directly by the user:
  "Build the store into an asset, not another daily reset" was rejected outright).
- The AI tells catalogued in the vault's Humanizer standard: no "not X, it's Y", no
  bold-first bullets, no rule-of-three padding, no em-dash overuse, no "here's the thing."

## Design Principles

1. Nothing competes above the video — no education, no mechanism explainer, no stat
   strip before the fold.
2. Proof is the backbone. A dense, real "testimonial bombardment" (Mason Bowstead's own
   term) beats a few polished quote cards, but the density must read as real screenshots,
   not labeled placeholder slots.
3. One accent color, one CTA, repeated. Restraint signals expense.
4. Every claim stays inside what's actually verifiable from the prospect's public
   material — this is a demo built to look already live, not a fictional mockup.
5. Scope note: this file describes the outreach landing-page product specifically
   (`output/standard-preview/`, the future `assets/landing-page-standard/` template). It
   does not describe the wider Pulsify-Brain vault, which also contains a separate
   product-register CRM/dashboard surface (`pulsify-dashboard`).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA baseline — no specific accommodation requirement given. Body text
contrast and readable type sizes on mobile matter most in practice, since prospects open
this page from a DM on their phone.
