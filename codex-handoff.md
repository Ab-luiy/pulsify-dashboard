## What still needs to be locked

### **1. The deliverable — what does the first engagement actually produce?**

The outreach playbook demos one asset per wound to get the call. But what does the signed engagement actually deliver? The 9-prompt launch pack implies a ~12-day buildout, but the specific deliverable list for a Pulsify engagement has never been documented.

What does the coach receive?
- How many YouTube videos (script? raw recording? edited + captioned? uploaded to their channel?)
- VSL + landing page (Lovable build? handed over or hosted by Pulsify?)
- Typeform (transferred to their account?)
- Email sequences (how many? pre-call only? welcome + nurture? in what tool?)
- ManyChat automation (trigger word, qualification flow, handoff)
- Story sequences
- DM templates for their setter
- Anything else?

This list does not exist in the vault. It needs to exist before `pulse-system.md` can be written.

### **2. The order — in what sequence do these assets ship?**

Does Pulse Engine always ship before Pulse Brain? Or does that change based on which wound Marcus walked in with? The 16-step pulsify-order-of-operations exists but was written before the five-wound framing and before the Pulse System was named. It may need reconciliation.

### **3. The promise — what does Marcus actually get?**

The call structure says "conservative path to $60K/mo in 90 days" for a $20K/mo coach. That's a 3x claim. Is that the actual promise Pulsify makes? Or is it framed differently?

The outreach closer uses: "stop guessing forever." The VSL architecture says "do not promise revenue Pulsify cannot prove."

These need to be reconciled into one locked promise statement.

### **4. Why "Pulse" — what does the metaphor mean operationally?**

This matters for the concept page introduction and for the call walkthrough. "Pulse" = what exactly? Heartbeat of the business? Rhythmic output (consistent content cadence, consistent lead flow)? Something else? If it's just a name with no operational meaning, say so and we move on. If it has meaning, it should be in the concept page.

### **5. Ownership / handoff — client acquisition context only**

For the first deal (5-day setup), does the client own everything Pulsify builds? Or does Pulsify retain admin access as part of an ongoing arrangement? This only matters at close, but it will come up on the call.
