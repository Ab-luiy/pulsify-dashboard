## Email 4 — "Second-order thinking"

"And then what?"

That simple question can be the difference between prosperity and poverty. It can lead you to potential problems before anyone else finds them. If you solve those problems, you might get rich.

But most people never think deeply enough to consider second order consequences.

Here's an example…

First-order consequence: "This girl is hot. I want to date her."
Second-order consequence: "She keyed my car, stole my Rolex and threw a chair off my balcony."

If you considered the second order consequence from the beginning, you probably wouldn't have made decision number one.

Make sense? Cool.

The same principle applies to business, especially with AI. Hold on now, don't click off yet – I know you're probably sick of those two letters.

Most agency owners, copywriters, and marketers are thinking… "Oh this is really cool, AI will do my work for me hurr durr." And what's the reaction to this? Their clients realize they don't need them. Low skill, basic service providers get replaced.

But this doesn't mean you're doomed… Because high level service providers become indispensable.

An online coach (or really any business owner) doesn't need doers. They need thinkers who can identify bottlenecks, strategize, and steer the ship… While leveraging AI to improve efficiency, quality, and everything in between.

In simple terms, this means… Information gets commoditized. Everyone will have the same tools. "I can build your funnel" is not a MOAT. And differentiation comes through thinking, experience, and context.

People who understand psychology, systems, and nuances will use AI to multiply their impact. Everyone else will get drowned out.

Pricing power shifts to those who own outcomes, not just provide services.

Imagine you build and manage the entire back-end of a $200k/month info biz. You track KPIs, manage the team, improve conversions, and build the whole system. You're not an expense, you're a partner. And that's why they'll pay you $40k/mo, instead of $2k/mo.

Meanwhile, the "AI agency, copywriter and arbitrageurs" are fighting over scraps, praying someone throws them a $1,500 bone.

All the tools are accessible now. The difference is the application and positioning. If you can tie yourself to revenue, AI becomes your leverage not your replacement.
