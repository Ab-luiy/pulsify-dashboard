## THE 6-STEP AI OUTREACH SYSTEM

---

### STEP 1 — FIND THE RIGHT PROSPECTS

**Ideal client profile:**
- Creator/coach who already has an audience and an offer
- Monetization is broken or underperforming — they have attention, they lack systems
- They're already making some money (even with bad infrastructure) — there's always more to extract

**Best niches to target:**
- Trading, real estate, ecom, dropshipping, Amazon FBA, TikTok shop — anything make-money-online with a high-ticket offer attached
- These audiences have a direct ROI mindset — easier to sell a $5K trading program than a fitness plan because the buyer sees a financial return
- Avoid "poverty niches" (fitness, dating, woo-woo) when starting out — not that they don't work, they require higher-level expertise to convert

**How to build a prospect list using AI:**

Prompt Claude:
> "Search YouTube and Instagram for people selling [niche] mentorships, actively posting in the last 30 days, with 1,000–20,000 subscribers or followers, averaging over 2,000 views per video. Give me a list with their channel and account links, audience size, and engagement stats."

Refine the output — AI won't be perfect on the first pass. Ask: "What information would make these results more accurate?" Feed it back and iterate until you have 20–30 highly qualified prospects.

**What to filter for:**
- Don't just look at follower count — look at what they're actually selling
- Do they have a landing page? Is it optimized?
- Do they have a VSL?
- The more broken their monetization infrastructure, the bigger your opportunity

### STEP 2 — THE 15-MINUTE RESEARCH PROTOCOL

Most people skip this. Don't. Every outreach must be custom and personalized — this is the step that makes that possible.

**Part 1 — Funnel hack (5 minutes)**
Go through their funnel as if you're a lead looking to purchase. Set a stopwatch the moment you hit submit/apply. If a setter/closer doesn't reach out within 5 minutes, their speed-to-lead is broken — show rate and close rate are suffering. Track:
- Landing page quality and copy
- VSL presence and quality
- Typeform structure (too long? clunky? letting unqualified leads through?)
- Email follow-up sequences (do they have them?)
- Confirmation page (are they nurturing leads before the call or just sending a calendar link?)

**Part 2 — Content audit (5 minutes)**
Go to their YouTube, Instagram, or wherever they post. Ask:
- Are they driving traffic to their offer effectively?
- Do they have a full funnel of top-of-funnel, middle-of-funnel, and bottom-of-funnel content — or are they only doing one type?
- Are the views converting to revenue, or just views?

Grab a YouTube transcript, feed it to AI with the prompt: "Analyze their content strategy. Are they driving traffic effectively to their offer? What is missing? What could they do better to convert viewers into buyers?" — get a full funnel restructure recommendation.

**Part 3 — Identify the specific opportunity (5 minutes)**
Get precise. Not "improve their funnel." Pick one or two things:
- Rebuild their landing page?
- Script a new VSL?
- Build an optimized Typeform?
- Set up a confirmation page + pre-call asset sequence?
- Write a 6-email follow-up cadence?

The more specific, the more powerful the outreach. With AI, you're not just identifying the opportunity — you're about to build it.

### STEP 3 — BUILD THE ASSET BEFORE YOU REACH OUT

This is the AI advantage. Almost nobody is doing this. Build a real, tangible, personalized deliverable for the prospect before sending a single message.

**What you can build in minutes:**

**Landing page:**
Use Lovable AI (or Claude to generate the prompt, then Lovable to build it).
Prompt structure:
> "Create a high-converting landing page for [niche] mentorship targeting [audience]. Their current page is weak on social proof, has no clear urgency. Here are the offer details: [details]. Build a version with strong copy, a clear CTA, and a compelling headline. Model it after [competitor's landing page URL] but unique to them."

Feed it their offer details, testimonials, and results from their existing page. Take the top competitor in their niche, grab their landing page link, add it to the prompt — replicate the highest-converting page in the niche, customized to this person.

**VSL script:**
Use Claude with the proven VSL framework prompt (based on Jeremy Haynes' 6-step VSL structure). Feed it their offer, audience, results, and story. The output will be a structured, high-converting VSL script personalized to their business. Even experienced operators are impressed by what Claude produces here.

**Optimized Typeform:**
Most creators have no application process or a badly structured one — letting unqualified, broke leads book calls and waste closer time.
Prompt:
> "Build a Typeform for [niche] coaching program that filters unqualified leads, pre-sells the prospect on the call, gauges intent, and ensures everyone who books is ready to buy."

Claude will build a full functional Typeform (not just a Google Doc with questions) — complete with UI — that you can send as a live link immediately.

**Pre-call asset / Confirmation page:**
Most coaches send a basic calendar confirmation email and nothing else. That's why their show rate suffers and their sales cycle is long.
Prompt:
> "Create a pre-call asset for [niche] coaching sales call. A sequence of short video scripts (45 seconds each) that pre-handle common objections: [list objections]. Include confirmation copy covering what to expect on the call, social proof from past students, answers to common objections, and content that gets them excited and ready to move forward."

Claude scripts the breakout videos. Take those scripts back to Lovable to build a full confirmation page with assets — all done before you ever reach out.

**The logic:** If someone showed up in your DMs and said "I built a landing page for your business — want to see it?" you would look every single time. That's the reaction you're generating.

### STEP 4 — THE ACTUAL OUTREACH

**Message 1 — Lead with the deliverable, skip permission**

Never ask "want me to send it over?" — that signals you're like everyone else. You've already done the work. Send it.

Format:
> "Yo [name], went through your funnel and put together a [VSL script / rebuilt landing page / optimized Typeform] specifically for your offer — sending it over now."

Then send the asset link immediately after. No buildup, no bait-and-switch. Show up, deliver value, move on.

**Message 2 — Show don't tell (if using a broad opener first)**

If you led with a casual opener to get a response first:
> "Hey, just went through your entire funnel — not going to lie, [what you found was broken]. Spent a few minutes putting together [what you built] based on what I found. It's personalized to your offer and your audience. Take a look, G."

Send the asset with this message.

**Why this works:**
- Demonstrates real knowledge — proves you actually understand their business
- Proves it's not a copy-paste template — it's specific to their niche, offer, audience, and gaps
- Creates immediate value before they've paid you a cent
- Impossible to ignore if the asset is good

### STEP 5 — FOLLOW-UP

Most deals don't close on the first message. Most people give up after 2–3 follow-ups — that's why they stay broke.

**Rule:** If someone replies once, they are interested. Do not stop following up until you've booked the call or exhausted every angle.

**Tactics:**

- **Story slide-up method:** Turn on post notifications for the prospect. The second they post a story, you're alerted they're active on their phone. React to the story immediately and follow up — they're online and will see it.

- **Win screenshot method:** When a current client has a big result (25K day, 100K week), send the screenshot to everyone in your follow-up pipeline with: "Going to keep hitting you up until you either block me or we start seeing days like this." Bold and direct.

- **AI-assisted follow-up:** Feed the conversation history to Claude with: "Here's my conversation with [prospect]. They replied once but went cold. What follow-up angle should I try next? Give me three options based on what's worked so far." Test and iterate.

Key fact: One of the biggest clients ever landed required over a year of follow-up. The money is in the follow-up.

### STEP 6 — THE CONSULTATION CALL / CLOSE

**Call structure:**

1. **Build rapport (3–5 minutes):** Casual conversation. You're a partner, not a salesperson. Calm, confident, authoritative.

2. **Diagnose their situation:** Ask questions that show you know what you're talking about. Show up with a notepad, take notes. Let them talk. The more they share, the easier the close. Key questions: What's working? What numbers are you hitting? What's the biggest thing holding you back from where you want to go?

3. **Present your solution:** Walk them through what you already built. Show them why you made each decision — why the VSL is structured that way, why the landing page is set up that way. They've seen previews in the DMs. Now show the full picture of what their business looks like when it's all running properly.

4. **Project revenue:** Show conservative, realistic numbers based on their current audience size and what's fixable. Make it concrete. "Right now you're doing X. Fixing the landing page, adding a proper Typeform, and having me manage it full stack — you could realistically go from where you are to Y in 60 days."

5. **Structure the deal:** For new operators — lead with revenue share, no upfront risk. Frame it as: "Worst case: you lose 20% margin for one month and tell me to go away. Best case: you start making way more money and we work together long-term. It's a no-brainer if you know you're leaving money on the table."

## THE 5 BIGGEST OUTREACH MISTAKES

1. **Pitching without giving value** — Never lead with your services. Build something first. Show before you ask.
2. **Skipping the research** — You can't build a personalized asset without understanding the prospect. Do the 15-minute protocol every time. No shortcuts.
3. **Building generic assets** — A landing page that could work for anyone is not impressive. It must be specific to their offer, their audience, and their gaps.
4. **Giving up too early** — If they replied once, they're interested. Follow up relentlessly. Don't stop until you've booked the call.
5. **Expecting AI to think for you** — AI is leverage, not a replacement for your brain. The more context you give it, the better everything it produces. You still need to do the research, be strategic, and provide real input.
