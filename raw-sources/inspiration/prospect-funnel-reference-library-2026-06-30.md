# Prospect funnel reference library — 2026-06-30

These live pages were supplied and approved by Abdul as references for the free
landing pages Pulsify builds for prospects. This is a dated capture. Live funnels
change, so re-check the URLs before using exact page details in a new build.

## Reference hierarchy

1. **Mason Bowstead is the primary design model.** Use the existing capture at
   `raw-sources/vsls/mason-bowstead-funnel-design.md`: clean white/off-white
   GoHighLevel-style page, Inter Tight headlines, one blue CTA, VSL, application,
   and testimonial bombardment.
2. **Luke Alexander is the secondary design model.** Use his AI-native visual
   language when the prospect sells AI, automation, software, or a technology-led
   mechanism. Keep Mason's conversion skeleton underneath it.
3. Charlie Morgan, Grant Garcia, and Ayman Arab are structural references. Pull
   specific mechanics from them. Do not average their visual styles together.

## Charlie Morgan — Imperium / High Ticket Heaven

- URL: https://go.imperiumacquisition.com/imperium-mastermind
- Current wrapper: short, premium B2B application page.
- Visual system: black background, muted gold accents, cream text, geometric line
  texture, uppercase `Archivo Black` headline, narrow centered column.
- Current sequence:
  1. Revenue qualifier badge for B2B businesses doing $500K+ per year.
  2. Mechanism-led headline: plug an eight-figure executive team into the business.
  3. Risk reversal directly under the promise.
  4. One sentence explaining why to watch.
  5. Large VSL player.
  6. Advisory-call calendar.
  7. "Trusted by over 4,000 businesses" and Trustpilot reinforcement.
- Reusable mechanic: qualify before the headline, state the mechanism in the
  headline, put the risk reversal beside the promise, then move straight to VSL
  and calendar.
- Boundary: the dark luxury treatment and large proof claims only work for a
  mature operator with the proof to carry them. This is not Pulsify's default
  prospect-page skin.

## Grant Garcia — The Reserve

- URL: https://start.reserveuniversity.com/
- Current wrapper: minimalist direct-response VSL/application page for beginners.
- Visual system: white background, black sans-serif copy, blue highlight on the
  monetary outcome, centered logo, centered headline, large video thumbnail.
- Current sequence:
  1. The Reserve logo.
  2. One outcome headline aimed at beginners with no experience.
  3. Large tap-to-play VSL.
  4. Application and booking instruction.
  5. Get Started CTA.
  6. "What our clients say."
  7. A very large screenshot-proof wall. The live page contained more than 120
     images at capture time.
  8. Final Get Started CTA.
- Reusable mechanic: no navigation, no competing offer explanation above the
  video, and overwhelming proof volume after the application prompt.
- Boundary: do not copy the beginner opportunity claim or income figures. Pull
  the compression and proof density.

## Luke Alexander — AI Insiders

- URL: https://joininsiders.ai/
- Current wrapper: long-form AI offer page with a VSL-led hero.
- Visual system: white and black, large breathing room, faint technical grid,
  minimalist AI Insiders mark, editorial `Source Serif 4` headline, product and
  system visuals that make the offer feel native to AI rather than like a generic
  coaching funnel.
- Current sequence:
  1. AI Insiders brand mark and VSL-led hero.
  2. Mechanism-heavy promise: client-acquisition systems, prebuilt AI agents, and
     AI implementation across the business.
  3. Hard qualifier: business owners only, not beginners.
  4. "What we will give you day one" asset reveal.
  5. Named system components such as Project Overlord, a custom Hermes agent, and
     120+ AI modules.
  6. Member interviews with revenue labels.
  7. Founder note and credibility.
  8. Large people-we-have-helped proof section.
  9. Repeated single CTA: book a call.
- Reusable mechanic: make an AI creator's offer tangible through named systems,
  interface-style visuals, agent diagrams, and day-one assets. The design should
  signal AI before the copy explains it.
- Boundary: use this as a visual overlay for AI prospects. Do not let the
  technology aesthetic bury the one conversion path.

## Ayman Arab — TikScale

- URL: https://www.tikscale.net/apply-o
- Current wrapper: long-form done-for-you service application page. No embedded
  VSL iframe was detected in the live capture; treat the current URL as a sales
  and application page even if an earlier version used a VSL.
- Visual system: off-white `#f5f5f7`, charcoal copy, `Geist`, restrained product
  design, clean case-study cards, minimal decoration.
- Current sequence:
  1. Audience qualifier.
  2. Outcome headline and done-for-you subhead.
  3. Apply CTA.
  4. Client-logo strip and quantified proof bar.
  5. Flagship case study with before/after.
  6. Multiple audience-stage case studies: established channel, zero audience,
     brand-new channel.
  7. Nine-part delivery breakdown.
  8. More client proof.
  9. Recording-friction section.
  10. Failed alternatives and YouTube buyer-quality argument.
  11. FAQ.
  12. Closing application CTA.
- Reusable mechanic: sequence proof so each prospect stage can find itself, reveal
  the complete delivery system, and change CTA copy to match the section while
  sending every CTA to the same Typeform.
- Boundary: this page is too long for the default free prospect mockup. Use its
  proof and delivery architecture only when the prospect has enough real material
  to support a long page.
