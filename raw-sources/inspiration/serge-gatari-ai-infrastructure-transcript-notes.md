# Serge Gatari - AI infrastructure transcript notes

This source is Serge's clearest public articulation of the AI infrastructure angle. It argues against selling single-use AI agents, workflows, or automations and instead packages AI as a set of connected mechanisms that bridge a business gap.

## Most important takeaways

1. The hook rejects a hot commodity category immediately: single-use AI workflows, automations, and agents.
2. The core mechanism is a set of AI-enabled mechanisms that bridge the whole gap, not one isolated tool.
3. The problem with single-use AI is twofold: it cannot create the full transformation, and it is easy to copy.
4. Serge frames broad functions like marketing, sales, support, and coding as dangerous categories because large model companies can commoditize them.
5. The build-and-release model starts with market and gap, not tool. Pick the niche, find the obstacle, then choose the AI mechanisms.
6. The pricing logic comes from business value, not software cost. The tools may be cheap, but the installed infrastructure can command a premium.
7. The phrase "process selling VSL" is the most important tactical extraction. The VSL shows the gap in the prospect's business and sells the process for closing it.
8. Acquisition sequence: pick a niche, diagnose the gap, set up the process-selling VSL, drive traffic, contact leads in under five minutes, sell upfront implementation.
9. Upfront implementation is framed as stronger than monthly retainer because it improves cash collection and buyer commitment.
10. The CTA leans on platform-shift urgency: early movers capture the upside before supply exceeds demand.

## Pattern notes for Pulsify

The main pattern is "category rejection into infrastructure." Pulsify should reject small categories like AI content, video editing, automation, and funnel building. Then it should reframe the buyer's real problem as missing connected infrastructure under content, offer, funnel, and backend.

The process-selling VSL idea is directly useful for cold outreach. A personalized VSL should show the prospect the specific gap in their business and make the proposed process feel obvious.

## What not to copy

- Do not use "AI agent" as the hero phrase.
- Do not sell AI as the outcome.
- Do not copy broad AI opportunity urgency without connecting it to Pulsify's actual proof.
- Do not position Pulsify as a traditional AI automation agency.
