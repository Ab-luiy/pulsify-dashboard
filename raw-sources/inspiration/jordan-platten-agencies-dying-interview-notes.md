# Jordan Platten - agencies dying interview notes

This source is not a VSL. It is a long-form interview on the agency model, retention, communication, AI, ethics, profit, ascension, and mastermind/event economics. It is useful as strategic context for agency and coach scaling, not as a script template.

## Most important takeaways

1. Jordan argues that agencies remain a strong gateway business because they force the founder to learn sales, client management, team management, and resilience.
2. The model becomes harder at higher scale. The interview frames roughly $200K-$300K/month as the point where stress and complexity can start creating diminishing returns.
3. The only fatal skill deficiency he names is communication. Marketing and delivery skills can be learned, but poor communication breaks clients and teams.
4. Retention matters more than acquisition once the agency has momentum. Strong service, trust, relationships, and referrals become the compounding asset.
5. Expectation setting is a close-rate and retention mechanism. Telling clients what will not happen can increase trust.
6. The trust crisis is worsened by bigger guarantees and bolder promises. The stronger move is to be more honest about fit, limits, and client responsibility.
7. AI will commoditize many service workflows, which makes brand, community, communication, creativity, and relationships more important.
8. The highest-leverage retention move is to own more of the client's real problem, not just the narrow service deliverable.
9. Stage maps create authority. Showing the client what phase they are in, where they need to go next, and what must happen first makes the work legible.
10. Ascension from beginner biz-op into mid/high-ticket is often weaker than founders expect. Ascension improves when the buyer is already an established business.

## Pattern notes for Pulsify

The strongest extraction is not a VSL tactic. It is expectation-setting as persuasion. Pulsify's personalized cold VSLs should not say yes to everything. They should show the prospect exactly what Pulsify can fix, what the prospect still owns, and what would make them a bad fit.

The second extraction is the stage map. A cold VSL should show the prospect where they are in the content -> offer -> funnel -> backend map. This makes the diagnosis visual and prevents the pitch from sounding like generic agency advice.
