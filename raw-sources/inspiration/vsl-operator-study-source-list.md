# VSL operator study source list

This source pack is approved for Pulsify pattern analysis. It is not a license to import every operator's strategy into Pulsify. The rule: extract mechanisms, proof architecture, funnel flow, offer positioning, and market language. Do not copy surface language. Do not turn competitor claims into Pulsify claims unless Pulsify has proof.

## Ingestion filter

The study pack is split into a core cluster and peripheral references.

Core cluster:

- Charlie Morgan / Imperium Agency - primary VSL pattern reference already stored in `raw-sources/vsls/imperium-agency-vsl-transcript.md`.
- Daniel Fazio / Client Ascension / Olympia - agency offer packaging, VSL/call funnel mechanics, mathematical client acquisition.
- Serge Gatari / Clientacquisition.io / Build & Release - AI infrastructure positioning and commoditized-service repositioning.
- Jordan Platten / Affluent / Elite Agency - agency scaling, systems, operator bottleneck, advisory-board positioning.
- Jeremy Haynes - call funnel, VSL, webinar, pre-call nurture infrastructure already stored in `raw-sources/frameworks/`.

Peripheral references:

- Cole Gordon - sales-team infrastructure only.
- Ayman Arab - YouTube sales funnel and content operating system only.
- Grayson Loupas - social funnel contrast only.
- Dan Bolton - simple organic authority and no-call funnel economics only.
- Joel Kaplan - ethical operator framing and free-education-to-paid-coaching model only.
- Abdoulaye Waly - current market language for coaches/agencies only.
- Sam Ovens - legacy consulting VSL structure only.

## Source limitations

- Many direct VSLs are gated, retired, ad-specific, or embedded behind custom players.
- Public pages often show offer architecture but not the full spoken VSL script.
- Third-party transcript and summary pages are useful for discovery, but direct pages and owned sources are preferred when available.
- Every claim pulled from these sources should be treated as competitor self-report unless verified elsewhere.
