# Jeremy Haynes - Inner Circle page notes

This source is a public high-ticket application page for Jeremy's Inner Circle. It is useful for application flow, scarcity, disclaimer posture, and minimal page structure. It is not a captured VSL transcript.

## Most important takeaways

1. The page is intentionally minimal: event anchor, high-ticket scaling promise, embedded VSL, email gate, testimonials, and legal disclaimers.
2. The headline is broad but clear: scale a high-ticket product or service business to the next level.
3. The application path begins with a name and email gate before access to the application page.
4. Scarcity is based on member count and a dated mastermind event. The page showed inconsistent member limits and a Q1 April 11-12, 2026 event date, which is stale as of 2026-05-10.
5. The embedded video is Wistia ID `dosbuqr428`, but the transcript was not publicly captured.
6. The disclaimer language is unusually strong. It explicitly refuses to promise $1M/month or $10M/year outcomes and says some people lose money taking action.
7. The page reports the average member around $200K-$300K/month gross revenue, but this is competitor self-report and not proof Pulsify can reuse.
8. The testimonial path is separate from the main page, keeping the front page focused on the VSL/application flow.

## Pattern notes for Pulsify

The useful pattern is minimal page confidence. The page does not explain every detail before asking for the next step. It lets the VSL and application path carry the persuasion.

For Pulsify's personalized VSLs, this means the video should earn the next diagnostic step. It does not need to explain every deliverable. The page or message around the video can stay narrow: problem, video, proof/disclaimer, CTA.

## What not to copy

- Do not copy stale date or inconsistent scarcity.
- Do not copy income-result framing before Pulsify has the proof.
- Do not use mastermind positioning unless Pulsify actually offers a mastermind.
- Do not treat the embedded video as analyzed until the transcript is captured.
