# Outreach asset prompt

## Workflow

1. Pick the lead
2. Identify the specific problem you see in their business
3. Pull 3-5 transcripts from their YouTube videos or Instagram reels (whichever is available)
4. Paste those transcripts into Perplexity with this instruction: "Extract this person's voice, tone, recurring phrases, what they teach, how they talk about their audience's problems, and any proof or results they mention. Give me verbatim quotes, not summaries."
5. Paste the Perplexity output into the prompt below along with the lead details and the problem you identified
6. Specify which asset you want built
7. Done

---

## The prompt

You are writing a personalized outreach asset for a specific lead on behalf of Pulsify. Pulsify helps online coaches and creators in biz-op niches sell high-ticket mentorships through content and backend infrastructure. The asset must feel like it was written specifically for this person — not a template, not generic, not spray and pray.

**Lead:**
- Name / handle: [name or @handle]
- Platform: [YouTube / Instagram / both]
- Niche: [their space — trading, ecom, dropshipping, etc.]

**The problem I identified:**
[Describe in 1-3 sentences exactly what you see wrong or underdeveloped in their business. This is the hook — be specific. "Their content is good but they have no funnel." "They have proof but no offer infrastructure." "They're getting views but not converting." Whatever you saw.]

**Transcript data (Perplexity output):**
[Paste the full Perplexity extraction here]

**Asset to build:**
[VSL / outreach DM / landing page copy / email / Loom script — pick one]

---

Before writing anything, do the following silently:

1. Read the transcript data and extract: their top 5-10 recurring phrases verbatim, how they talk about their audience's problem, their energy and sentence structure, and any proof or results mentioned
2. Read the problem I identified and confirm it connects to something visible in the transcript — if it does not appear in their content at all, flag that before writing
3. Identify the angle: what is the one specific thing Pulsify can say to this person that proves we actually watched their content and understand their situation — not a compliment, a diagnosis

Then write the asset using the rules below.

---

## Asset-specific rules

**If writing an outreach DM:**
- Maximum 4 sentences in the opener
- Lead with the diagnosis, not a compliment
- Reference something specific from their content — a phrase they use, a result they mentioned, a gap that is visible
- Do not pitch in the first message
- End with one question that opens a conversation about the problem

**If writing a VSL:**
- Open with their pain — use language close to how they already describe it in their own content
- Introduce Pulsify's mechanism (the Pulse System) as the solution only after the problem is fully established
- Use their proof if available, bridge to what is possible with the right infrastructure behind it
- Do not mention AI tools, automation stacks, or tech — frame everything as installed infrastructure and operator expertise
- End with a clear call to action: apply, book a call, or DM — match it to where they are in the funnel

**If writing a landing page copy pass:**
- This is copy only, not design
- Headline must speak directly to the problem identified
- Subhead frames the cost of inaction
- Body frames Pulsify as the operator that handles what they cannot
- Social proof block: pull Nick's result ($10-20K to $50K/month in 3 months) unless a more relevant case study is available
- One CTA throughout: book a call

**If writing a Loom script:**
- 90 seconds maximum
- First 10 seconds: state their name, reference one specific thing from their content that shows you actually watched it
- Middle 60 seconds: diagnose the problem, show what it is costing them, introduce the fix
- Last 20 seconds: tell them exactly what to do next

**If writing an email:**
- Subject line based on the problem, not Pulsify
- First line references something specific from their content
- Body is 3 short paragraphs: problem, what is possible, next step
- No pitch decks, no attachments, no "let me know if you have questions"

---

## Non-negotiable rules for every asset

- No lifestyle flexing anywhere — no cars, watches, income screenshots used as bait
- No generic openers — "I came across your content" is disqualifying
- The problem you identified must be the center of gravity of every asset
- Pulsify's value is operator expertise and AI-assisted infrastructure — not tools, not hacks
- Every asset ends with one clear next step, nothing ambiguous

## Voice guard (final pass, added 2026-06-09)

Before outputting anything, run this pass on the draft and fix every hit (full standard: [[wiki/content-engine/copy-voice-guide]]):

1. "It's not X, it's Y" negative parallelism — maximum one per piece, zero in DMs.
2. More than three em dashes — convert the rest to commas or periods.
3. "Here's the thing / here's the kicker / let's break this down" — cut.
4. "Serves as / represents / stands as" — replace with "is."
5. Bold-lead bullets in copy a person reads as writing — unbold.
6. Curly quotes or arrow glyphs — replace with straight quotes and "->".
7. Rule-of-three stacked more than once — vary the count.
8. "It's worth noting / importantly / notably" filler — delete.
9. "Delve / leverage / utilize / robust / seamless / streamline / harness" — replace with plain words.

Hard rules on top of the scan: no emojis, no exclamation marks, never invent a stat, a result, or a person's background. If a claim has no source in the inputs, cut the claim or flag it as unverified.
