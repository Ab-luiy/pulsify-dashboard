# YouTube script prompt

The scripting subroutine from [[wiki/concepts/pulse-system-mechanism]]'s Content Engine. Input is one approved angle (the founder already killed 7 of 10 and rewrote the hooks). Output is a script the coach can shoot same-day. Works for client channels and for Pulsify's own channel.

Titles and thumbnails are NOT this prompt's job — packaging runs through [[wiki/content-engine/packaging-rules]] and the titles-thumbnails skill, before scripting. The script serves the committed title, never drifts from it.

## Workflow

1. Angle approved, title and thumbnail concept locked. The script must pay off the exact promise the title makes.
2. Pull voice data from the Brand Brain (or Creator Profile for a prospect demo asset).
3. Pick the template: Alexei structure (mechanism-teaching, micro-niche authority), Ayman three-types (matched to whether this video is TOF attract, MOF nurture, or BOF convert), or Pulsify default below.
4. Run. Founder reviews hook and structure; client approves voice fit before shoot.

---

## The prompt

You are scripting a YouTube long-form video for a coach. The video's job inside the channel funnel is specified below — a TOF video earns attention, a MOF video builds trust in the mechanism, a BOF video moves a warm viewer to the funnel. Write for that job only; a video that tries to do all three does none.

The viewer must never feel a script. Write in the coach's spoken voice from the voice data: their phrases, their pacing, their way of explaining. Where the voice data shows how they teach (analogies they reuse, how they handle numbers), copy the pattern.

**Video:**
- Committed title: [exact title]
- Thumbnail concept: [one line — the script's open must connect to it]
- Funnel job: [TOF / MOF / BOF]
- Target length: [8-15 min typical]
- Template: [Alexei / Ayman three-types / Pulsify default]

**Angle (approved):**
[The angle with its rationale — why this, why now, what it proves]

**Voice data:**
[Brand Brain extraction: verbatim phrases, energy, sentence length, what they never say]

**Demo surface:** [Miro board / Google Doc / Gamma / screen-share / talking head only — the visual the teaching happens on]

**CTA:** [where this video sends people — IG profile, VSL link, next video. BOF gets a direct CTA, TOF gets a soft one.]

---

Structure (Pulsify default — adapt if another template was chosen, keep the hook rules regardless):

**Hook (0:00-0:45).** Pays off the title immediately. State what the viewer gets and why it's credible coming from this coach, in their voice. Open loops allowed, bait-and-switch never — retention dies at minute 2 when the title promise isn't being delivered. Write 3 hook variants; mark the recommended one.

**Setup (0:45-2:00).** Why this matters to the viewer's situation right now, and the cost of the way they currently do it. One real example beats three abstract claims.

**Body (2:00 onward).** The teaching, in 3-5 segments. For each segment write: the spoken lines, [B-ROLL] cues, [MIRO] or [SCREEN] cues describing exactly what's on the visual at that moment, and a one-line open loop into the next segment. Numbers and specifics over adjectives, always.

**Payoff (final 2 min).** The synthesis the title promised, delivered fully. No new topics.

**CTA (last 30 sec).** One destination, framed as the natural next step for the viewer who got value. Never beg for subscribes; mention once if at all.

Output the script with timestamps, then a shoot sheet: total word count, estimated runtime at the coach's pace, the visual assets to prepare before recording (each [MIRO]/[B-ROLL] cue collected into a checklist), and the one line that carries the video if everything else gets fumbled.

## Rules

- The title's promise is a contract. Every segment either advances the payoff or gets cut.
- Authority comes from specificity: real numbers, real screenshots, named steps. Never invent an example — pull from the Brand Brain or mark [NEEDS REAL EXAMPLE] for the coach to fill.
- No lifestyle flexing on screen or in script. Authority signals only.
- Spoken register: contractions, short sentences, direct address. Read-aloud test before output.

## Voice guard

Before outputting anything, run this pass on the draft and fix every hit:

1. "It's not X, it's Y" negative parallelism — maximum one per piece, zero in DMs.
2. More than three em dashes — convert the rest to commas or periods.
3. "Here's the thing / here's the kicker / let's break this down" — cut.
4. "Serves as / represents / stands as" — replace with "is."
5. Bold-lead bullets in copy a person reads as writing — unbold.
6. Curly quotes or arrow glyphs — replace with straight quotes and "->".
7. Rule-of-three stacked more than once — vary the count.
8. "It's worth noting / importantly / notably" filler — delete.
9. "Delve / leverage / utilize / robust / seamless / streamline / harness" — replace with plain words.

Hard rules on top of the scan: no emojis, no exclamation marks, no lifestyle flexing, never invent a stat, a result, or a person's background. If a claim has no source in the inputs, cut the claim or flag it as unverified.
