Once you have extracted all variables above, build the full responsive landing page below. This pass is structure and design only. Use placeholder copy throughout — real copy comes in a separate pass. Focus everything on layout, hierarchy, and feel.

### Structure, top to bottom

1. Hero: bold headline slot + subhead slot, with one primary CTA button above the fold. No clutter competing with it.
2. VSL slot: a clean 16:9 video player with a play button, centered, no autoplay. This is the visual anchor of the page.
3. Qualifying application form directly under the VSL. Fields: First Name, Email, Instagram (optional), Phone, Age, Current Situation, 3 Month Target, Why now, and a financial qualification question ("Are you in a position to invest $X–$Y in solving this today?" — pull the range from the offer in the context block; this field is non-negotiable per the VSL framework, it is what keeps unqualified leads off the calendar). Style it like a serious application, not a newsletter signup, so unqualified leads self select out.
4. A clear "Book Your Call" CTA that the form leads into.
5. Testimonial wall: a grid of real proof presented as screenshot tiles and short video tiles, not retyped quote graphics that look fake. Pull proof from the context block — use real names, real numbers, no invented testimonials.
6. Short closing CTA section that restates the next step.

### Length — match the page to the audience size you extracted

- Over 50k followers: full long form, more social proof, more form fields.
- Around 10k: leaner, shorter VSL slot, tighter sections.
- Under 5k or micro niche: strip it down to headline plus VSL plus booking link, the viewer is basically pre sold.

### Design rules — non-negotiable

- Mobile first. Headline, video, and form all have to work on a narrow screen first.
- Raw and authentic over flashy. Absolutely no lifestyle flexing anywhere: no cars, watches, money fans, private jets. Trust over decoration.
- Clean and modern. Generous spacing, strong type hierarchy, one clear action per screen. Lean minimal for a serious or affluent buyer, slightly more visual structure for a broad audience, but never hype or scam energy.
- Make it look genuinely custom, not a template. Distinct layout, intentional sections.
- Color and tone should match the niche you extracted. Trading and finance: dark, serious, minimal. Ecom and TikTok Shop: slightly warmer, more energetic but still clean.
