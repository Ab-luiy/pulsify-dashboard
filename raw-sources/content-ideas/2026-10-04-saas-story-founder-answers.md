# Founder answers: Pulsify SaaS story sequence intake (2026-10-04)

Founder voice answers to Claude's 18-question intake for the SaaS highlight sequence. Condensed faithfully; reuse for future sequences, carousels, reels and the in-app story-sequence SOP.

## Audience and job
- Lives permanently in a highlight. Viewers are prospects arriving from outreach who click the profile; goal is "this guy has a real business and knows what he's talking about", lifting outreach reply rate.
- ICP: money / biz-op / make-money-online creators. Core struggle is content direction and purpose: what to talk about, then "I made this, now what?" Some quit even while getting good views because there is no path.

## Market struggle (his words, condensed)
- Content creation is genuinely hard for everyone; not a thing the market "got wrong".
- Ideation: the drive to create but no idea what to talk about.
- Planning: story sequence or carousel or reel? 5x a day, 3x, 2x? Or YouTube instead? What's the video about? "What I have to say takes 5 minutes, how do I stretch it to 10-15?"
- Tried: ChatGPT/Claude and Notion mostly; agencies/courses less (trust recession); VAs rarely. Generic AI use lacks "the missing piece to the full picture".
- Against: lifestyle selling lost its touch; now transparency and consistency, posting daily while practising what you preach. Poor-quality broad content attracts the wrong worldwide audience and bloats the CRM; specific content for one audience and one pain, attacked from many angles, is how you stand out. Pulsify helps discover that.

## Product
- One sentence: one place for everything info-business: content creation, turning that traffic into a landing page (VSL or webinar), then closing a high-ticket mentorship ($3k-10k one-time).
- Feature to remember: the AI copywriting chat / content section. Plus tracking: every piece is tracked (UTMs) so even a flop gives data: who follows, who clicks, what stage they're at. He mentions one other tool by "Yusuf" does similar but isn't widespread (unverified; do not name in copy).
- Unique: depth on content, the prospect's real needs, what they want to track; adapts to the creator's personality. MVP, improving.
- Sold together with the done-for-you service, not standalone. Access only for people who work with him. No urgency/scarcity claims yet.
- Launches: deliberately excluded; founder thinks launch planning is low priority.

## Founder angle
- Content is the driver of everything; good presence makes people want to work with you without pushing. Built it so creators can be themselves, pick what's proven, and stay accountable when they slack.
- Inside view: people quit when they lack purpose and direction; the ones who make it act anyway without imposter syndrome.
- Origin: when Claude Code and coding LLMs arrived he saw the window to build a SaaS in his own niche before it closed. Building since the start of 2026: brain, docs, frameworks first, then the app. Wants the due diligence visible: thousands of hours of research, not built overnight.

## Proof and ask
- No users yet. Plan: bring ~5 people on a free trial, upsell once they get results (later).
- Ask: just watch. No CTA, no scarcity.
- Format: talking-to-camera + motion graphic video in the MIDDLE of the sequence. Motion graphic shows the UI layout/features without intricate detail (regenerated look, not raw screens).
- Also wants: a carousel and a heavily edited reel showing the product, from the same material. Codex already holds his X/Twitter motion references (wiki/synthesis/pulsify-product-launch-direction.md).
