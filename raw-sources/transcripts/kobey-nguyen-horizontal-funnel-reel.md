# Horizontal content funnel (reel transcript)

Top of funnel, middle of funnel, and bottom of funnel is completely dead. Here's what actually is working. Most people think that content is a vertical, linear path, when in reality, it's a horizontal transformation. And when you can understand this concept, every single video is going to serve the three funnel stages.

The first 20% of your video should be top of funnel. This should appeal to a mass market audience and speak to everybody to get as many views as you can, possible. Now, in the middle of the video is where you go into your middle funnel. This speaks to your dream follower, introduces a problem or pain point that is still mass market, but also contains your ICP inside of it.

Then at the end of the video, you reach the bottom of funnel. This is where you speak fully to your ICP. Their pain points, their desires, things that will actually bring them closer to a sale. When you understand this horizontal transformation, you stop worrying about creating top of funnel, middle of funnel, bottom of funnel content because you know that every single piece of content can serve every funnel stage, meaning that even your most micro-niched value video can still get the same reach as a top of funnel video.
