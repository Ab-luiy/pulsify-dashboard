## Transcript 1: How to create Miro Board YouTube videos that convert coaching clients

### The headline numbers

This strategy generates **$800–$1,200 for every 100 views**. No editing, no production — just a Miro board and a screen recorder. It works for any make-money business model: ecom, real estate, trading, SMA, AI, YouTube automation. Implemented correctly, it can take a coaching business to $100k/month in 2026.

### The broken content cycle (what NOT to do)

Most creators stuck under $30k/month follow the same broken loop:

```
See a competitor → Copy their format → Post → Pray it works
  → Get no feedback → Repeat for months or years with no direction
```

They try the Instagram profile funnel, Instagram reels, trending YouTube formats — spending hours on content while getting some views but no sales. **The core problem is no strategy, no feedback loop, and no mechanism for diagnosing what's broken.**

### Why lifestyle content fails at the start

The Instagram funnel requires:
- Constant B-roll filming
- An expensive lifestyle to stay relevant
- Travel
- In-person masterminds and events
- Optimizing for views rather than trust

The market in 2025–2026 is in a **"trust recession"** — the same hooks constantly repackaged, too many copycats, courses that overpromise and underdeliver. This creates a market full of uncertainty.

> Prospects now watch an average of **five YouTube videos** before booking a call. They've consumed all your free content and exhausted their ability to self-execute before they reach out. That level of sophistication requires genuine value to convert — lifestyle signals alone don't work when starting out.

### The micro niche YouTube approach

**All you need:**
- Miro (or a similar visual tool)
- A VA to build the boards ($300–$400/month)
- A laptop
- OBS Studio or Loom to record

No villa rental, no ads budget, no 10-hour work days.

#### The most critical principle: go niche specific

If you sell a drop shipping offer, go drop shipping specifically for supplements. **Always go one level deeper than the obvious niche.**

> Example positioning: "I help people scale high-ticket info products with a micro niche YouTube channel, specifically for coaches under $30k/month trying to reach their first $100k/month." Nothing beyond that.

This follows Rogers' diffusion model — take a small, clearly defined segment, become the early adopter who signals value, refine product-market fit, then scale.

**Going micro niche means:**
- YouTube's algorithm can accurately push your videos to your exact ICP
- You face no real competition
- You don't need to be compared to lifestyle-based competitors
- You can show genuine expertise rather than a flashy image
- One micro niche owned by you makes you the go-to person, which compounds over time into $200k–$500k months

**Content ratio:** 80% YouTube to convert, 20% Instagram for an extra 20–30% boost. Instagram is optional but additive.

### Step 1: Detecting market outliers

The first step is reverse-engineering structural performance variables — figuring out exactly why a specific video outperformed every other video on that channel.

> Example: "Speedrunning cold calls from $0 to first sale — I show everything" performed 100x better than the channel average.

Break down the variables:

**Macro concept structure:** What was the underlying promise of the video? You cannot out-package a bad concept. Most creators skip this and wonder why nothing works. The promise must be something people actually want.

**Packaging:** How was the idea translated into a clickable title and thumbnail pair? Where most creators compete is where channels plateau — great ideas die unclicked.

**Hook and framing:** In the first 30 seconds, does the video clearly confirm it's delivering exactly what was promised in the title and thumbnail? Nothing else.

**Authority positioning:** What signals made the audience believe the creator was worth listening to? The viewer is deciding whether to watch and buy simultaneously. **Credibility does the selling.** Embed indirect authority nuggets throughout — reference a client you've implemented something for, mention a specific result. Over time, viewers figure out on their own how to work with you.

### Step 2: Creating exceptional videos

Three components:

#### 1. Content angle

Take a saturated outlier angle you've found in the market, then bring your own new market angle and turn it into a common concept. **That middle ground — familiar enough to attract views, fresh enough to stand out — is where growth happens.**

#### 2. Storytelling

Every video follows the same arc:

```
Problem → Struggle → Discovery → Result
```

This is the emotional journey that keeps viewers watching.

#### 3. Unique mechanism

You must constantly reference your unique mechanism in every video.

> The unique mechanism is the specific underlying reason or system that explains why something works — turning a general idea into a distinct framework that feels logical, credible, and uniquely yours.

Example: "the micro niche YouTube funnel."

If you stop mentioning it, nobody will want it. When you do mention it, **people need to already know what it is and how it works** so their only question is whether to implement it with your help.

### Step 3: The content funnel structure

#### Top of Funnel — Attract

Goal: spark engagement, shift beliefs, attract views.
- Create attention-driven content
- Qualify viewers through retention loops
- Break their limiting beliefs
- Do not give too much information — give them the **"wow" not the "how"**
- They need: a clear "this is for me" signal, a broken assumption, a fast win or insight, an open loop pulling them toward middle funnel

#### Middle of Funnel — Nurture

Goal: begin converting.
- Post contradicting content, social proof, in-depth breakdowns
- Better retention here
- This is where sales calls start getting booked and payments begin coming in
- Viewers are in a qualifying phase — getting ready to buy
- They need: personal relevance, emotional certainty, momentum, removal of fears ("this won't waste my time or money")

#### Bottom of Funnel — Convert

- Every bottom-of-funnel video is treated like a video sales letter
- Use client interviews and behind-the-scenes content that proves the solution works
- Be intentional about every CTA placement
- These viewers are high-intent — they know your solution works and just need the final push

**The ideal customer journey:**

```
1 TOF video → 2-3 MOF videos → 1 BOF video → Landing page → Booked call
```

### Speaking style and video format

Develop a unique speaking style based on your ICP. If everyone else is monotone and generic, differentiate through energy, pace, or delivery. **Do it consistently** — this builds your personal brand and creates trust and authority. Find your own way, not a copy of someone else's.

### The three CTA types

**1. Indirect CTA:** Embedded inside storytelling, results, or examples. Naturally leads viewers toward the offer without directly asking. Most powerful for trust-building.

**2. Soft CTA:** Subtle, lightly suggests the next step without pressure. Framed as an optional resource or further help.

**3. Hard CTA:** At the end of the video. Direct, explicit, tells viewers exactly what to do next — book a call, go to the landing page, opt into the funnel. Spend one to two minutes on it, go in-depth.

> The frequency of CTAs matters less than how you present them. Place all three types intentionally throughout each video.

### Step 4: The landing page and conversion funnel

**Landing page design depends on audience size:**

| Audience | Page design |
|---|---|
| Large audience | Longer VSL, longer headline, more qualifying questions, more social proof, broad copy |
| Mid-size (~10k followers) | Shorter headline, shorter VSL, shorter landing page overall |
| Micro niche (extremely specific content) | Just a headline and a Calendly link — pre-sold |
| B2B seller | Just a Calendly link |

**All landing pages, regardless of size, include qualifying questions.** When selling B2C, you must filter out time-wasters.

The form captures:
- Commitment level
- Desires
- Roadblocks
- Current income
- Contact info
- Objections
- Budget

This data tells you whether they're worth getting on a call and how to handle them when you do. Once qualified, they book, receive email and SMS reminders, show up, get closed, and get onboarded.

### Step 5: Data tracking and management

> You can hit $100k/month once without tracking. You cannot do it consistently without it.

Always use the same system for any KPI:

```
Set the KPI → Identify where to retrieve the data → Analyze it
  → Identify the flaws → Iterate and implement
```

**Only track the highest-leverage metrics.** Tracking vanity metrics that move the needle 1–3% is a waste. Focus on:
- Close rate
- Show rate
- CTA performance within videos
- Calls booked per video
- Conversion rate per content type

**Track where every call is coming from** — which YouTube video, which Instagram reel, which specific piece of content. This is what separates people hitting record months from those plateauing.

#### Team-level tracking

**Sales team dashboard:**
- Previous sets, conversation examples
- Setter KPIs
- Closer KPIs (close rate, cash collected per week, deal flow, show-up rate, minimum cash per lead)
- Daily checklist
- Critical reminders

**Content team:**
- Miro board output per week
- AI usage efficiency
- Software tracking for video data
- Conversion rate per video
- Feedback loop with you

### Student onboarding (post-sale)

```
Payment collected
  → Onboarding email
  → Sign contract (prevents chargebacks)
  → Onboarding form (captures why they bought)
  → Auto-create client card in Airtable
  → Assign VA
  → Book onboarding call
  → AI client folder custom train
  → Deliver course modules and assets
```

**Structure the first 90–120 days to produce the biggest possible results** — this sets up any upsell. They'll only buy an upsell if the core program actually works.

## Transcript 2: How to scale a micro niche YouTube channel — results and proof

### Context and proof of concept

The creator (Alexei, CEO of Info Accelerator) has scaled 50+ coaches to six and seven figures. The channel used in the examples has 4,500 subscribers and generates **$58,000/month** — now scaling past $20,000/week with increased content output. **Revenue per 100 views: $800–$1,200.**

The channel results from clients using the same micro niche system:
- $200k/month, $400k/month, $50k/month — all started from zero
- 0 to $50k/month in one month
- $3k/month to $20k/week
- $30k–$50k/month from 3,000 subscribers

> The approach works specifically because the market is sick of being sold by a lifestyle. Micro niche content sells on value — no Lamborghinis, no villas, no production team required.

### Why this beats the Instagram profile funnel

| Instagram approach | Micro niche YouTube approach |
|---|---|
| Constant B-roll filming | Simple videos, screen recordings |
| Expensive lifestyle requirements | Filmed from an office |
| Travel, in-person events | No location requirements |
| Optimizing for views not trust | Pre-sold prospects, zero objections |
| 30–60 second clips, constant churn | Long-form, 10x more trust |
| Increasingly saturated, declining ROI | No competition in your micro niche |
| Algorithm spreads content broadly | Algorithm finds your ICP automatically |

### The three common mistakes (0 to $100k/month)

**1. Too broad with content:** Wrong people find the videos, tanks conversion rate.

**2. Videos educate but don't sell:** Give the "wow" not the "how." Viewers learn and leave — you've trained a free audience, not buyers.

**3. No clear transition to CTAs:** Traffic stays cold, no calls get booked, no feedback loop, always stuck diagnosing what's broken.

### Offer positioning foundation

Build a foundational Google Doc that clearly defines:
- Your ideal customer profile
- Their limiting beliefs
- Psychographic traits
- Behaviors
- High-level resources

Then build your actual offer with:
- A value equation
- Personal anecdotes
- A unique mechanism (different from everyone else's)
- A unique brand identity
- A one-page breakdown
- A structured delivery system

**Monetization structure depends on audience size.**

### Final note on execution

> The information is not the bottleneck. People who watch this content and spend 6–12 months trying to implement it on their own face an **execution problem, not a knowledge problem**.

The system works in every make-money niche: trading, vape vending machines, ecom, freelance brand scaling, high-ticket sales, and more. Every funnel, every system, and every piece of content built under this approach is designed specifically for **make-money offers — not generic business content**.
