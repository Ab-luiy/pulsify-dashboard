# Mason Bowstead funnel — design reference

The benchmark for our client landing/VSL pages. Clean, premium, minimal, trust over
decoration. The whole thing is two GoHighLevel pages.

## Design tokens (real, from page source)
- **Fonts (the core of the look):** `Inter Tight` for headlines (weights 700/800/900),
  `Inter` for body (400/500/600). Loaded from Google Fonts. Inter Tight at heavy weight
  is what gives the bold, modern, premium headline feel.
- **Palette:** white / off-white background, dark charcoal near-black body text, a single
  solid accent CTA color (blue). No color blocking, no gradients, no noise.
- **Layout:** single centered column, narrow content width, generous whitespace between
  every section. Mobile-first — headline, video, form all work on a narrow screen first.
- **Vibe:** minimal + direct. ZERO lifestyle flexing. Proof does the selling, not decoration.

## Page 1 — Opt-in (go.masonbowstead.com)
Top-to-bottom, deliberately bare:
1. Small eyebrow line naming the audience ("For operators, coaches, creators & sales reps…").
2. Big bold headline (Inter Tight) + value subhead with scarcity ("Usually $197/mo, right now FREE").
3. One primary CTA button ("Get Instant Access"), solid, high contrast, nothing competing.
4. Single course/community preview image for credibility.
5. Legal/footer.
That's it. Headline + offer + one button. The viewer is basically pre-sold by the YouTube video.

## Page 2 — VSL (go.masonbowstead.com/freetraining)
The conversion page. Order:
1. "Congrats!" confirmation header (warm, conversational).
2. Headline about the training/result.
3. **VSL video** — clean centered player, the visual anchor.
4. **"Apply Below" typeform/application gate** directly under the video (qualifies, books call).
5. Named success stories with specific dollar figures + timeframes.
6. **Testimonial bombardment** — dozens of screenshot tiles (saw ~73) in a continuous
   vertical feed. Volume IS the argument. Real screenshots, never retyped quote graphics.
7. Final CTA button ("CLICK HERE TO APPLY NOW").
8. Legal/footer.

## What makes it convert
- Social proof at scale (overwhelming screenshot volume) over a few polished quotes.
- Specificity: named people, exact $ figures, real timeframes.
- Application gate implies selectivity, filters tire-kickers.
- Minimal design noise so the proof and the one CTA dominate every screen.
- Conversational copy ("don't just take my word for it") builds trust without hype.
